## Wirth (ed.) 2013 — Microreactors in Organic Chemistry and Catalysis (Wiley) and Hessel et al. — Chemical Micro Process Engineering (2013-04-08)

- **id**: `spielmann-2017-microreactors-industry-textbook`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Thomas Wirth (ed.); Volker Hessel et al.
- **disclosure**: T. Wirth (ed.), 'Microreactors in Organic Chemistry and Catalysis', 2nd ed., Wiley-VCH 2013, ISBN 978-3527332991; V. Hessel, S. Hardt, H. Löwe 'Chemical Micro Process Engineering' Wiley-VCH 2004, ISBN 978-3527308354
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Two canonical industrial-microreactor texts that document the engineering of continuous-flow chemical reactors at micro/milli scale. Cover: micromixer designs (T, Y, multilamination, interdigital, split-and-recombine), residence-time engineering, heat-exchange in microreactors, scale-out vs scale-up, and catalytic micropacked beds. Anticipates: industrial-process patent claims to 'continuous-flow microreactor with high heat-transfer coefficient and short residence time' as obvious in view of the textbook tradition. Particularly load-bearing for fine-chemical and pharma-flow patents.
