- **creator**: GSK (GlaxoSmithKline Biologicals)

- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: droplet-generator
- **creator**: GSK (GlaxoSmithKline Biologicals / GSK Vaccines)
- **disclosure**: Garcon N. et al., Recent clinical experience with vaccines using MPL- and QS-21-containing adjuvant systems, Expert Rev Vaccines 8(11):1459-1476 (2009).
- **ip status**: patented
