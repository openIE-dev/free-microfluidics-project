## Avanti Polar Lipids Mini-Extruder / LIPEX large-volume extruder (1985)

- **id**: `avanti-polar-lipids-mini-extruder`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Avanti Polar Lipids (now Croda); LIPEX from Northern Lipids / Transferra Nanosciences
- **disclosure**: Olson F. et al., Preparation of liposomes of defined size distribution by extrusion through polycarbonate membranes, Biochim Biophys Acta 557:9-23 (1979); commercial Avanti Mini-Extruder / Lipex extruder from ~1985.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses production of size-defined liposomes by repeatedly forcing multilamellar vesicles through a track-etched nanoporous polycarbonate membrane under pressure, with pore size setting vesicle diameter - a nanofluidic membrane-extrusion process. Anticipates claims to liposome manufacture by membrane extrusion of defined pore size, to handheld two-syringe extruders, and to scaled barrel/cartridge extruders for liposomal drug and adjuvant manufacture. Upstream prior art for gsk-as01-liposomal-adjuvant-system.
