## Catalog Technologies Shannon DNA Storage Write/Read System (2019-06-25)

- **id**: `catalog-technologies-dna-storage`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Catalog Technologies Inc. (MIT spinout)
- **disclosure**: Catalog Technologies press release 2019-06-25 'Wikipedia in DNA'; Park et al., Sci. Adv. 2024, 10, eadk5827 (combinatorial DNA storage).
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: First commercial-scale combinatorial DNA storage system. Bypasses slow phosphoramidite DNA synthesis by pre-synthesizing a small library of oligonucleotide 'inks' and combining them in droplets via inkjet-printer-style microfluidic deposition; the combination identity encodes data. Discloses: a massively parallel inkjet-style printhead depositing pre-synthesized oligos in defined combinations onto a substrate; pre-synthesized oligo-ink reservoirs; merging chemistry that combines oligos in a droplet to form unique data-encoding molecules. Anticipates: combinatorial DNA storage as an alternative to de novo synthesis-based storage; inkjet-style microfluidic write architectures for DNA data storage; the entire architectural pattern of pre-synthesized oligo libraries + combinatorial droplet-merging as the write mechanism for sub-second/Mb DNA storage. The microfluidic content is genuinely load-bearing (this is fundamentally a microfluidic system).
