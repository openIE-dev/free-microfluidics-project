## Psaltis Group EPFL Optofluidic Microscope and Sensor Platform (2008)

- **id**: `psaltis-epfl-optofluidic-platform`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Demetri Psaltis (EPFL/Caltech), with Yang Lab
- **disclosure**: Psaltis D., Quake S. R., Yang C., Nature 442:381-386 (2006), doi:10.1038/nature05060; Heng X. et al. (Psaltis lab) Lab Chip 6:1274 (2006), doi:10.1039/B604676B
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Psaltis group EPFL/Caltech discloses optofluidic microscopy architectures wherein a CMOS image sensor is bonded directly beneath a microfluidic channel; flowing cells cast shadows on the sensor and are reconstructed by sub-pixel scanning. Anticipates: (i) lensless on-chip imaging cell counters and morphologists; (ii) the integration of CMOS sensors directly into disposable microfluidic cartridges; (iii) field-deployable optofluidic imagers (predecessors to smartphone-microscopy work). Sister entry to psaltis-2006-optofluidic-review and ozcan-2010-smartphone-microscopy.
