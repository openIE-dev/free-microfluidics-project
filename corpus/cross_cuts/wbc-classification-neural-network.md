## CellaVision Hematology Image Analysis Patent Estate (International) (2000)

- **id**: `cellavision-vs-roche-cellavision-international-ip`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: CellaVision AB
- **disclosure**: CellaVision AB Swedish and international patent filings 1998-2020 covering automated digital morphology of peripheral blood; US7702172B2; EP1488380B1
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses CellaVision's automated peripheral blood digital morphology architecture: motorized microscope stage with autofocus, image segmentation pipeline isolating individual cells, and neural-network classification into WBC subclasses. Anticipates: (a) any slide-based hematology workflow employing automated cell-by-cell classification; (b) the architectural pattern of decoupling sample preparation (manual or automated smear) from downstream digital classification; (c) OEM-integrated digital morphology modules within larger Sysmex/Beckman hematology lines. Material as Swedish/EU prior art for adjacent US claims (Bloodhound, Scopio Labs, Sight Diagnostics) attempting similar digital morphology architectures. Roche / OEM relationships further extended the international footprint.
