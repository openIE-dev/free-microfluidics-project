## SHERLOCK-on-Paper Lateral-Flow CRISPR Diagnostic (2018-04-26)

- **id**: `sherlock-on-paper-zhang-watson`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Zhang lab (MIT/Broad) + Watson lab (MIT) + Sherlock Biosciences
- **disclosure**: Myhrvold et al. (Zhang lab MIT/Broad), Science 2018, 360, 444-448. DOI:10.1126/science.aas8836; Joung et al. (Watson lab MIT) N. Engl. J. Med. 2020, 383, 1492-1494 (SARS-CoV-2 SHERLOCK paper).
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Lateral-flow paper variant of SHERLOCK (Cas13a CRISPR diagnostic). Discloses: pre-amplification (RPA or LAMP) followed by Cas13a collateral cleavage of FAM/biotin reporter; lateral-flow paper strip with anti-FAM gold conjugate at the conjugate pad and streptavidin at the test line, producing visible band detection without instrument; HUDSON one-pot heat-and-detergent lysis for direct sample input. Anticipates: paper-strip CRISPR diagnostics with no-instrument readout; HUDSON-style one-pot sample preparation eliminating dedicated extraction; the architectural pattern of CRISPR-collateral-cleavage as a transduction step before lateral-flow detection.
