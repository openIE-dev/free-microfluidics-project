# Cross-cut: `solid-phase-purification-cartridge`

**3 corpus entries disclose this subsystem.**

## GE Healthcare FASTlab FDG Synthesizer (2008)

- **id**: `ge-fastlab-fdg`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: GE Healthcare (now GE HealthCare)
- **disclosure**: GE Healthcare FASTlab brochure HK04-08; product launch 2008; Lemaire et al., Theranostics 2014, 4, 1010-1027.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Single-use cassette-format radiosynthesizer for F-18 fluorodeoxyglucose (FDG), the dominant PET tracer. Discloses: thermoplastic injection-molded cassette with welded PFA tubing forming all radiolabeled-fluid paths; syringe-driven actuators on the cassette interface for solvent / reagent delivery; pre-packed reagent vials integrated with the cassette; integrated solid-phase QMA and HLB cartridges for purification. Anticipates: single-use cassette radiopharmacy synthesis as the dominant architecture for clinical PET tracer manufacturing; the pattern of a sterile disposable handling all radioactivity-containing fluids with a re-usable instrument providing only mechanical actuation; extension of the cassette concept to other F-18, Ga-68, Cu-64 tracers.

## IBA Synthera + Synthera Extension Radiosynthesizer (2008)

- **id**: `iba-synthera`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Ion Beam Applications (IBA)
- **disclosure**: IBA Synthera brochure 2008; Eberl et al., Nucl. Med. Commun. 2012, 33, 1085-1090.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: IBA Synthera competes directly with GE FASTlab for cassette-based clinical FDG synthesis. Discloses: Integrated Fluidic Processor (IFP) disposable cassette with all wetted parts pre-assembled and validated for single-use; clean-in-place not required (cassette discarded); supports 2-3 batches per work shift on a single instrument with cassette swap. Anticipates: cassette-based radiosynthesis with integrated solid-phase purification within the same disposable; multi-batch / multi-tracer flexibility through cassette-swap workflow; the architectural pattern of the cassette as a single, factory-validated, GMP-supply-chain element rather than the radiopharmacist assembling components.

## Trasis miniAllInOne Compact PET Tracer Synthesizer (2014)

- **id**: `trasis-mini-allinone`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Trasis SA
- **disclosure**: Trasis SA product launch 2014; Lemaire et al., J. Nucl. Med. 2014, 55(Suppl 1), 1208 abstract.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Compact academic-scale PET tracer synthesizer competing with GE FASTlab and IBA Synthera. Single-use cassette architecture similar to GE FASTlab but at ~3x smaller form factor for university-scale or rare-tracer R&D. Discloses: pre-packed disposable cassette with ~10-15 valves, reaction vessel, purification cartridge stack, and reagent reservoirs; syringe-driven cassette interface; extensible chemistry library covering F-18, C-11, Ga-68 isotopes; software-controlled multi-step radiolabeling sequences. Anticipates: compact bench-scale single-use cassette PET radiosynthesis for academic and rare-tracer development; the architectural pattern of trace-element-clean disposables enabling rapid switching between tracer chemistries without cross-contamination.
