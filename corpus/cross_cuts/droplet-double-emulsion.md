## SEPPIC Montanide ISA water-in-oil emulsion adjuvant (1993)

- **id**: `seppic-montanide-isa-water-in-oil-adjuvant`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: droplet-generator
- **creator**: SEPPIC (Air Liquide Healthcare)
- **disclosure**: Aucouturier J. et al., Montanide ISA 720 and 51: a new generation of water in oil emulsions as adjuvants for human vaccines, Expert Rev Vaccines (2002); SEPPIC Montanide ISA technical brochures, Air Liquide / SEPPIC, from ~1993.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses water-in-oil and water-in-oil-in-water vaccine emulsion adjuvants based on mannide monooleate surfactant in mineral oil or squalane, formed by emulsification (syringe extrusion, microfluidization, rotor-stator). Anticipates claims to mannide-monooleate W/O and W/O/W emulsion adjuvants of defined aqueous:oil ratio and to therapeutic cancer-vaccine, malaria-vaccine and veterinary-vaccine formulations using them. The syringe-extrusion / microfluidization emulsification step is anticipated by impinging-jet and extrusion microfluidic prior art.
