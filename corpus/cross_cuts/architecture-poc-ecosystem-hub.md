## IDEXX VetLab Station (2001)

- **id**: `idexx-vetlab-station-network-hub`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: IDEXX Laboratories, Inc.
- **disclosure**: IDEXX VetLab Station product launch 2001; IDEXX operator's manual; US patent 7,054,727 (multi-instrument data integration)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Disclosed not as a microfluidic device per se but as the connectivity substrate that defines the IDEXX in-clinic ecosystem: a hub PC integrating barcode-tagged sample identity across multiple disposable-cartridge analyzers and consolidating results onto a single patient record. Anticipates: integrated point-of-care analyzer ecosystems where multiple cartridge-based instruments share a common sample-ID and result-aggregation layer; the architectural pattern of a vendor-agnostic hub orchestrating multi-analyte panels across heterogeneous cartridge formats.
