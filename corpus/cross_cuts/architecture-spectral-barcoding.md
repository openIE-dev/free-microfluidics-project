## Luminex xMAP bead-multiplex platform (Luminex 100/200, FLEXMAP 3D, MAGPIX) (1997)

- **id**: `luminex-xmap-bead-multiplex-platform`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Luminex Corporation (now part of DiaSorin)
- **disclosure**: Fulton R.J. et al., Advanced multiplexed analysis with the FlowMetrix system, Clin Chem 43:1749-1756 (1997); Luminex Corporation xMAP technology; foundational patent Chandler et al. US5736330.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses spectrally-barcoded microspheres each bearing a distinct capture probe, read by single-file sheath-flow laser interrogation (or CCD imaging of settled magnetic beads) to multiplex up to 500 analytes per well. Anticipates broad claims to fluorescence-coded bead multiplex assays, sheath-flow optical bead classification, magnetic-bead settling-and-imaging readout, and multiplexed immunoassay/nucleic-acid panels on coded microspheres. Foundational prior art for bio-rad-bioplex-2200-multiplex and bio-techne-ella-simple-plex (and many others).
