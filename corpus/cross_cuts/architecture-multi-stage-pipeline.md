## Kodak C-41 Color Negative Process Chemistry (Historic Microfluidic Anchor) (1972)

- **id**: `kodak-c-41-process-1972`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Eastman Kodak Company
- **disclosure**: Eastman Kodak Company, Kodak Publication Z-131 'Using Process C-41' (1972, periodically revised); US3415649A 'Color photographic developer composition' (1968)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a controlled multi-fluid pipeline (developer, bleach, fix, wash, stabilizer) operated on perforated 35 mm film passing through sealed tank stations, with strict temperature, agitation, and replenishment specifications. Anticipates as historic anchor: (a) integrated multi-stage chemistry pipelines with timed fluid contact; (b) standardized chemistry bottles + replenisher metering; (c) the architectural pattern of a sealed continuous-flow chemistry stack with mechanical specimen transport. Predates and is foundational to many later 'lab-on-tape' and 'cassette processor' microfluidic patents.

## Kodak E-6 Color Reversal Slide Film Process (Historic Anchor) (1976)

- **id**: `kodak-e-6-process-1976`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Eastman Kodak Company
- **disclosure**: Eastman Kodak Company, Kodak Publication Z-119 'Using Process E-6' (1976)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a seven-bath chemistry pipeline with chemical reversal step replacing mid-process re-exposure, with strict timing and temperature controls between baths. Anticipates as historic anchor: (a) chemical reversal as an in-pipeline microfluidic step; (b) seven-station film-handling chemistry as a precursor to multi-step lab-on-chip workflows. Distinct from C-41 by reversal step.

## Fujifilm CN-16 Color Negative Process (Historic Anchor) (1980)

- **id**: `fujifilm-cn-16-process`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Fuji Photo Film Co. Ltd (now Fujifilm)
- **disclosure**: Fuji Photo Film Co. Ltd technical bulletin 'Process CN-16' (1980)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Fujifilm's six-stage color-negative chemistry pipeline equivalent to Kodak C-41, with vendor-specific bleach formulation and minilab-station mechanical transport. Anticipates as historic anchor parallel to C-41: (a) vendor-parallel multi-stage chemistry pipelines; (b) minilab compact-pipeline microfluidic-relevant transport. Distinct from C-41 by chemistry vendor and minilab handling format.
