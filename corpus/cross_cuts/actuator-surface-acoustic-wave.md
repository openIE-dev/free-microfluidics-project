## Friend & Yeo SAW Microfluidics Review (2011-04)

- **id**: `friend-yeo-2011-saw-microfluidics-review`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: James Friend & Leslie Yeo (RMIT/Monash)
- **disclosure**: Friend J., Yeo L. Y., Reviews of Modern Physics 83, 647 (2011), doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.83.647
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: This Reviews of Modern Physics article codifies the field of surface-acoustic-wave (SAW) microfluidics, providing element-by-element disclosures of: (a) interdigital transducer (IDT) layouts on Y-cut/X-propagating lithium niobate for Rayleigh SAW generation; (b) acoustic streaming inside sessile droplets via leaky-SAW coupling; (c) standing-SAW pressure-node manipulation of particles in microchannels coupled to PDMS overlays; (d) SAW-driven atomization for nebulizer applications. Anticipates broad downstream patent claims to IDT-driven droplet manipulation, SAW pumping, and SAW-induced acoustic streaming for mixing or particle focusing in PDMS-on-LiNbO3 hybrid devices. Provides 102 disclosure for any combination of these elements claimed after April 2011.

## Respite SAW Nebulizer (RMIT) (2012)

- **id**: `rezk-yeo-2012-saw-nebulizer`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: dispenser-pipettor
- **creator**: Rezk, Yeo, Friend (RMIT)
- **disclosure**: Qi A., Yeo L. Y., Friend J. R., Lab on a Chip 9, 2184 (2009), doi:10.1039/B903575C; Rezk A. R. et al., Lab on a Chip 12, 773 (2012)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a lithium-niobate IDT pumping liquid up a paper wick into the SAW propagation region, where Rayleigh-wave coupling atomizes the meniscus into a respirable aerosol. Anticipates: (i) SAW-based handheld nebulizers as alternatives to vibrating-mesh nebulizers; (ii) the combination of capillary wicking with SAW atomization; (iii) low-shear aerosolization claims relevant to protein, mRNA, and viral-vector pulmonary delivery.

## RMIT/Monash SAW Droplet Microfluidic Platform (2014)

- **id**: `yeo-friend-2014-saw-droplet-platform`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: droplet-generator
- **creator**: Leslie Yeo & James Friend (Monash/RMIT)
- **disclosure**: Yeo L. Y., Friend J. R., Annual Review of Fluid Mechanics 46, 379-406 (2014), doi:10.1146/annurev-fluid-010313-141418
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Annual Reviews chapter discloses SAW-driven droplet manipulation primitives in microfluidic chips combining IDT-patterned LiNbO3 substrates with PDMS-replicated channel overlays. Anticipates: (i) on-chip SAW droplet sorters competing with dielectrophoretic and pressure-actuated equivalents; (ii) focused-IDT geometries used for site-specific energy delivery to a moving droplet; (iii) hybrid SAW-microfluidic chip architectures wherein the actuation substrate and the channel substrate are physically distinct and reversibly coupled.

## Yeo & Friend Acoustofluidics Review (ARFM 2014 + AnnRev BME 2018) (2014-01)

- **id**: `yeo-2018-acoustofluidic-review`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Leslie Yeo & James Friend
- **disclosure**: Yeo L. Y., Friend J. R., Annual Review of Fluid Mechanics 46:379-406 (2014), doi:10.1146/annurev-fluid-010313-141418; Yeo L. Y., Friend J. R., Annual Review of Biomedical Engineering 20:33-58 (2018), doi:10.1146/annurev-bioeng-062117-120756
- **ip status**: open-permissive

## Tony Jun Huang Lab Tunable Acoustic Tweezers (2014-12)

- **id**: `huang-penn-state-2014-tsaw-acoustic-tweezers`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Tony Jun Huang group (Penn State, later Duke)
- **disclosure**: Ding X., Peng Z., Lin S.-C. S., et al. (Huang lab), PNAS 111(36):12992-12997 (2014), doi:10.1073/pnas.1413325111
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses tilted-angle standing surface acoustic wave (taSSAW) chip wherein two opposing IDTs generate a standing wave at an angle to the flow direction; particles experience a pressure-gradient force that translates them across streamlines as a function of size and compressibility. Anticipates: (i) SSAW-based label-free cell sorters with continuous outflow; (ii) acoustic separators tuned by transducer angle rather than frequency; (iii) integration of taSSAW with downstream sequencing inputs (e.g., CTC enrichment). Companion to Ding 2012 acoustic-tweezer entry but adds productizable separation geometry.

## Duke SAW Single-Cell Acoustic Printer (2019)

- **id**: `wang-duke-2019-saw-single-cell-printer`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: dispenser-pipettor
- **creator**: Wang/Huang collaborators (Duke)
- **disclosure**: Collins D. J., Neild A., et al. (Wang/Huang collaborators), Lab on a Chip 19, 1174 (2019), doi:10.1039/C8LC01323C
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a SAW-driven single-cell printer in which a focused IDT delivers an acoustic pulse calibrated to eject a single cell-containing droplet into a target well. Anticipates: (i) acoustic droplet ejection devices specifically configured for single-cell deposition; (ii) closed-loop image-triggered SAW ejection feedback; (iii) coupling acoustic single-cell printers to downstream genomic library prep. Distinguished from Echo acoustic dispenser (compound dispensing) by single-cell biological payload and image-triggered firing.
