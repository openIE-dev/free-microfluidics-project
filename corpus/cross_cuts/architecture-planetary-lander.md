## Hayabusa-2 MASCOT Lander Sample-Handling Subsystem (2017)

- **id**: `hayabusa2-mascot-lander-sample-handling`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: DLR / CNES (Mascot lead: Tra-Mi Ho)
- **disclosure**: Ho, T.-M. et al. 'MASCOT - The Mobile Asteroid Surface Scout Onboard the Hayabusa-2 Mission.' Space Science Reviews 208, 339-374 (2017), doi:10.1007/s11214-016-0251-6; CNES/DLR MASCOT mission documentation 2018
- **ip status**: unknown
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the MASCOT lander, a 10-kg companion to Hayabusa-2 hosting four instruments inside a sealed dry-N2 enclosure that doubles as thermal-protection and contamination control for the MMEGA imaging spectrometer and MARA radiometer. Anticipates: (a) compact sealed-volume payload architecture for low-mass asteroid surface landers; (b) reference-blackbody calibration cavity integrated into a sub-1-kg radiometer; (c) hopping mobility actuator using eccentric-mass tumbler in vacuum/microgravity environment. Cite against later patents claiming sealed-N2 lander cavities for asteroid contact instruments.

## ISRO Chandrayaan-3 ChaSTE / Pragyan Rover Fluidic Instruments (2023)

- **id**: `isro-chandrayaan3-chaste-pragyan-instrument`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) / Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre / Physical Research Laboratory
- **disclosure**: ISRO Chandrayaan-3 mission press release 2023-08-23 (lunar landing); Karanam, D. P. et al. 'ChaSTE - Chandra's Surface Thermophysical Experiment onboard Chandrayaan-3.' Acta Astronautica 218, 230-237 (2024), doi:10.1016/j.actaastro.2024.01.034
- **ip status**: unknown
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Pragyan rover instrument suite. The APXS sealed alpha-source housing and LIBS internal Argon calibration reservoir constitute fluidic prior art for: (a) sealed radioisotope alpha-source modules in lunar instruments operating across thermal extremes (-180 to +120 C lunar diurnal); (b) on-instrument inert-gas calibration reservoirs feeding LIBS plasma generators (used to maintain wavelength reference in vacuum); (c) deployment-actuator architecture for thermal-probe penetrators in unconsolidated regolith. Cite against later patent claims for integrated calibration-gas-reservoir LIBS instruments on planetary rovers.
