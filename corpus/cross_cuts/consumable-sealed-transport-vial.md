- **disclosure**: Codex: Space Marines 2nd ed. (Games Workshop, 1995); Index Astartes I (Games Workshop, 2002); detailed mechanics in Soul Drinkers Omnibus (Ben Counter, Black Library, 2007, ISBN 978-1-84416-456-4)

- **prior art notes**: Discloses a dedicated organ-explant tool distinct from the narthecium that bores through chest plate and skull, locates a specific implanted endocrine organ (the progenoid gland), aspirates and isolates it, and deposits it directly into a sealed pre-cooled cryogenic vial integrated with the tool.
