## Canon Oce ProStream 1800 Continuous-Feed Inkjet Press (2017)

- **id**: `canon-oce-prostream-1800-continuous-feed-inkjet`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Canon Production Printing (formerly Oce Printing Systems GmbH, Poing, Germany)
- **disclosure**: Canon Oce ProStream 1800 launch announcement drupa 2016 (full availability 2017); ProStream 1800 datasheet 2017
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a continuous-feed commercial inkjet press whose key architectural primitive is in-line aqueous fluid pre-treatment (ColorGrip) applied to uncoated offset stock immediately before the inkjet bars, allowing pigment ink to print on standard paper rather than requiring inkjet-treated stock. The DigiDot piezo drive achieves 6 grayscale levels per native dot via Kyocera variable-drop firing. Anticipates: continuous-feed inkjet presses using in-line pre-treatment fluid + multi-level grayscale piezo + Kyocera printhead arrays organized as a stitched bar across web width. The combined architecture (pre-treatment bar -> 4-color Kyocera printhead array -> drying tower -> fixing) defines the modern Production-Printing inkjet web press category.
