## Reynolds 1886/1903 — Reynolds equation of lubrication theory (thin-film flow) (1886)

- **id**: `reynolds-1903-lubrication-theory`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Osborne Reynolds
- **disclosure**: O. Reynolds, 'On the theory of lubrication and its application to Mr. Beauchamp Tower's experiments', Phil. Trans. Roy. Soc. London 177, 157-234 (1886); reprinted in O. Reynolds, Scientific Papers vol 2 (Cambridge Univ. Press, 1901)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Foundational derivation of the Reynolds lubrication equation governing thin-film flow between nearly parallel surfaces. Foundational for: Hele-Shaw cell microfluidics, valve-membrane closure dynamics, squeeze-film damping in MEMS, and any microfluidic process where the flow occurs in a gap of small aspect ratio. Anticipates: claims to 'thin-film microfluidic device' or 'membrane valve with controlled closure dynamics' whose operating physics is the Reynolds equation — derivation is 140 years old.
