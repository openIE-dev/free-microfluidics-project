- **device class**: printer-tooling

- **disclosure**: Continuus Pharmaceuticals technical bulletin 2017-2019; Mascia, S. et al. 'End-to-end continuous manufacturing of pharmaceuticals: integrated synthesis, purification, and final dosage formation.' Angewandte Chemie International Edition 52, 12359-12363 (2013), doi:10.1002/anie.201305429
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Continuus iCMM end-to-end continuous manufacturing module integrating API synthesis, MSMPR crystallization, filtration, drying, and tablet formation in a single skid. Cite against later patents claiming end-to-end continuous manufacturing modules with integrated MSMPR crystallization.
