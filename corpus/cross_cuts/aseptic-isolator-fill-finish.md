## Vanrx (Cytiva) Microcell aseptic vial filler workcell (2018)

- **id**: `vanrx-microcell-aseptic-workcell`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: dispenser-pipettor
- **creator**: Cytiva (Vanrx Pharmasystems)
- **disclosure**: Vanrx Microcell launch (2018; Vanrx acquired by Cytiva 2021); https://www.cytivalifesciences.com/en/us/shop/aseptic-fill-finish/equipment/microcell; US10843820B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a compact gloveless-isolator aseptic vial filler optimized for clinical-trial and personalized-medicine batch sizes (10-1000 nested vials), with robotic vial pickup, peristaltic-pump dose delivery, stoppering, and seal-crimp inside an HEPA-filtered Grade A isolator that is decontaminated by VHP. The Microcell complements the production-scale SA25 workcell in the same product family. Anticipates: clinical-scale gloveless aseptic-isolator fillers for personalized CGT, autologous cell-therapy, and small-batch mRNA-LNP fill-finish; specifically the architectural pattern of using a fully robotic gloveless isolator at small batch scale to eliminate operator-glove contamination paths in low-volume aseptic operations.
