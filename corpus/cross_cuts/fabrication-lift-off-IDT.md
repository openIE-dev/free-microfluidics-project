## Friend & Yeo SAW Microfluidics Review (2011-04)

- **id**: `friend-yeo-2011-saw-microfluidics-review`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: James Friend & Leslie Yeo (RMIT/Monash)
- **disclosure**: Friend J., Yeo L. Y., Reviews of Modern Physics 83, 647 (2011), doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.83.647
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: This Reviews of Modern Physics article codifies the field of surface-acoustic-wave (SAW) microfluidics, providing element-by-element disclosures of: (a) interdigital transducer (IDT) layouts on Y-cut/X-propagating lithium niobate for Rayleigh SAW generation; (b) acoustic streaming inside sessile droplets via leaky-SAW coupling; (c) standing-SAW pressure-node manipulation of particles in microchannels coupled to PDMS overlays; (d) SAW-driven atomization for nebulizer applications. Anticipates broad downstream patent claims to IDT-driven droplet manipulation, SAW pumping, and SAW-induced acoustic streaming for mixing or particle focusing in PDMS-on-LiNbO3 hybrid devices. Provides 102 disclosure for any combination of these elements claimed after April 2011.
