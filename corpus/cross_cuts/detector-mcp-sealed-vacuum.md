## ISRO Aditya-L1 PAPA (Plasma Analyser Package for Aditya) (2023)

- **id**: `isro-aditya-l1-papa-payload`

- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) / Space Physics Laboratory, VSSC
- **disclosure**: ISRO Aditya-L1 mission documentation 2023-09-02 launch; PAPA payload technical documentation, Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre 2023
- **ip status**: unknown
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the PAPA payload aboard Aditya-L1 with sealed-vacuum MCP detector housing including bake-out fluidics (vacuum purge ports and resistive heaters) for in-flight contamination removal. Anticipates: (a) sealed-MCP detector architectures qualified for L1 Halo-orbit thermal cycling; (b) on-orbit detector bake-out fluidics as a contamination-control technique for solar-wind analyzers; (c) shared mechanical/fluidic interface between SWEEP and SWICAR for cross-instrument calibration. Cite against later patent claims for sealed-MCP solar-wind analyzers with on-orbit bake-out.
