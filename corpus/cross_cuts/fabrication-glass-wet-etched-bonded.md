## 908 Devices ZipChip Capillary Electrophoresis-MS Interface (2015)

- **id**: `908-devices-zipchip`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: 908 Devices Inc. (UNC Chapel Hill / Ramsey-lab spinout)
- **disclosure**: Mellors et al., Anal. Chem. 2008, 80, 6881-6887 (UNC academic predecessor) DOI:10.1021/ac800428w; 908 Devices ZipChip product launch 2015; commercial datasheet 2015.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Microfluidic CE-ESI chip combining capillary electrophoresis separation with integrated nano-electrospray emission on a single etched-glass substrate. Discloses: wet-etched glass channels with bonded cover; co-integrated CE separation channel and ESI emitter with co-fabricated orifice; high-voltage electrode interface for combined CE + ESI biasing. Anticipates: integrated CE-ESI chips for direct coupling to commercial mass spectrometers; intact-mAb charge-variant analysis using glass-chip CE-ESI; 3-min total analysis times competitive with traditional CE-MS that takes >30 min. Founding 908 Devices technology.
