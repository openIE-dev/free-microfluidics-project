## Sysmex CA-1500 automated coagulation analyzer (historical) (1995)

- **id**: `sysmex-ca-1500-coag-analyzer-historical`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Sysmex Corporation
- **disclosure**: Sysmex Corporation CA-1500 product brochure (1995); 1990s-era operator manuals
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a benchtop automated coagulation analyzer using a rotating cuvette wheel with photometric clot detection (scattered-light at 660-800 nm). Anticipates: rotating-cuvette automated coagulation platforms with optical clot endpoint detection. Historically important as one of the first widely-deployed automated coagulation analyzers below the high-throughput tier; predecessor architecture for the CS-2500 and CN-3000.

## Sysmex CA-2500 automated coagulation analyzer (historical) (2002)

- **disclosure**: Sysmex Corporation CA-2500 product brochure (2002); operator manuals

## Sysmex CN-3000 automated coagulation analyzer (2020)

- **id**: `sysmex-cn-3000-coag-analyzer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Sysmex Corporation
- **disclosure**: Sysmex Corporation product brochure CN-3000 (2020); CE-IVD declarations; FDA 510(k) clearances
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a fully-automated benchtop coagulation analyzer using glass cuvettes and a thermoplastic rotor for sample/reagent handling. Each cuvette is incubated, dosed with reagent, and monitored optically for clot formation by scattered-light intensity. Anticipates: high-throughput coagulation analyzers integrating cuvette transport, photometric and scattered-light detection, and automated reagent dosing in a single benchtop platform. Distinguishable from earlier Sysmex CA-series by higher throughput and integrated track-interface for laboratory automation.
