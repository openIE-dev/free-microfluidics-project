## Microsaic 4500 MiD Compact Mass Spectrometer (2014)

- **id**: `microsaic-4500-mid`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Microsaic Systems plc
- **disclosure**: Microsaic Systems plc Annual Report 2014; product launch 2014; Wells et al., Org. Process Res. Dev. 2015, 19, 1500-1506.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Successor to 4000 MiD with extended mass range and improved sensitivity. Discloses: refined silicon ChipSpray emitter with longer service life (>30 days continuous); extended mass range to 2000 Da to cover small biomolecules; integrated GMP-compliant PAT software stack. Anticipates: in-line process-MS for pharmaceutical kilo-lab synthesis monitoring; the architectural distinction between centralized lab-MS and at-reaction MS using MEMS emitters.
