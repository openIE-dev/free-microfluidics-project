- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Specialty Coffee Association
- **disclosure**: Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) Coffee Taste Initiative whitepaper 'Standardized microfluidic taste references for SCA cupping protocols' (2023)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a microfluidic sensor format (electrochemistry on screen-printed graphene electrodes under a thermoplastic micro-channel) for objective measurement of acidity, body, and bitterness as references for human cupping protocols.
