## Moving-boundary electrophoresis (Tiselius apparatus) (1937)

- **id**: `tiselius-1937-moving-boundary-electrophoresis`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Arne Tiselius (Uppsala University)
- **disclosure**: Tiselius, A. A new apparatus for electrophoretic analysis of colloidal mixtures. Trans. Faraday Soc. 1937, 33, 524-531. DOI: 10.1039/tf9373300524
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: The founding apparatus of analytical electrophoresis. Tiselius separated serum proteins by mobility in free solution and detected the boundaries optically, establishing electrophoresis as a quantitative analytical separation. Anticipates: electrophoretic separation of biomolecules by charge-to-size ratio, optical detection of separated zones, and the entire conceptual basis of capillary and microchip electrophoresis. Every microfluidic CE chip, gel-on-chip, and isoelectric-focusing device traces to this method; the separation principle cannot be claimed as novel over it.
