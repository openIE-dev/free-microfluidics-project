## IntelliCyt iQue Screener Plus / Forecyt Software (2011)

- **id**: `intellicyt-ique-screener-plus`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: IntelliCyt Corp (acquired by Sartorius / Essen Bioscience)
- **disclosure**: IntelliCyt iQue Screener launch (2011); IntelliCyt acquired by Sartorius 2016; iQue3 product brochure 2019; US patent 8,431,386
- **ip status**: patented

Anticipates: (i) air-gap-segmented sample loading architectures for multi-well-to-flow-cytometer feed; (ii) plate-context-aware analysis software (Forecyt) tied to cytometric data; (iii) high-throughput suspension-cell screening workflows. Sister to BD HTS Sampler but distinct architecture.

## Sony SH800 Microfluidic Cell Sorter (2013)

- **id**: `sony-sh800-microfluidic-cell-sorter`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Sony Biotechnology Inc. (formerly iCyt acquisition)
- **disclosure**: Sony Biotechnology SH800 launch 2013; product brochure SH800S; US patent 9,134,221
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: SH800 discloses a flow cytometric cell sorter built around a single-use, factory-aligned microfluidic sort chip integrating the flow cell, nozzle, and piezoelectric droplet break-off transducer. Anticipates: (i) disposable-microfluidic-chip cell sorters with chip-encoded calibration parameters; (ii) cartridge-based contamination isolation in FACS workflows; (iii) automated nozzle-size selection via cartridge ID. Lineage-distinct from BD FACSAria (manual stainless nozzle) and NanoCellect WOLF (cartridge with no droplet-in-air sort).

## NanoCellect WOLF Cell Sorter (2017)

- **id**: `nanocellect-wolf-microfluidic-sorter`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: NanoCellect Biomedical Inc.
- **disclosure**: NanoCellect Biomedical Inc. WOLF launch press release 2017; product datasheet WOLF G2; US patent 9,372,144
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: WOLF discloses a disposable thermoplastic microfluidic cartridge with a piezoelectric microvalve that diverts target droplets into collection wells; gentle pressures and short residence times preserve viability of sensitive cells (stem, primary T cells). Anticipates: (i) cartridge-based gentle cell sorters distinct from droplet-in-air FACS; (ii) piezo-microvalve-actuated sort decisions in microfluidic chips; (iii) sterile single-use sample paths for clinical-adjacent sorting. Distinguished from BD/Sony FACS by closed-cartridge fluidics rather than nozzle-and-deflection-plate architecture.

## Beckman Coulter CytoFLEX SRT Cell Sorter (2020)

- **id**: `beckman-cytoflex-srt-microfluidic-sort`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Beckman Coulter Life Sciences
- **disclosure**: Beckman Coulter Life Sciences CytoFLEX SRT launch 2020; product brochure CYTOFLEX-SRT-BR-EN-V2; US patent 9,470,617
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: CytoFLEX SRT discloses a benchtop cell sorter with avalanche-photodiode-array detection (WDM optical bench) and piezo-driven droplet deflection sort, packaged in a compact form factor for research labs. Sister to Sony SH800 and Cytek Aurora CS but with different detection optics.
