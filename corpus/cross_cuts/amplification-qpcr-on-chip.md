- **disclosure**: Pargett D.M. et al. 'Development of a mobile ecogenomic sensor (3G ESP)' OCEANS 2015 conference (2015); Birch J.M. et al. 'Third-generation Environmental Sample Processor for autonomous in-situ molecular analysis' Limnology and Oceanography Methods (2017) doi:10.1002/lom3.10171
