- **corpus**: fictional

- **creator**: Games Workshop / Rick Priestley et al.
- **disclosure**: Warhammer 40,000 Rogue Trader era; codified in Codex: Space Marines (Games Workshop, 2nd ed. 1995, ISBN 1-872372-92-X); detailed in Codex: Space Marines 4th ed. (Games Workshop, 2004); novel-length depictions in Aaron Dembski-Bowden's Soul Hunter (Black Library, 2010, ISBN 978-1-84416-841-8) and Helsreach (Black Library, 2010, ISBN 978-1-84416-842-5)
- **ip status**: fictional
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a wrist-mounted handheld medical multitool combining (a) a multi-needle injector array drawing from a clip of pre-loaded combat drug ampoules (anaesthetic, stimulant, anti-coagulant suppressor for geneseed extraction), (b) an integrated bone-saw and reductor port for tissue retrieval, (c) a small refrigerated reservoir for stabilizing biological samples in field conditions, and (d) a diagnostic readout coupled to fluid sensors that types blood and tags samples. Element-by-element this anticipates: handheld point-of-care cartridges that combine a sample-acquisition needle, a reservoir of stabilizer reagents (ACD-A, EDTA), an on-board diagnostic readout, and a temperature-controlled storage compartment in a single battery-powered glove or gauntlet form factor. Specifically anticipates wearable forearm-mounted diagnostic+therapeutic units with multi-drug ampoule clips and integrated cold-chain reservoirs.
