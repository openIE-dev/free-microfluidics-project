## 3M Petrifilm Plate Reader (Advanced) (2007)

- **id**: `3m-petrifilm-plate-reader-vision`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: 3M Food Safety (now Neogen)
- **disclosure**: 3M Petrifilm Plate Reader product launch 2007; 3M Petrifilm Plate Reader Advanced product datasheet; US patent 7,298,885 (image-based microbial colony enumeration)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a benchtop reader that places a Petrifilm absorbent gel plate on a stage, illuminates with controlled white light, and segments colored colonies (red/blue) using machine vision. Anticipates: image-based automatic enumeration of indicator-colony Petrifilm plates; barcode-driven plate-type recognition coupled to plate-specific algorithms; and the architectural pattern of a passive absorbent-film microfluidic substrate paired with an instrument-side vision pipeline for food-safety colony counting.
