## Ottobock C-Leg Microprocessor-Controlled Hydraulic Knee (1997)

- **id**: `ottobock-c-leg-hydraulic-knee`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Ottobock SE and Co. KGaA
- **disclosure**: Ottobock C-Leg launch 1997 (OTWorld / ISPO); Kastner J et al. studies late 1990s; Ottobock 3C98 technical documentation.

- **prior art notes**: Anticipates: a microprocessor-controlled variable-orifice hydraulic damper in which valve passages are servo-actuated at high rate to modulate fluid flow resistance for prosthetic gait; sensor-fused gait-phase recognition driving closed-loop adjustment of a small hydraulic-fluid circuit.
