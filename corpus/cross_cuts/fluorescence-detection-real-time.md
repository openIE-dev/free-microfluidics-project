- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Neogen Corporation
- **disclosure**: Neogen ANSR product launch 2013; Neogen ANSR Salmonella AOAC validation report; US patent 8,632,975 (isothermal NA amplification with strand-displacement)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an 8-tube reagent strip holding lyophilized isothermal amplification reagent beads (nicking-enzyme-amplification-reaction chemistry) plus a small benchtop fluorescence reader/heater for pathogen detection in food enrichment cultures. Anticipates: lyophilized-bead reagent storage in low-cost strip-tube cartridges; isothermal NA amplification driven by pre-formed lyophilized reagent pellets reconstituted by enrichment broth aliquot; and the architectural pattern of pathogen-class-specific cartridges decoupled from instrument optics in food testing labs.
