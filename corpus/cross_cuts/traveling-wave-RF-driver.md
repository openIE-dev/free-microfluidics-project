## Waters SELECT SERIES Cyclic Ion Mobility MS (2019-06-02)

- **id**: `waters-select-series-cyclic-im`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Waters Corporation
- **disclosure**: Giles et al., Anal. Chem. 2019, 91, 8564-8573. DOI:10.1021/acs.analchem.9b01838; Waters product launch ASMS 2019.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Cyclic ion-mobility separator that circulates ions through a ring-shaped traveling-wave drift region for arbitrary path length. Discloses: stacked-ring electrode stack arranged in a closed cycle with traveling-wave RF excitation; ion injection/ejection switching electrodes that permit multi-pass operation; coupled to QToF analyzer for IM-MS. Anticipates: cyclic / re-circulating ion-mobility geometries that decouple resolving power from physical drift length; carbohydrate and lipid isomer separation by gas-phase mobility; the architectural pattern of multi-pass IM as the key alternative to extending physical drift length.
