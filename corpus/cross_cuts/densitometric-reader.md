## Neogen Reveal Lateral Flow Test Family (1999)

- **id**: `neogen-reveal-lateral-flow-allergen`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Neogen Corporation
- **disclosure**: Neogen Reveal product launch 1999; Neogen Reveal product datasheets; US patent 6,001,658 (lateral flow allergen detection)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Neogen's flagship lateral flow product family for food safety. Anticipates: mycotoxin-specific competitive lateral flow architectures for cereal extracts; food-allergen sandwich lateral flow strips using monoclonal capture; and the broad pattern of a vendor's instrument-readable strip ecosystem spanning allergens, toxins, and pathogens on a common cartridge platform.
