## SUSS MicroTec PiXDRO Inkjet for Semiconductor and Display (2010)

- **id**: `suss-microtec-pixdro-inkjet`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: SUSS MicroTec SE (PiXDRO line via OTB Solar / Roth & Rau acquisition)
- **disclosure**: SUSS MicroTec acquisition of OTB Solar / Roth & Rau printing assets 2014; PiXDRO LP50 datasheet 2010 (originally Roth & Rau heritage); PiXDRO IP410 datasheet
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a research-and-pilot-line inkjet platform for semi/display industries. Architectural primitives parallel Notion Systems n.jet (precision XY + multi-vendor printhead) but specifically address 300mm-wafer substrate handling and integration with semi-fab cluster tools. Anticipates: semi-fab compatible inkjet deposition tools using piezo printhead arrays on 300mm wafer substrates within a SEMI-standard equipment-front-end-module configuration.

## Komori Impremia IS29 B2 Sheetfed Inkjet Press (2014)

- **id**: `komori-impremia-is29-b2-sheetfed-inkjet`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Komori Corporation (Tokyo)
- **disclosure**: Komori Impremia IS29 product launch IGAS 2013, full commercial release 2014; Impremia IS29 datasheet 2015
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a B2 sheetfed UV-inkjet press that combines an offset-press sheet transport (Komori Lithrone heritage) with a Konica Minolta piezo printhead array and inline UV-LED cure. The architectural primitive is the marriage of (offset-press sheetfed paper path + UV-curing piezo inkjet bar) producing offset-equivalent quality on B2 sheets. Anticipates: B2 sheetfed inkjet presses using a piezo printhead bar over an offset-derived gripper-driven sheet path with inline UV cure, replacing wet offset for jobs below the offset breakeven. Sister product Impremia IS32 extends to 32-inch sheets.

## Notion Systems n.jet Display Manufacturing Inkjet (2014)

- **id**: `notion-systems-njet-display-inkjet`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Notion Systems GmbH
- **disclosure**: Notion Systems GmbH (Schwetzingen, Germany) n.jet product launch 2014; n.jet flex datasheet
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an industrial inkjet platform purpose-built for display manufacturing rather than graphic arts. Architectural primitives include: (1) sub-2-micron drop placement accuracy via precision granite-base XY stage; (2) inert atmosphere process chamber preventing OLED emitter oxidation during deposition; (3) modular printhead mounting accepting multiple commercial printhead families. Anticipates: industrial inkjet platforms for display fabrication (OLED, QLED, color filter) using precision XY stages with inert process atmospheres and modular multi-vendor printhead support. The (precision XY + inert chamber + modular printhead + sub-2um drop placement) configuration defines the display-manufacturing inkjet category as distinct from graphic-arts piezo systems.

## Canon Oce VarioPrint i-Series Cut-Sheet Inkjet Press (2015)

- **id**: `canon-oce-varioprint-i-series-cut-sheet-inkjet`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Canon Production Printing
- **disclosure**: Canon Oce VarioPrint i300 launch 2015; VarioPrint iX product datasheet 2020
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Cut-sheet adaptation of the continuous-feed inkjet press concept. Discloses a vacuum-belt cut-sheet transport synchronized to a stitched Kyocera piezo bar, with the architectural innovation of a direct duplex-perfecting path (sheet inverted in-line and second side printed without leaving the press). Anticipates: cut-sheet inkjet presses using a single-pass duplex-perfecting transport coupled to a multi-color stitched Kyocera printhead bar over a vacuum-belt sheet path. Useful prior art against any single-pass duplex inkjet sheetfed press claim.

## Konica Minolta AccurioJet KM-1 B2 UV Inkjet Press (2015)

- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Konica Minolta Inc. (in technology partnership with Komori for transport)

## Komori Impremia NS40 B1 Nanographic Sheetfed Inkjet Press (2018)

- **id**: `komori-impremia-ns40-b1-sheetfed-inkjet`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Komori Corporation in collaboration with Landa Corporation
- **disclosure**: Komori Impremia NS40 launch drupa 2016, full commercial release 2018; Komori-Landa joint announcement 2014
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Komori-licensed implementation of Landa's nanographic process on a B1 sheetfed press. The Landa architectural primitive is the indirect transfer: ink jetted onto a heated rubber blanket where water flashes off leaving an ultra-thin pigment film that is then offset-transferred to the sheet (paper sees no water; works on any stock). Anticipates: any offset/litho-style sheetfed press using piezo inkjet onto a heated intermediate blanket with mechanical transfer to substrate. Combines Landa nanographic patents with Komori sheetfed transport patents.

## Landa S10 Nanographic Sheetfed Press (2018)

- **id**: `landa-s10-nanographic-press`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Landa Corporation (Benny Landa)
- **disclosure**: Landa S10 launch drupa 2012 (concept), commercial shipment 2018; Landa Corporation S10 datasheet
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Landa's flagship nanographic press. Discloses the full nanographic architectural primitive: (1) NanoInk - aqueous pigment dispersion with sub-100nm pigment particles enabling ultra-thin transferred films; (2) inkjet-onto-blanket rather than inkjet-onto-paper, with the blanket heated to flash off water; (3) mechanical transfer from blanket to substrate at the impression nip; (4) the resulting print sits on top of the substrate rather than absorbing into it, enabling print on any stock. Anticipates: a wide swath of indirect-transfer inkjet press claims, particularly the use of a heated intermediate blanket as the imaging surface in a digital-offset hybrid. Foundational to the entire nanographic process category.
