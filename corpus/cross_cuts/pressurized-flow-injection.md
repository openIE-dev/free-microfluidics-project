## Hach BioTector B7000 / B3500 TOC Analyzer (2000)

- **id**: `hach-biotector-b7000-toc-flow-cell`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Hach Company / BioTector Analytical Systems Ltd.
- **disclosure**: BioTector Analytical Systems Ltd. B7000 product launch 2000; Hach BioTector B7000 datasheet; US patent 6,723,565 (two-stage advanced oxidation TOC)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the BioTector two-stage advanced oxidation reactor architecture for online TOC analysis: pressurized sample is mixed with NaOH, acidified, oxidized in a hot persulfate stage with hydroxyl-radical generation, and CO2 is quantified by NDIR through a PTFE flow cell. Anticipates: oxidation-then-NDIR TOC architectures with self-cleaning chemistry tolerant of fouling water streams; integrated flow-cell + reactor PTFE assemblies in process water analyzers; and the architectural pattern of advanced-oxidation chemistry as the primary anti-fouling primitive for industrial TOC.
