- **disclosure**: Drägerwerk AG product brochure 'DrugTest 5000' (2009); US7943386B2 'Lateral flow drug test cartridge with optical reader'
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use oral-fluid drug-screen cartridge integrating saliva collection, capillary microfluidic transport, lateral-flow immunoassay strips for THC, COC, OPI, AMP, MET, METH, BZD, KET, and an optical reader providing roadside positive/negative readings.
