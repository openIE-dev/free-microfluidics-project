## Cellix Vena8 Biochip with Magnetofluidic Bead Capture (2009)

- **id**: `cellix-vena8-magnetofluidics-chip`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Cellix Limited (Trinity College Dublin spinout)
- **disclosure**: Cellix Limited Vena8 product launch 2009; Lynch I., Cellix product literature; product page https://www.cellixltd.com/
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Cellix Vena8 discloses an 8-channel COC biochip operated with the Mirus Nanopump for precise pressure-driven shear, with external rare-earth magnet arrays applied to capture magnetic-bead-labelled cells against the channel floor. Anticipates: (i) parallel multi-channel COC chips for shear-controlled cell adhesion assays; (ii) integration of external magnet arrays with disposable polymer chips for magnetofluidic bead capture; (iii) productized flow chambers for platelet adhesion testing.
