## Sun Chemical Streamline / SunJet Inkjet Ink Family (2002)

- **id**: `sun-chemical-streamline-inkjet-ink`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Sun Chemical Corporation (DIC Group, SunJet division)
- **disclosure**: Sun Chemical SunJet division formed 2002 (Cambridge UK); SunJet Crystal UV-curable inkjet ink technical data sheet 2010; Streamline aqueous inkjet ink datasheet
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Material/consumable entry. Sun Chemical's SunJet division is a major commercial supplier of inkjet inks to OEM press manufacturers; their architectural contribution is printhead-specific ink tuning - the recognition that the same pigment chemistry must be reformulated (viscosity, surface tension, particle size, additive package) for each target printhead family. Anticipates: ink-supplier business models and ink formulations specifically tuned to commercial printhead families, particularly low-migration UV-curable inks for food-contact packaging applications.
