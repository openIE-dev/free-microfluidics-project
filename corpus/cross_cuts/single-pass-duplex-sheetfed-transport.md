## Konica Minolta AccurioJet KM-1 B2 UV Inkjet Press (2015)

- **id**: `konica-minolta-accuriojet-km1-b2-uv-inkjet-press`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Konica Minolta Inc. (in technology partnership with Komori for transport)
- **disclosure**: Konica Minolta KM-1 launch drupa 2012 (concept), commercial release 2015; KM-1e refresh 2018; Konica Minolta KM-1 datasheet 2015
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: B2 sheetfed UV inkjet press combining Konica Minolta's KM1024 piezo printhead bar with low-energy UV-LED inline cure. Architectural primitives include the use of UV-LED rather than mercury UV (eliminates ozone, reduces cooling load) and a single-pass duplex sheet transport with face-up-to-face-down inversion between print stations. Anticipates: B2 sheetfed UV inkjet presses with UV-LED cure and single-pass duplex transport using piezo printhead bars across the full sheet width.
