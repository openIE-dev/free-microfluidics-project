## Stokes 1851 — On the effect of internal friction of fluids on the motion of pendulums (Stokes drag, low-Re flow) (1851)

- **id**: `stokes-1851-viscous-flow-sphere`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: George Gabriel Stokes
- **disclosure**: G.G. Stokes, 'On the effect of the internal friction of fluids on the motion of pendulums', Trans. Camb. Phil. Soc. 9, 8-106 (1851)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Foundational analysis of viscous flow at low Reynolds number; derives the Stokes drag formula F=6πµaU on a sphere — the canonical scaling for micro-particle motion in microfluidic channels. Anticipates: any patent that recites Stokes-drag-based separation, particle settling, or microbead capture in microfluidic flow as its operative physics. Required prior art for any 'particle separation by drag balance' claim. Also foundational for cell-handling microfluidics where Stokes drag balances against electrophoretic, dielectrophoretic, acoustic, or magnetic forces.

## Bruus 2008 — Theoretical Microfluidics (textbook) (2008-01-10)

- **id**: `bruus-2008-theoretical-microfluidics-textbook`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Henrik Bruus
- **disclosure**: ISBN 978-0199235094; Oxford University Press, 2008
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Definitive theoretical textbook covering the full Navier-Stokes/electrokinetic/acoustofluidic framework for microfluidic devices, with closed-form analytical solutions and dimensionless-number scaling. Includes complete derivations of: Hagen-Poiseuille flow in arbitrary cross-sections, Stokes drag, electroosmotic flow with EDL, capillary filling, acoustic radiation forces, and dielectrophoresis. Anticipates: any patent claim whose only novelty is the application of one of these textbook physics to an obvious microfluidic geometry. Frequently cited in invalidity contentions for 'novel acoustofluidic device' or 'novel electrokinetic separator' claims that merely re-implement textbook physics.
