## UCL Goyanes Group Printlets (3D-Printed Personalized Tablets) (2014)

- **id**: `ucl-goyanes-printlets-3d-tablets`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: University College London (PI: Abdul Basit, Alvaro Goyanes, Simon Gaisford)
- **disclosure**: Goyanes, A.; Buanz, A. B. M.; Basit, A. W.; Gaisford, S. 'Fused-filament 3D printing (3DP) for fabrication of tablets.' International Journal of Pharmaceutics 476, 88-92 (2014), doi:10.1016/j.ijpharm.2014.09.044; Goyanes, A. et al. 'Automated therapy preparation of isoleucine formulations using 3D printing for the treatment of MSUD: First single-centre, prospective, crossover study in patients.' International Journal of Pharmaceutics 567, 118497 (2019)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the UCL/FabRx body of academic literature establishing FDM 3D printing as a feasible route for pharmaceutical solid oral dosage forms. Anticipates: (a) the use of geometric design (shape, infill density, surface area) as a primary lever to control drug-dissolution kinetics independent of formulation; (b) multi-active 'polypill' tablets manufactured in a single 3D print job; (c) clinical translation pathway for personalized 3D-printed tablets in pediatric inborn-errors-of-metabolism patients. Cite against later patents claiming geometric-control-of-dissolution 3D-printed pharmaceutical tablets.
