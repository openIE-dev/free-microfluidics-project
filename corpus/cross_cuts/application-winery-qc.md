## Anton Paar DMA Density Meter U-Tube Microfluidic Flow Cell (Wine Configuration) (2003)

- **id**: `anton-paar-dma-wine-density-flow-cell`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Anton Paar GmbH
- **disclosure**: Anton Paar GmbH product brochure 'DMA 4500 Density Meter' (2003); Anton Paar 'Alcolyzer Wine ME' brochure (2008)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an oscillating-U-tube glass micro-flow cell as the measuring element of a benchtop wine density meter, with a coupled NIR module determining ethanol content. Anticipates: (a) glass U-tube microfluidic resonator as a wine density sensor; (b) coupling of a microfluidic resonator to NIR for combined density+alcohol on a single instrument; (c) OIV-compliant winery-floor microfluidic alcohol determination. Borderline as microfluidic but qualifies as a sealed micro-volume oscillating flow cell.

## FOSS WineScan Auto Wine Analyzer (2008)

- **id**: `foss-winescan-auto-ftir`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: FOSS Analytical A/S
- **disclosure**: FOSS Analytical A/S product datasheet 'WineScan Auto' (2008); FOSS press release 2008-09 announcing automated 120-sample/hr wine FT-IR throughput
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an automated FT-IR wine analyzer with a microfluidic CaF2 flow cell and integrated autosampler delivering >100 samples/hour. Anticipates: (a) high-throughput FT-IR microfluidic flow-cell architecture for wine; (b) winery and contract-lab automation of a microfluidic spectrometer; (c) standardized 12+ parameter wine fingerprint readout per sample. Does not disclose CaF2 window bonding method.

## FOSS WineScan SO2 FT-IR Wine Analyzer (2012)

- **id**: `foss-winescan-so2-ftir`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: FOSS Analytical A/S
- **disclosure**: FOSS Analytical A/S product datasheet 'WineScan SO2' (2012); OIV-MA-AS323-04B reference for free SO2 in wine
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a benchtop wine analyzer with an integrated CaF2 microfluidic FT-IR flow cell plus a gas-diffusion microfluidic SO2 module that converts free sulfite to gas-phase SO2 across a hydrophobic membrane and reads it by IR. Anticipates: (a) on-chip gas-diffusion separation as a wine SO2 microfluidic measurement; (b) the architectural pattern of an FT-IR flow cell coupled to a chemistry module on a single instrument; (c) winery deployment of a microfluidic gas-diffusion module replacing manual Ripper titration. Does not disclose membrane chemistry detail.

## Erbslöh Geisenheim Wine Cartridge Test Kits (2016)

- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Erbslöh Geisenheim AG
- **disclosure**: Erbslöh Geisenheim AG product brochure 'Quick Test Cartridges for Wine Analysis' (2016)
