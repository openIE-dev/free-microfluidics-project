## Twist Bioscience Silicon-Chip Oligo Synthesis Patent Family (2015)

- **id**: `twist-bioscience-silicon-chip-oligo-patent-family`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Twist Bioscience Corporation
- **disclosure**: US10384188B2 'High resolution silicon-based substrate for chemical synthesis' (priority 2015); US10618024B2; Twist Bioscience commercial launch 2016
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Twist's silicon-substrate platform for high-density parallel oligonucleotide synthesis: a silicon wafer DRIE-etched into millions of nanoliter wells, organized into addressable clusters of ~121 wells each, with phosphoramidite reagents delivered by inkjet-style precision dispensing. Anticipates: (a) silicon DRIE wells as substrate for solid-phase phosphoramidite chemistry; (b) cluster-addressed parallel synthesis where one cluster yields one gene-length pool; (c) the combination of high-density silicon substrates with non-contact piezoelectric dispense for reagent delivery. Material to invalidity contentions on Agilent SurePrint or Roche Madison ink-jet array patents asserting required glass or fiber substrates.

## Twist Bioscience DNA Data Storage Patent Family (2017)

- **id**: `twist-bioscience-dna-storage-patent-family`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Twist Bioscience Corporation
- **disclosure**: US10907274B2 'Methods for storing information on nucleic acids' (priority 2017); WO2018191525A1; Twist + Microsoft Research collaboration disclosed 2016
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses methods for encoding arbitrary digital data into oligonucleotide pools synthesized on Twist's silicon platform, including codec design, error-correction blocks, addressable index regions, and storage-and-retrieval protocols using selective PCR amplification. Anticipates: (a) any DNA-based archival storage workflow employing silicon-substrate parallel synthesis as the write head; (b) combination of address-region indexing with reed-solomon-style ECC for random-access retrieval; (c) the architectural pattern of separating data payload, address, and ECC regions within fixed-length oligos.
