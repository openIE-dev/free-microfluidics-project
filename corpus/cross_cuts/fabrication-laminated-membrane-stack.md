- **id**: `lumiradx-hba1c-cartridge`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: LumiraDx Limited (now LumiraDx Group)
- **disclosure**: LumiraDx Limited HbA1c product launch 2020-08; CE-mark 2020; FDA 510(k) K222085 2023-04.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Adds HbA1c assay to the LumiraDx microfluidic strip platform (parent platform already in corpus as lumiradx-platform-microfluidic-strip). Discloses: capillary-blood HbA1c quantitation via boronate-affinity chemistry on the LumiraDx microfluidic strip with on-strip RBC lysis, glycated-Hb capture, and quantitative reflectance / fluorescence readout. Anticipates: cartridge-format HbA1c at the point of care via boronate-affinity chemistry; integrated capillary-blood handling + RBC lysis + Hb fractionation in a microfluidic strip; extension of the same strip platform to additional clinical chemistry analytes (Hb, INR, NT-proBNP, troponin) sharing the strip mechanical architecture.
