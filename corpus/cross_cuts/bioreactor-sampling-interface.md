## 908 Devices REBEL Single-Cell Metabolomics Cell Culture Analyzer (2020-09)

- **id**: `908-devices-rebel`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: 908 Devices Inc.
- **disclosure**: 908 Devices REBEL product launch 2020-09; commercial datasheet 2020.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Combines a ZipChip-style CE-ESI front end with an integrated benchtop MS for cell-culture bioprocess monitoring. Discloses: glass CE-ESI chip directly coupled to a small-footprint MS analyzer (ion-trap geometry); automated sample uptake from bioreactor culture media; multi-analyte quantitation panel for AA + metabolite + glucose / lactate. Anticipates: at-line bioprocess MS in cell-therapy and biologic-drug manufacturing; the architectural pattern of compact MS + CE-ESI chip as a benchtop replacement for centralized analytical-lab LC-MS for cell-culture monitoring; integration with single-use bioreactor sampling.
