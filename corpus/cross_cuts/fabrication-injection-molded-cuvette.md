## HemoCue Hb 201+ Capillary Hemoglobin Analyzer (1986)

- **id**: `hemocue-201-capillary-eof-analyzer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: HemoCue AB (Sweden, now Radiometer / Danaher)
- **disclosure**: HemoCue AB original Hb system launch 1982-1986; Hb 201+ product manual 2002; US patents 4,780,419 and 5,166,051
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: HemoCue Hb 201+ discloses a disposable injection-molded microcuvette containing dried sodium nitrite/azide reagents; ~10 uL of capillary blood is drawn in by surface-tension-driven capillary action, lyses, reacts to azidemethemoglobin, and is read photometrically by the dedicated reader. Anticipates: (i) capillary-action-driven dry-reagent microcuvettes for point-of-care quantitative measurement; (ii) the architectural pattern of a sealed cuvette with photometric path coincident with the reaction chamber; (iii) low-volume capillary blood quantification without external pumps. Note: this is a capillary-driven device, NOT an electroosmotic-flow analyzer; it is included to anchor the capillary-fill primitive in the corpus.
