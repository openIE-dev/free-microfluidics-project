## Applikon ez-Control + my-Control Bioreactor Controllers (2013)

- **id**: `applikon-ez-control-bioreactor`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Applikon Biotechnology (Netherlands, now part of Getinge)
- **disclosure**: Applikon Biotechnology ez-Control product brochure 2013-2017; Applikon my-Control benchtop bioreactor brochure 2015
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Applikon ez-Control / my-Control bioreactor controller family. The my-Control variant introduces a stackable miniature controller architecture for small-volume (50-1000 mL) parallel bioreactor banks at lower capex than DASGIP-class systems. Anticipates: (a) stackable miniature parallel bioreactor controllers as a low-cost academic/biotech alternative to DASGIP/ambr; (b) tower-mount + stackable hybrid controller portfolio sharing the same SCADA software stack; (c) parallel-vessel banks of 4-12 vessels at 50-1000 mL volumes for early-process-development DoE. Cite against later patents claiming stackable miniature parallel bioreactor controllers with shared SCADA software stack.
