## Shimadzu LCMS-IT-TOF Ion-Trap Time-of-Flight Mass Spectrometer (2006)

- **id**: `shimadzu-it-ms`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Shimadzu Corporation
- **disclosure**: Shimadzu LCMS-IT-TOF brochure C146-E120; product launch 2006.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Hybrid ion-trap + TOF analyzer with ESI front end. Microfluidic-relevant content: ESI source with stainless-steel needle emitter capable of stable nL/min flow regime, providing the front-end fluidic interface to the analyzer. Discloses: 3D quadrupole ion trap performing MS-n fragmentation; orthogonal acceleration into reflectron TOF for high-resolution m/z measurement; combined trap + TOF architecture as alternative to QToF or Orbitrap. Anticipates: trap-TOF hybrid mass analyzers for MS-n + accurate mass; coupling of nano-ESI to ion traps via stainless-steel emitter geometry; the architectural pattern of multi-stage fragmentation in the trap followed by accurate-mass measurement in the TOF.
