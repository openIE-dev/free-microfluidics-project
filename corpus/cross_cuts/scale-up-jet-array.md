## Knauer IJM (Impingement Jets Mixer) for mRNA-LNP Manufacturing (2021)

- **id**: `knauer-impingement-jets-mrna`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: mixer-component
- **creator**: Knauer Wissenschaftliche Geraetebau GmbH
- **disclosure**: Knauer Wissenschaftliche Geraetebau GmbH IJM product literature 2021; commercialized for BioNTech Comirnaty manufacturing.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Impingement-jet mixer (IJM) is an alternative to staggered-herringbone for high-throughput LNP formulation. Two opposing high-velocity jets (ethanol-lipid solution and aqueous-mRNA solution) collide at a sub-mm orifice, producing turbulent sub-millisecond mixing that drives LNP nucleation. Knauer commercialized the design at GMP scale during the COVID-19 vaccine manufacturing ramp; reportedly used for BioNTech/Pfizer Comirnaty production. Discloses: impingement-jet mixer geometry with controlled jet diameters and impingement chamber dimensions; stainless-steel construction with CIP/SIP capability; scale-up by jet array. Anticipates: impingement-jet-mixer LNP formulation as a high-throughput alternative to staggered-herringbone; GMP-scale stainless-steel LNP mixer for clinical mRNA vaccine manufacturing; the architectural choice between micro-mixing (NanoAssemblr) and macro-jet impingement (Knauer) for different scale-up regimes.
