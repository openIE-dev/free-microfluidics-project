## Cytiva BioWelder TC Sterile Tubing Welder (2008)

- **id**: `cytiva-biowelder-tc`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Cytiva (formerly GE Healthcare Life Sciences)
- **disclosure**: GE Healthcare BioWelder TC launch 2008; Cytiva (rebrand) datasheet 29-1014-23 AB rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a thermoplastic tubing welder supporting multiple tubing chemistries (C-Flex, PharMed, Tygon) with a disposable blade per weld cycle. Anticipates: thermoplastic-tubing-welder architectures with disposable blade-per-weld eliminating cross-contamination, supporting the broad range of single-use tubing chemistries used across CGT, mRNA, and traditional biopharma. Direct competitor to Terumo TSCD-Q and Sartorius BioWelder in the sterile-connection equipment market.

## Sartorius BioWelder TC Sterile Tubing Welder (2010)

- **id**: `sartorius-biowelder-tc`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Sartorius Stedim Biotech
- **disclosure**: Sartorius BioWelder TC product launch ~2010; product datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a Sartorius-ecosystem sterile tubing welder paired with the Biosealer TC sealer/disconnector, providing a complete weld + seal + disconnect workflow for single-use process trains. Anticipates: integrated weld-and-seal sterile-connection ecosystems where a single vendor provides both the welder (joining tubing) and the sealer (closing tubing for component removal); specifically supports the closed-system manufacturing requirement for CGT and mRNA where every connection / disconnection must maintain sterility.

## Terumo TSCD-Q Sterile Tubing Welder (2018-09)

- **id**: `terumo-tscd-q-tubing-welder`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Terumo BCT, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Terumo TSCD-Q product launch 2018-09; product datasheet rev 2024; predecessor TSCD II launched ~2005
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a sterile tubing welder using a heated copper wafer to simultaneously cut and thermal-fuse two PVC tubing ends, maintaining the sterile fluid path through the weld operation. Anticipates: sterile-tubing-welder architectures for biopharma single-use manufacturing where two pre-sterilized fluid paths are connected without aseptic-isolator manipulation; specifically the architectural pattern of disposable copper wafer per weld eliminating wafer-to-wafer cross-contamination. Critical to CGT, mRNA, and vaccine manufacturing where dozens of weld operations connect single-use components into the process train. Competes with Cytiva BioWelder TC and Sartorius BioWelder.
