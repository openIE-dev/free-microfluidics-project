## Cytiva Capto Q ImpRes Anion Exchange Resin (2010-09)

- **id**: `cytiva-capto-q-impres`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Cytiva (formerly GE Healthcare Life Sciences / Danaher)
- **disclosure**: GE Healthcare Capto Q ImpRes launch 2010-09; Cytiva (rebrand) datasheet 29-0506-39 AB rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an anion-exchange agarose-bead chromatography resin used as the polishing step after AAV affinity capture, where the Q (quaternary amine) ligand resolves full (genome-loaded) from empty (capsid-only) AAV particles based on the negative charge contribution of the encapsidated DNA. Anticipates: full / empty AAV separation by anion-exchange chromatography in AAV CGT manufacturing trains; specifically the architectural pattern of POROS-AAVX-affinity-capture → Capto-Q-ImpRes-anion-exchange-polish as the standard two-step AAV downstream process. Critical to delivering low-empty-capsid AAV product for clinical use.

## Merck KGaA Eshmuno Ex Anion Exchange Resin (2014-06)

- **id**: `merck-eshmuno-ex`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Merck KGaA / MilliporeSigma
- **disclosure**: Merck Millipore Eshmuno Ex launch 2014-06; MilliporeSigma datasheet rev 2024; US8,765,897B2
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a tentacle-polymer-grafted bead architecture with quaternary amine ligands at the tentacle tips, providing higher binding capacity at high flow rates compared to conventional ligand-coupled beads, used for mAb polishing and increasingly for AAV full / empty separation. Anticipates: tentacle-architecture chromatography resin for biopharma polishing applications; the architectural pattern of grafted-polymer-tentacle-with-tip-ligand provides accessibility advantages over surface-immobilized ligands.
