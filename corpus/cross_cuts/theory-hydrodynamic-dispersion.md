## Aris 1956 — On the dispersion of a solute in a fluid flowing through a tube (Aris extension to Taylor dispersion) (1956-04-24)

- **id**: `aris-1956-dispersion-extension`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Rutherford Aris
- **disclosure**: R. Aris, 'On the dispersion of a solute in a fluid flowing through a tube', Proc. Roy. Soc. A 235, 67-77 (1956); doi:10.1098/rspa.1956.0065
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Aris's mathematical extension of Taylor's 1953 dispersion analysis to arbitrary cross-sections and to the asymptotic-time regime, giving the canonical 'Taylor-Aris dispersion coefficient' D_eff = D + Pe^2 D / 192 (for circular tube). Anticipates: any microfluidic patent that exploits or claims to mitigate hydrodynamic dispersion in a channel — the Taylor-Aris coefficient is the standard reference value, 70 years old. Critical to chip-CE, chip-HPLC, and any continuous-flow on-chip reactor where peak broadening matters.
