## Stand on Zanzibar New-Genome Design Lab (1968)

- **id**: `brunner-stand-on-zanzibar-genome-design-lab`
- **corpus**: fictional
- **device class**: fictional-laboratory
- **creator**: John Brunner
- **disclosure**: John Brunner, 'Stand on Zanzibar' (Doubleday, 1968, ISBN 0-385-04147-9)
- **ip status**: fictional
- **prior art notes**: 1968 disclosure of a national-program-scale genome optimization facility coupling bench-scale wet labs to perfusion-tank gestation. Predates much subsequent biotech sci-fi. Element-by-element discloses (a) lead bench scientist's bioreactor prototype, (b) scale-out to national clinical infrastructure, (c) coupling to political/regulatory pipeline. Anticipates: nationally-coordinated reproductive biotech infrastructure including coupling between research-grade bioreactor prototypes and clinical-scale gestational hardware. Of historical note as one of the earliest detailed depictions of national-policy-driven germline engineering programs.
