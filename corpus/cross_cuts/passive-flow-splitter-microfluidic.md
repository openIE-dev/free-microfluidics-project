## SCIEX Eksigent ekspert nanoLC 400 / 425 Nanoflow Liquid Chromatography (2002)

- **id**: `sciex-eksigent-nanolc`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: pump-component
- **creator**: Eksigent Technologies (acquired by AB Sciex 2010)
- **disclosure**: Eksigent Technologies founding publications, Anal. Chem. 2003, 75, 4818-4825 (Tang/Smith); AB Sciex acquisition of Eksigent 2010-04; ekspert nanoLC 400 datasheet 2012.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Pioneering microfluidic flow-splitter pump for nano-LC. Discloses: a passive splitting microfluidic chip that divides high-pressure HPLC pump output into precise sub-uL/min flows for nano-ESI emitter coupling; pressure feedback maintains flow stability under variable column impedance; integrates with capillary trap-and-elute column architecture. Anticipates: chip-based passive flow splitting as the standard architecture for commercial nano-LC; the architectural pattern of splitting a high-flow (mL/min) pump output into nano-flow at the column head, with the rest dumped to waste, that has dominated nano-proteomics for 20 years.
