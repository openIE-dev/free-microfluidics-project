## Tonejet Electrostatic Charged-Particle Drop-on-Demand Printhead (1993)

- **id**: `tonejet-electrostatic-printhead`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Tonejet Limited (spun out of Cambridge Consultants)
- **disclosure**: Tonejet Ltd. (Royston, UK) founded 1993 from Cambridge Consultants spin-out; Tonejet T-Series printhead datasheet 2012; Cyclone printhead datasheet 2018; foundational US patent 5,838,349
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a fundamentally distinct drop-on-demand mechanism: charged pigment particles suspended in a non-conductive carrier fluid are extracted by an electrostatic field at an array of micro-electrodes; only the pigment particles eject (the carrier stays behind in the printhead reservoir), so the drop landing on the substrate is essentially solid pigment with negligible solvent. This is NEITHER piezo squeeze NOR thermal bubble - it is electrostatic charged-particle extraction. The architectural primitive is (charged pigment ink + electrode array + electrostatic extraction of pigment-only drop), eliminating the dry-time / penetration / mottle problems of solvent-based inkjet on non-absorbent substrates. Anticipates: any electrostatic-extraction drop-on-demand printhead using charged-particle inks. Critical prior art that defends against any patent claiming electrostatic alternatives to piezo/thermal printheads. Particularly important for can/bottle/curved-substrate decoration markets where solvent inkjet struggles.
