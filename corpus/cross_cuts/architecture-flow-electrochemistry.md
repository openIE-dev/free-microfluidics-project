## Asahi Kasei Microsep / Cation MS Flow Electrolysis Cell (2010)

- **id**: `asahi-kasei-microsep-cation-electrolyzer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Asahi Kasei Corporation (Japan)
- **disclosure**: Asahi Kasei Microsep flow electrolysis cell product brochure 2010-2014; Yoshida, J. et al. 'Cation Pool Method.' Chemical Reviews 108, 2265-2299 (2008), doi:10.1021/cr0680843
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Asahi Kasei Microsep / Cation MS flow electrolysis cell with proton-exchange membrane separation between anolyte and catholyte chambers. The architecture enables the Yoshida 'cation pool method' in which reactive cations are generated and stored in the anolyte for subsequent reaction with nucleophiles. Anticipates: (a) PEM-separated flow electrolysis cells for organic electrosynthesis; (b) cation-pool method as a synthetic strategy enabled by membrane-separated flow electrolysis; (c) industrial scale-up of flow electrosynthesis from mL/min lab-scale to kg/h production via numbering-up of identical cells. Cite against later patents claiming PEM-separated flow electrolysis cells for cation-pool organic electrosynthesis.
