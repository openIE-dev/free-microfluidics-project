## MacoPharma THERAFLEX pathogen-reduction systems (UVC platelets / MB-Plasma) (1995)

- **id**: `macopharma-theraflex-pathogen-reduction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: MacoPharma
- **disclosure**: Mohr H. et al., Photodynamic virus inactivation of blood components, Immunol Invest 24:73-85 (1995); THERAFLEX MB-Plasma in routine use from the 1990s; THERAFLEX UV-Platelets developed 2000s; MacoPharma product literature.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a methylene-blue + visible-light photodynamic plasma-treatment set with a downstream membrane filter that removes the dye and photoproducts, and a photosensitizer-free 254 nm UVC platelet-treatment set using a thin agitated illumination bag. Anticipates claims to methylene-blue plasma pathogen reduction with membrane dye removal and to photosensitizer-free UVC platelet pathogen reduction. Related to cerus-intercept-blood-system-pathogen-reduction and terumo-mirasol-prt-pathogen-reduction.
