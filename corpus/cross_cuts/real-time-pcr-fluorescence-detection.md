## Hygiena BAX System Real-Time PCR Pathogen Detection (1996)

- **id**: `hygiena-bax-system-pathogen-pcr`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Hygiena, LLC (acquired from DuPont/Qualicon)
- **disclosure**: Qualicon BAX System product launch 1996; DuPont/Hygiena BAX Salmonella AOAC OMA 2003.09; US patent 5,491,068 (PCR pathogen detection cartridge)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Foundational food-safety PCR cartridge: a polypropylene strip tube holding a single lyophilized reagent tablet containing primers, probes, polymerase, dNTPs, and stabilizers. User adds enrichment broth aliquot, dissolving the tablet, and runs real-time PCR on the Q7 instrument. Anticipates: single-tablet lyophilized PCR reagent storage in strip-tube cartridges; AOAC-recognized food-safety PCR architectures; and the architectural pattern of vendor-locked enrichment-to-PCR workflows where assay specificity is encoded entirely in the tablet chemistry.

## Roche LightCycler 480 Real-Time PCR System (2006)

- **id**: `roche-lightcycler-480-multiwell-plate`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Roche Diagnostics GmbH
- **disclosure**: Roche LightCycler 480 product launch 2006; Roche LightCycler 480 instrument operator's manual; US patent 6,303,343 (continuous fluorescence monitoring PCR), US patent 7,238,517 (multiwell qPCR plate)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Roche's white-walled multiwell qPCR cartridge architecture coupled with a high-resolution-melt-capable thermal cycler. The plate's white well walls reflect excitation light to maximize per-well fluorescence isolation; aluminum optical seals enable plate-format real-time PCR including HRM. Anticipates: white-walled multiwell qPCR plates as the dominant clinical-research qPCR consumable; combined real-time + HRM-capable thermal cycler architectures; and the architectural pattern of standardized 96/384-well plates as universal qPCR cartridges across vendor-agnostic assay menus.

## bioMérieux GENE-UP Real-Time PCR Pathogen Detection (2014)

- **id**: `biomerieux-gene-up-pcr-pathogen`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: bioMérieux SA
- **disclosure**: bioMérieux GENE-UP product launch 2014; GENE-UP Salmonella AOAC OMA 2016.01 report; US patent 9,932,634 (multiplex pathogen PCR cartridge)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Modern bioMérieux food-safety PCR cartridge replacing legacy iQ-Check: 8-tube strip with lyophilized multiplex assay reagents, scaling to 96 reactions on the GENE-UP thermocycler. Anticipates: lyophilized strip-tube PCR cartridges with vendor-controlled assay specificity; multiplex pathogen panels packaged as drop-in strips for high-throughput food labs; and AOAC-OMA-aligned architectures spanning Salmonella, Listeria and STEC on common consumables.
