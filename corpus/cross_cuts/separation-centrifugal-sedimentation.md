## Analytical ultracentrifuge (1924)

- **id**: `svedberg-1924-analytical-ultracentrifuge`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: The Svedberg, Herman Rinde (Uppsala University)
- **disclosure**: Svedberg, T.; Rinde, H. The ultra-centrifuge, a new instrument for the determination of size and distribution of size of particle in amicroscopic colloids. J. Am. Chem. Soc. 1924, 46, 2677-2693. DOI: 10.1021/ja01677a011
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: The instrument that established centrifugal sedimentation as a quantitative tool for sizing and separating macromolecules. Anticipates the conceptual and physical basis of all centrifugal sample-preparation: density-gradient separation, sediment-and-resuspend nucleic-acid prep, and centrifugal microfluidic (lab-on-a-disc) platforms where rotation supplies the body force for valving, mixing, and plasma separation. Any device claiming novelty in using centrifugal force for size-based separation of biological particles contends with this foundational disclosure.
