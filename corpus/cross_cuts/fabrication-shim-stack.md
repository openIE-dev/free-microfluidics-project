## Slot-Die Microfluidic Coating Head for Perovskite Solar Cells (2015)

- **id**: `slot-die-perovskite-coating-head`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: CSIRO / FlexLab consortium (Hwang, Vak et al.)
- **disclosure**: Hwang K. et al. 'Toward large scale roll-to-roll production of fully printed perovskite solar cells' Adv. Mater. 27:1241-1247 (2015) doi:10.1002/adma.201404598
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a slot-die microfluidic coating head with shim-defined slot geometry depositing perovskite precursor inks onto a moving web for roll-to-roll solar cell manufacturing. Anticipates: (a) slot-die microfluidic coating as a perovskite solar cell deposition method; (b) the architectural pattern of shim-stacked microfluidic slot for sub-micron wet films; (c) meter-scale microfluidic deposition tooling for thin-film photovoltaics.
