## Smiths Detection IONSCAN Ion Mobility Spectrometer (Trace Detection) (1995)

- **id**: `smiths-detection-ionscan`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Smiths Detection (formerly Barringer Instruments)
- **disclosure**: Karpas, Anal. Chem. 1989, 61, 684-689 (academic IMS lineage); Smiths Detection IONSCAN 500DT datasheet; TSA approved IONSCAN 600 2014.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Bulk-deployed ion-mobility-spectrometer for airport explosive trace detection. Microfluidic-relevant content: heated micro-channel inlet that thermally desorbs analyte from a swab into a controlled-pressure gas stream entering the IMS drift tube. Discloses: heated swab-desorption inlet; carrier-gas microflow delivery to the ionization region; Ni-63 radioactive (or corona-discharge non-radioactive) ionization; atmospheric-pressure drift cell for sub-nanogram detection of trinitrotoluene, RDX, cocaine, heroin. Anticipates: heated swab-desorption inlets as a microfluidic interface for IMS trace detection; the architectural pattern of consumable-swab + heated-inlet + drift-cell deployed in checkpoint security.
