## Immunofluorescence (fluorescent antibody method) (1941)

- **id**: `coons-1941-immunofluorescence`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Albert Coons, Hugh Creech, Norman Jones (Harvard Medical School)
- **disclosure**: Coons, A. H.; Creech, H. J.; Jones, R. N. Immunological properties of an antibody containing a fluorescent group. Proc. Soc. Exp. Biol. Med. 1941, 47, 200-202. DOI: 10.3181/00379727-47-13084P
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: The first fluorescent-antibody conjugate. Discloses the principle that a fluorophore can be covalently attached to an immunoglobulin without destroying its specificity, enabling fluorescence as the readout of an antigen-antibody binding event. Anticipates: fluorophore-labeled detection antibodies, fluorescence-readout immunoassays, and (conceptually) flow-cytometric and microfluidic fluorescence immunodetection. Microfluidic immunoassay chips reading bound fluorescent conjugate descend from this method.
