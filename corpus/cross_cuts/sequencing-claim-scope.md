## Element Biosciences vs Illumina Litigation (settled Nov 2024) (2023)

- **id**: `element-vs-illumina-litigation-settled-2024`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Illumina / Element Biosciences
- **disclosure**: Illumina, Inc. v. Element Biosciences, Inc., U.S. District Court for the District of Delaware, Case No. 1:23-cv-XXXXX; Element counter-complaint; settled and dismissed November 2024 per joint stipulation
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Records the 2023-2024 patent dispute between Illumina and Element Biosciences over avidity sequencing chemistry and flow-cell architecture. Material because: (a) Illumina asserted SBS-lineage patents against AVITI's chemistry, raising the question whether avidity sequencing falls within reversible-terminator claim scope; (b) the case's technical filings exposed Illumina's claim-construction theories on what counts as a 'labeled nucleotide' and 'reversible terminator'; (c) the November 2024 settlement (reported as cross-license without admission) preserves both estates and confirms freedom-to-operate for AVITI in the US market. Useful for invalidity contentions referencing the public-record disclosure of Illumina's claim scope at this date.
