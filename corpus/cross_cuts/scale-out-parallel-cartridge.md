## Cytiva NanoAssemblr Spark / Benchtop / Ignite LNP Microfluidic Mixer Family (2018)

- **id**: `cytiva-nanoassemblr-spark-benchtop-ignite`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: mixer-component
- **creator**: Precision NanoSystems Inc. (acquired by Cytiva, a Danaher company, 2021)
- **disclosure**: Precision NanoSystems NanoAssemblr Benchtop product launch 2014; Spark launched 2018; Ignite launched 2020; Cytiva acquired Precision NanoSystems 2021-08-04.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: NanoAssemblr family (Spark / Benchtop / Ignite) is the dominant family of microfluidic LNP formulation devices, originating from Precision NanoSystems and now owned by Cytiva. Each device wraps a disposable staggered-herringbone-mixer cartridge with appropriate-scale pump driver. Discloses: staggered-herringbone-mixer cartridge (Stroock-style) sized for sub-mL to multi-mL throughput; programmable flow-rate-ratio (FRR) ethanol/aqueous co-flow for nucleation control; single-use cartridge eliminating cross-contamination between formulations; scale-out (vs scale-up) via parallel cartridge banks for GMP manufacturing. Anticipates: cartridge-format staggered-herringbone LNP mixers across the development scale (research -> preclinical -> GMP); the architectural choice of scale-out via parallel disposable cartridges (rather than scale-up of single mixer geometry) for GMP LNP manufacturing; FRR-controlled LNP nucleation as the standard formulation parameter.
