## Eckert & Ziegler ModularLab MX PET Tracer Synthesizer (2009)

- **id**: `eckert-ziegler-modularlab`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Eckert & Ziegler Eurotope GmbH
- **disclosure**: Eckert & Ziegler Eurotope GmbH ModularLab brochure 2009.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Modular skid-format radiosynthesizer (vs disposable cassette format). Discloses: configurable arrangement of valves, reactors, and HPLC purification on a stainless-steel skid; replaceable PFA tubing kit per synthesis providing the disposable element while the valve/actuator deck is reusable; particular suitability for Ga-68 + DOTATATE peptide labeling for neuroendocrine tumor PET. Anticipates: modular-skid radiosynthesis as the alternative to the cassette format for chemistry-development settings; the architectural distinction between the (more flexible, less GMP-validated) modular skid and the (less flexible, fully-validated) cassette.
