## BD Hypak SCF Prefilled Glass Syringe (1980s)

- **id**: `bd-hypak-scf-prefilled-syringe`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Becton, Dickinson and Company (BD Medical - Pharmaceutical Systems)
- **disclosure**: Becton Dickinson Hypak SCF launched 1980s as ready-to-fill glass syringe; BD Hypak SCF datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the dominant ready-to-fill glass prefilled syringe platform, providing the legacy baseline against which polymer alternatives (Daikyo CZ, SCHOTT TopPac) compete. Anticipates: ready-to-fill nested-tray glass prefilled syringe architectures for high-speed automated fill-finish; specifically the architectural pattern of vendor-supplied pre-sterilized syringes in nested trays that drop directly onto the fill-finish line, eliminating customer-side wash and depyrogenation steps. Despite tungsten and silicone-oil concerns, BD Hypak remains the volume leader and is referenced as the comparator in essentially every polymer-PFS regulatory submission.

## SCHOTT TopPac Polymer Prefilled Syringe (2007)

- **id**: `schott-toppac-prefilled-syringe`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: SCHOTT Pharmaceutical Systems (SCHOTT AG)
- **disclosure**: SCHOTT TopPac polymer syringe launch ~2007; product datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a COC polymer prefilled syringe addressing the tungsten-residue and break-resistance limitations of glass syringes. Anticipates: COC polymer prefilled syringe architectures for biologics where tungsten residue from glass-syringe needle staking would degrade the drug product; specifically the architectural pattern of injection-molded polymer syringe eliminating both tungsten and silicone-oil interactions. Critical for sensitive biologics in autoinjector and on-body delivery contexts. Differs from Daikyo CZ (COP) by using cyclic-olefin copolymer (COC) chemistry.

## BD Effivax Vaccine Prefilled Syringe (2014)

- **id**: `bd-effivax-vaccine-syringe`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Becton, Dickinson and Company (BD Medical - Pharmaceutical Systems)
- **disclosure**: BD Effivax product launch 2014; BD Pharmaceutical Systems datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a vaccine-optimized glass prefilled syringe (BD Effivax) with siliconization tuned for vaccine drug products and integrated passive needle-safety options. Anticipates: vaccine-optimized prefilled syringe architectures distinguishing from biologic-PFS by silicone-oil profile and integrated needle safety; specifically supports the high-volume, low-cost vaccine market where the BD Hypak base platform is adapted with vaccine-specific siliconization and safety features.
