## Eppendorf BioBLU 0.3c / 1.4f Single-Use Bioreactor (2013)

- **id**: `eppendorf-bioblu-single-use-bioreactor`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Eppendorf AG (Germany)
- **disclosure**: Eppendorf BioBLU c-series and f-series product brochure 2013-2018; Lemoine, A. et al. 'Performance loss of mammalian cells in stirred single-use bioreactors due to incomplete catalogue knowledge.' Biotechnology Journal 12, 1700015 (2017)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Eppendorf BioBLU single-use rigid-wall bioreactor family with pre-installed amperometric DO and potentiometric pH probes embedded in the molded vessel wall. Anticipates: (a) injection-molded rigid-wall single-use bioreactor architecture (vs. flexible-bag designs); (b) pre-installed welded-in DO/pH probes in single-use vessels eliminating user-side probe installation; (c) parallel single-use bioreactor architecture spanning cell (c-series) and microbial (f-series) configurations on the same controller. Cite against later patents claiming rigid-wall single-use bioreactors with pre-installed welded-in DO/pH probes.
