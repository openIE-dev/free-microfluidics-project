## Janssen Prezista Continuous Direct Compression Manufacturing Line (2016)

- **id**: `janssen-prezista-continuous-direct-compression-line`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Janssen Pharmaceuticals (Johnson & Johnson) Puerto Rico / Belgium
- **disclosure**: FDA Approval, Prezista (darunavir) Supplement: Continuous Manufacturing, NDA 021976/S-049, approved 2016-04-12; Lee, S. L. et al. 'Modernizing Pharmaceutical Manufacturing: from Batch to Continuous Production.' Journal of Pharmaceutical Innovation 10, 191-199 (2015), doi:10.1007/s12247-015-9215-8
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Janssen continuous direct-compression line approved by the FDA in 2016 for Prezista (darunavir), the first FDA approval of a switch from batch to continuous for an already-approved drug. The line architecture includes: (a) loss-in-weight feeders metering API + excipients into a continuous blender; (b) in-line NIR PAT spectrometers measuring blend uniformity at multiple points; (c) rotary tablet press (Korsch XL series) integrated with PAT-driven control loop; (d) residence-time-distribution-based diversion valve sending out-of-spec material to waste while in-spec material continues. Anticipates: (a) the regulatory and architectural template for continuous direct-compression of solid oral dosage forms; (b) RTD-gated diversion as a real-time release strategy substitute for batch sampling; (c) feed-forward NIR-driven control loops in pharmaceutical manufacturing.

## Pfizer ECO/ECOnTab Continuous Tableting Platform (2017)

- **id**: `pfizer-eco-econtab-continuous-tableting`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Pfizer Inc., Groton CT and Sandwich UK
- **disclosure**: Allison, G. et al. 'Regulatory and Quality Considerations for Continuous Manufacturing.' Journal of Pharmaceutical Sciences 104, 803-812 (2015), doi:10.1002/jps.24324; Pfizer continuous-manufacturing program, ISPE conference presentations 2017-2019
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Pfizer's ECO/ECOnTab continuous tableting platform integrating wet granulation, drying, and tableting in a sealed skid. The platform shares the GEA-Pfizer-Glatt collaboration heritage but introduces Pfizer-proprietary control software and PAT integration patterns. Anticipates: (a) two-tier scale (5 kg/h portable / 100 kg/h fixed) implementations of the same control software stack; (b) Daurismo-class continuous-manufacturing approvals using ECO; (c) integration of in-line viscosity and torque sensors as soft-sensors for real-time release. Cite against later patent claims for two-tier-scale continuous tableting platforms.

## Janssen ConTabLine Continuous Tableting Platform (2018)

- **id**: `janssen-contabline-continuous-tableting-line`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Janssen Pharmaceuticals (Johnson & Johnson)
- **disclosure**: Vanhoorne, V. et al. 'Continuous Manufacturing of Solid Oral Dosage Forms.' Drug Discovery Today: Technologies 28, 27-37 (2018), doi:10.1016/j.ddtec.2018.03.003; Janssen ConTabLine technical disclosure ISPE Pharmaceutical Engineering 38(4), 2018
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Janssen's ConTabLine modular continuous-manufacturing skid combining wet granulation, drying, milling, and tableting on a single integrated control system with multi-point PAT monitoring. Anticipates: (a) the modular skid architecture for continuous tableting (later imitated by GEA ConsiGma and L.B. Bohle Kompress); (b) multi-PAT feedforward/feedback control across each unit operation in the line; (c) the design pattern of validating a single skid template that hosts multiple drug products with formulation-specific recipe parameters. Cite against later patents claiming integrated wet-granulation-to-tableting modular skids with PAT control loops.
