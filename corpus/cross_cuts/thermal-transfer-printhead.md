## Domino V-Series Thermal Transfer Overprinter (2010)

- **id**: `domino-v-series-thermal-transfer-overprinter`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Domino Printing Sciences plc
- **disclosure**: Domino V-Series TTO datasheet 2010; Domino V230i product brochure 2014
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Counterpart to Domino's CIJ line; explicitly NOT a fluidic printhead but included in the corpus as an architectural alternative to inkjet for flexible-film overprint. Anticipates: variable-data overprinting on flex film via thermal transfer rather than inkjet, including ribbon-economy algorithms that reposition the ribbon between prints to reuse unused ribbon area. The architectural primitive disclosed is (resistive thermal head + ribbon path + servo-controlled ribbon advance algorithm) as the dominant alternative to inkjet for short-text, high-resolution variable-data on flex film. Useful prior art for hybrid printer-tooling claims.
