## ScreenCell CTC capture cartridge (2011)

- **id**: `screencell-ctc-capture-cartridge`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: ScreenCell SA
- **disclosure**: ScreenCell SA product brochure (2011); EP2329214B1; CE-IVD declarations
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use cartridge incorporating a precision-pore (8 µm) microporous polymer membrane for size-based isolation of circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from whole blood. Cartridge integrates blood dilution chamber, membrane filtration, and on-membrane staining/recovery. Anticipates: size-based CTC enrichment cartridges using precision-pore polymer membranes. Distinguishable from CellSearch (immunoaffinity-based EpCAM capture) by size-based separation and from microfluidic CTC chips (Toner / CTC-iChip) by membrane rather than channel architecture. Useful as gene-therapy QC for circulating-cell monitoring.
