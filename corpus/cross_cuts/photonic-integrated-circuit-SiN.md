# Cross-cut: `photonic-integrated-circuit-SiN`

**4 corpus entries disclose this subsystem.**

Earliest disclosure: 1991

Listed in chronological order. Each entry's `prior_art_notes` and
`disclosure_citation` constitute the citeable prior art material.

## Lukosz Grating-Coupler Optical Biosensor (PIC ancestor) (1991)

- **id**: `lukosz-1991-grating-coupler-biosensor`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Walter Lukosz (ETH Zurich)
- **disclosure**: Lukosz W., Biosensors and Bioelectronics 6(3):215-225 (1991), doi:10.1016/0956-5663(91)80006-J
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Lukosz 1991 discloses the integrated-optical grating coupler biosensor: a SiN waveguide on glass with input/output grating couplers covered by a microfluidic flow cell, where evanescent-field interaction with surface-bound analyte shifts the resonant coupling angle. Anticipates: (i) all subsequent grating-coupler PIC biosensors; (ii) the use of SiN as the bio-compatible PIC waveguide material; (iii) microfluidic flow cells over PIC biosensors as a standard architectural pattern. Academic ancestor of LioniX/Imec/MERIDIAN PIC biosensor commercial efforts.

## Erickson Cornell Nanofluidic Optofluidic Platform (2008)

- **id**: `erickson-cornell-2008-nanofluidic-optofluidic`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: David Erickson (Cornell)
- **disclosure**: Erickson D., Mandal S., Yang A. H. J., Cordovez B., Microfluid Nanofluid 4:33-52 (2008), doi:10.1007/s10404-007-0198-8; Mandal S., Erickson D., Optics Express 16:1623 (2008)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Erickson group disclosures cover the integration of photonic ring resonators and slot waveguides with sub-micron nanofluidic channels for evanescent-field trapping of nanoparticles, viruses, and biomolecules. Anticipates: (i) PIC + nanofluidic hybrid platforms for optical trapping; (ii) slot-waveguide trapping geometries integrated with microfluidic delivery; (iii) the broader concept of using PIC evanescent fields as both sensor and tweezer in a single device. Academic ancestor of LUMICKS-style optical-trapping/microfluidic combinations and PIC-biosensor + trap commercialization.

## LioniX International TriPleX PIC + Microfluidic Flow Cell (2015)

- **id**: `lionix-triplex-pic-microfluidic`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: LioniX International BV (spinout U Twente)
- **disclosure**: LioniX International TriPleX platform disclosure (2015); Heideman R. G. et al., IEEE Photonics J 4:480 (2012); product page https://www.lionix-international.com/photonics/
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: LioniX TriPleX platform discloses a multi-layer SiN/SiO2 photonic IC stack with low propagation loss across visible and NIR, integrated with microfluidic flow cells for asymmetric Mach-Zehnder interferometer biosensing. Anticipates: (i) the use of TriPleX-style multi-layer SiN waveguide stacks as biosensor substrates; (ii) wafer-scale bonding of polymer or glass microfluidic caps over PIC sensor arrays; (iii) Mach-Zehnder phase-shift detection of evanescent-field binding events. Sister disclosure to imec-photonic-biosensor-lab-on-chip.

## Imec Silicon Nitride Photonic Biosensor Platform (2018)

- **id**: `imec-photonic-biosensor-lab-on-chip`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Imec (Interuniversity Microelectronics Centre, Leuven)
- **disclosure**: Imec photonic IC biosensor platform announcement 2018; Souza M. C. M. M. et al., Sensors and Actuators B 297:126770 (2019); Imec annual report 2020
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Imec discloses a silicon-nitride photonic-integrated-circuit (PIC) biosensor platform integrating arrays of ring resonators or Mach-Zehnder interferometers as evanescent-field affinity sensors, bonded to a microfluidic flow cell that delivers analyte to functionalized waveguide surfaces. Anticipates: (i) CMOS-compatible mass-manufactured PIC biosensor + microfluidic combinations; (ii) multiplexed ring-resonator arrays addressed by tunable laser sweep; (iii) integration of photonic biosensors with microfluidic sample loops for clinical workflows. Sister to LioniX/MERIDIAN approaches but with high-volume CMOS fab integration.
