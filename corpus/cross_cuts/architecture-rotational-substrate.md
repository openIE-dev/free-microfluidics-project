## Kodak Disc Film Format with Microfluidic Process Cartridge (1982)

- **id**: `kodak-disc-film-1982`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Eastman Kodak Company
- **disclosure**: Eastman Kodak Company press release 'Kodak Disc film system' 1982-02-03; Kodak Publication Z-131 supplement on Disc film
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a 15-frame rotating-disc film format whose minilab processing required specialized small-format carrier and chemistry transport, including custom developer-bleach-fix transport adapted to the disc geometry. Anticipates: (a) non-strip rotational substrate carrying photographic chemistry as a precursor to centrifugal microfluidic concepts; (b) cartridge-housed small-format substrate chemistry. Borderline microfluidic but historically relevant.
