## Nano Dimension DragonFly LDM 3D Circuit Printer (2017)

- **id**: `nano-dimension-dragonfly-ldm-3d-circuit-printer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Nano Dimension Ltd.
- **disclosure**: Nano Dimension DragonFly Pro launch 2017; DragonFly LDM (Lights-out Digital Manufacturing) launch 2019; DragonFly IV launch 2021
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an additive PCB-printing architecture combining two distinct piezo printheads in a single machine: one jets nano-silver conductive ink, the other jets photopolymer dielectric ink. They alternate layer by layer with cure stages between (UV for dielectric, thermal sintering pulse for conductor). The result is a multilayer PCB printed in one build, with arbitrary 3D conductor geometries and buried passive components - eliminating the entire subtractive PCB manufacturing chain (drilling, plating, etching, lamination). Anticipates: any inkjet-based additive PCB manufacturing system using alternating conductive + dielectric ink deposition with intermediate cure/sinter stages. The architectural primitive is the (two-head, two-ink, alternating-deposit-and-cure) sequence as a complete PCB-fabrication replacement. Defends against most additive electronics manufacturing claims for printed multilayer circuits.
