## Iota Biosciences Ultrasonic Neural Dust Implant (2016-08-03)

- **id**: `iota-biosciences-neural-dust`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Iota Biosciences (UC Berkeley spinout; Carmena/Maharbiz labs)
- **disclosure**: Seo et al., Neuron 2016, 91, 529-539. DOI:10.1016/j.neuron.2016.06.034; Piech et al., Nat. Biomed. Eng. 2020, 4, 207-222.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Sub-mm wireless neural implant powered and read out via external ultrasonic transducer. Discloses: PZT crystal that backscatter-encodes recorded neural signal; silicon front-end with parylene-C encapsulation forming the tissue/electronics microfluidic boundary; implantable form factor small enough for arbitrary peripheral-nerve placement. Anticipates: untethered, sub-mm neural implants powered by ultrasound rather than RF; backscatter-encoded data transmission for ultra-low-power BMIs; parylene encapsulation as the chronic-implant boundary at sub-mm scale (a more aggressive ratio of seal length / volume than Utah array). The fluidic-relevant element is again parylene as the saline barrier.
