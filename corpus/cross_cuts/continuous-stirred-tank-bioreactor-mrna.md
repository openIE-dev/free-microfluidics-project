## Quantoom Biosciences Ntensify continuous IVT module (2022)

- **id**: `quantoom-ntensify-ivt-module`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Quantoom Biosciences (spun out of Univercells)
- **disclosure**: Quantoom Biosciences (Univercells spinout) Ntensify continuous IVT presentation, ASGCT 2022; https://www.quantoom.com/ntensify; WO2023194543A1.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a continuous (rather than batch) in-vitro-transcription reactor module: a controlled-feed reactor maintains NTP, plasmid template, T7 polymerase, and buffer at steady-state concentrations to produce mRNA at constant productivity, with continuous product withdrawal feeding downstream capping/tailing/purification/LNP modules. The IVT module is deliberately specified separately from the LNP encapsulator for plug-and-play CGT manufacturing trains. Anticipates: continuous IVT reactor architectures as a class; specifically the architectural pattern of decoupling IVT, capping/tailing, polishing, and LNP modules as discrete continuous-flow units in an integrated mRNA manufacturing skid; foundational prior art for end-to-end continuous mRNA manufacturing claims.
