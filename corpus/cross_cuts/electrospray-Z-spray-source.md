## Waters Xevo TQ Triple Quadrupole Mass Spectrometer (2008)

- **id**: `waters-xevo-tq-ms`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Waters Corporation
- **disclosure**: Waters Corporation Xevo TQ-S brochure 720003200en; ASMS 2008 introduction; Xevo TQ-S launch 2010.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Triple quadrupole MS optimized for regulated bioanalysis (FDA-validated PK assays). Discloses: Z-spray ESI source providing orthogonal ion sampling that rejects neutrals (a fluidic-engineering choice that gates source contamination); StepWave ion guide using off-axis ion transfer to further reject neutral particulates between source and analyzer; precision-machined ceramic quadrupole rods. Anticipates: dual-orthogonal off-axis ion source designs that maintain sensitivity over weeks of dirty-matrix injection; the architectural pattern of source robustness vs raw sensitivity for regulated PK bioanalysis.
