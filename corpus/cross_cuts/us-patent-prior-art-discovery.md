## USPTO Patent Public Search (PPUBS) Tool (2022-02-01)

- **id**: `uspto-ppubs-patent-public-search-tool`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: United States Patent and Trademark Office
- **disclosure**: USPTO Patent Public Search public launch 2022-02-01 replacing legacy PatFT and AppFT; URL: ppubs.uspto.gov
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: PPUBS is the USPTO's modern free public search interface replacing PatFT/AppFT. It exposes the same Boolean operators, classification (CPC) browsing, and image PDF retrieval that USPTO examiners use internally. Material to defensive publication because: (a) it is the canonical primary source for US patent text after Feb 2022; (b) its classification-tree navigation supports 'CPC peer review' methodology required for thorough prior-art searches; (c) it provides reverse-citation data critical for tracing patent family chains. Documenting its existence in this commons supports its inclusion as a recommended step in commons-grade prior-art investigations.
