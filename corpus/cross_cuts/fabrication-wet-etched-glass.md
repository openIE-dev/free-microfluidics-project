## Jacobson & Ramsey Electroosmotic Pumping Foundation (1994-1996) (1994)

- **id**: `ehd-electroosmotic-flow-foundation-jacobson`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: J. Michael Ramsey group (ORNL / UNC Chapel Hill)
- **disclosure**: Jacobson S. C., Hergenroder R., Koutny L. B., Ramsey J. M., Analytical Chemistry 66:1107 (1994), doi:10.1021/ac00079a028; Ramsey J. M. et al., Anal. Chem. 67:2059 (1995); Ramsey J. M., Nature Biotechnology 17:1061 (1999)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: The Ramsey group's 1994-1999 publications and patents disclose the productizable foundation of electroosmotic flow (EOF) pumping in glass microfluidic chips, including T-injector and double-T sample injection geometries, EOF-driven serial dilution, and electrokinetic mixing. Anticipates: (i) all subsequent EOF-driven on-chip electrophoretic platforms (Caliper LabChip, Agilent Bioanalyzer); (ii) T-injector and gated-injection sample-handling primitives; (iii) the foundational architecture of voltage-multiplexed electrokinetic chips. Direct ancestor of caliper-acla-chip-1999 and perkinelmer-labchip-gxii-ez-reader.

## Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer chip-based capillary electrophoresis (1999)

- **id**: `agilent-bioanalyzer-2100`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Agilent Technologies (chip technology by Caliper Technologies)
- **disclosure**: Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer launch 1999 (joint with Caliper Technologies); https://www.agilent.com/en/product/automated-electrophoresis/bioanalyzer-systems; US6042710A; US5942443A.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a chip-based microfluidic capillary gel electrophoresis platform: a disposable glass microfluidic chip carries 16 sample wells, a separation channel, and electrode contacts; the chip mounts in a benchtop instrument that performs sample loading, voltage application, and laser-induced fluorescence detection; software computes the RNA Integrity Number (RIN) from electropherogram peaks. Foundational prior art for: lab-on-chip nucleic-acid QC; the RIN algorithm as a quantitative mRNA quality metric (later required for IVT mRNA drug-substance release testing); chip-based pharma-grade nucleic-acid analytical instruments; the architecture of disposable glass-chip electrophoresis cartridges as a class. Anticipates essentially every claim broadly directed to chip-based mRNA quality scoring or microfluidic capillary electrophoresis QC.

## PerkinElmer LabChip GXII Touch / EZ Reader (2002)

- **id**: `perkinelmer-labchip-gxii-ez-reader`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Caliper Life Sciences (acquired by PerkinElmer 2011)
- **disclosure**: Caliper Life Sciences LabChip product launches (2002 onward); PerkinElmer LabChip GXII Touch product brochure 2014; US patent 6,808,609; Caliper acla-chip-1999 patent foundation
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: LabChip family discloses glass microfluidic chips with sipper-based plate sampling, electrokinetic injection and electrophoretic separation of DNA, RNA, proteins, and small molecules. Mobility-shift EZ Reader configurations couple this with substrate/product separation for kinase-activity quantification. Anticipates: (i) sipper-interfaced plate-fed lab-on-chip electrophoresis platforms; (ii) EOF-driven mobility-shift kinase assays as a productized lab-on-chip workflow; (iii) glass-chip cartridges with integrated sample interface for high-throughput screening. Builds on caliper-acla-chip-1999 (existing entry); this entry covers the productized GXII/EZ Reader generation.
