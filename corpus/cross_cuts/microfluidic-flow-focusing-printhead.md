## Aspect Biosystems RX1 Microfluidic Printhead (head specifically) (2015)

- **id**: `aspect-biosystems-rx1-microfluidic-printhead`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Aspect Biosystems Ltd.
- **disclosure**: Aspect Biosystems RX1 launch 2015 (Vancouver, BC); Aspect Lab-on-a-Printer technology whitepaper; foundational US patent 9,857,784
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Extension to wave 1 aspect-biosystems-rx1 entry, focusing on the printhead specifically. Discloses a microfluidic flow-focusing bioprinter printhead - the printhead itself is a microfluidic chip with multiple bioink inlet channels, a crosslinker inlet channel, and a coaxial flow-focusing junction at the extrusion orifice. Anticipates: any bioprinter printhead that integrates multi-channel bioink input with coaxial crosslinker introduction at the orifice, producing already-crosslinked fiber as it deposits. The architectural primitive is (microfluidic chip-as-printhead + multi-bioink switching + coaxial crosslinker convergence + on-the-fly fiber crosslinking at the orifice). This is fundamentally different from extrusion-from-cartridge bioprinting, where crosslinking happens after deposition. Critical prior art for any microfluidic-printhead bioprinting claim.
