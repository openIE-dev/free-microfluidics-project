## Berkeley Lights Beacon (PhenomeX/Bruker) (2016-09)

- **id**: `berkeley-lights-beacon-optofluidic`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Berkeley Lights Inc. (later PhenomeX, acquired by Bruker 2023)
- **disclosure**: Berkeley Lights Inc. Beacon launch press release Sep 2016; Le K. et al. SLAS Discovery 23(1):44-55 (2018), doi:10.1177/2472555217735282; OptoSelect chip US patent 9,403,172
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Berkeley Lights Beacon discloses an optoelectronic positioning (OEP) chip wherein a virtual electrode pattern is generated by projecting light onto a photoconductive layer (a-Si:H) integrated into a microfluidic chamber; the resulting localized DEP forces translate single cells into NanoPen sequestration pens for clonal expansion and serial assay. Anticipates: (i) OEP/light-induced DEP single-cell platforms; (ii) the architectural pattern of thousands of micro-pen sequestration chambers addressed by light-defined virtual electrodes; (iii) coupling of OEP with on-chip imaging and downstream nanoliter export for sequencing/cell-line workflows. Builds on Chiou/Wu 2005 OEP foundation, but adds productized single-use OptoSelect chip and Beacon instrument workflow.
