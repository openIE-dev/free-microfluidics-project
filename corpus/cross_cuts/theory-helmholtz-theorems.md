## Helmholtz 1858 — On integrals of the hydrodynamic equations corresponding to vortex motion (1858)

- **id**: `helmholtz-1858-vortex-motion`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Hermann von Helmholtz
- **disclosure**: H. Helmholtz, 'Über Integrale der hydrodynamischen Gleichungen, welche den Wirbelbewegungen entsprechen', J. Reine Angew. Math. (Crelle) 55, 25-55 (1858)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Foundational paper on vortex dynamics; establishes Helmholtz's vortex theorems. For microfluidics, foundational to: chaotic-advection mixers, secondary-flow effects in curved channels (Dean vortices), inertial microfluidics (Di Carlo), and any device exploiting vorticity for particle focusing or mixing. Anticipates: any patent claim that recites controlled vortex generation as its operating principle for mixing or focusing in micro-channels — the underlying vortex theorems are 168 years old.
