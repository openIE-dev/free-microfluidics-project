## Daikyo Crystal Zenith (CZ) Cyclic-Olefin Polymer Vials (1991)

- **id**: `daikyo-crystal-zenith-vials`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Daikyo Seiko Ltd. (West Pharmaceutical Services partnership)
- **disclosure**: Daikyo Seiko CZ vial launch 1991; West Pharmaceutical Daikyo CZ partnership 1995-onwards; West Daikyo CZ datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses cyclic-olefin polymer (COP) primary containers (vials, prefilled syringes) as a glass-alternative for biopharma fill-finish, addressing glass delamination and silicate-extractable risks for sensitive biologics. Anticipates: COP-based primary container architectures for fill-finish of sensitive biologics including mRNA-LNP; specifically the architectural pattern of injection-molded polymer container replacing borosilicate glass to eliminate delamination flakes and silicate ions that can degrade mRNA, monoclonal antibodies, and other sensitive biologics. Critical for mRNA-LNP fill-finish where glass-induced lipid degradation has been documented.
