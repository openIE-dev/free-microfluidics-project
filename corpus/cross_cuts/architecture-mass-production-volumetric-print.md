## DISH: digital incoherent synthesis of holographic light fields (2026-02-11)

- **id**: `wang-2026-dish-volumetric-3d-printing`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Dai / Wu / Fang group, Tsinghua
- **disclosure**: Wang, X.; Ma, Y.; Niu, Y.; Xiong, B.; Zhang, A.; Zhang, G.; Chen, Y.; Wei, W.; Fang, L.; Wu, J.; Dai, Q. Sub-second volumetric 3D printing by synthesis of holographic light fields. Nature 2026. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10114-5
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Discloses sub-second volumetric 3D printing by holographically synthesized multi-angle DMD projections through a high-speed rotating periscope (10 rotations/s) instead of rotating the sample. Anticipates: rotating-periscope-rather-than-rotating-sample architecture for tomographic volumetric printing, holographic optimization of binary DMD patterns against a wave-optics propagation model with refraction at the air-resin interface, adaptive-optics calibration via two orthogonal cameras as wavefront sensors, integration of volumetric printing with a flow channel for continuous mass production of arbitrary 3D microparticles in low-viscosity resins (4.7 cP), and the use of incoherent synthesis of binary holograms to generate high-contrast 3D dose distributions across depth ranges 25× the objective's depth of field.
