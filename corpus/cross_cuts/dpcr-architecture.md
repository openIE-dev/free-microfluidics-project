## QIAGEN Acquisition of Stilla Technologies (announced 2024) (2024)

- **id**: `qiagen-stilla-acquisition-2024`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: QIAGEN N.V.
- **disclosure**: QIAGEN N.V. press release 2024 announcing strategic agreement to acquire Stilla Technologies; SEC 6-K filings 2024-2025
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Records the corporate combination through which QIAGEN absorbed Stilla's Naica Crystal Digital PCR patent estate (chamber-free 2D droplet partitioning, parallel-partition Sapphire/Ruby/Opal chip masks). Following close, the combined party owns or controls dPCR architectural patents in three otherwise-distinct technical lineages: (i) chambered Bio-Rad/RainDance droplet dPCR (via cross-licenses), (ii) crystal/2D-monolayer Stilla dPCR, and (iii) QIAcuity nanoplate dPCR. Useful as defensive-publication evidence of (a) the prior public availability of Stilla's chip architectures and (b) the consolidation that may justify FRAND or compulsory cross-licensing arguments in future dPCR antitrust analyses.
