## Open COVID Pledge Patent Commons (2020) (2020-04-07)

- **id**: `open-covid-pledge-patent-commons-2020`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Open COVID Pledge Coalition (Creative Commons + Mark Lemley + Jorge Contreras et al.)
- **disclosure**: Open COVID Pledge launched 2020-04-07 at opencovidpledge.org; founding signatories include Amazon, Facebook, HP, IBM, Intel, Microsoft, Sandia National Laboratories, Mozilla
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: The Open COVID Pledge committed signatories to a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to all of their patents and copyrights for the purpose of diagnosing, preventing, and treating COVID-19, for the duration of the WHO-declared pandemic plus one year. Material to defensive publication because: (a) the OCL license text is itself a primary source establishing a public-domain-equivalent zone for COVID-related diagnostic and microfluidic technology during 2020-2024; (b) signatory patent portfolios (Amazon, Microsoft, IBM, Intel, HP, Sandia) included substantial sensor, microfluidic, and bioinformatics patents that became royalty-free for COVID use; (c) the pledge's expiration triggers (WHO declaration end + 1 year) define a clear sunset, after which residual royalty-free use rights persist for any technology actually deployed under the pledge. Useful as a 'commons safe-harbor' precedent for future-pandemic patent-pool design.
