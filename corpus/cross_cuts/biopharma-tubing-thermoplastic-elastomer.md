## Saint-Gobain Performance Plastics C-Flex / PharMed BPT Tubing (1980s)

- **id**: `saint-gobain-cflex-pharmed-tubing`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Saint-Gobain Performance Plastics
- **disclosure**: Saint-Gobain C-Flex tubing patent US4,100,237A 1978; PharMed BPT launched 1990s; Saint-Gobain Performance Plastics datasheets rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses thermoplastic elastomer tubing (C-Flex 374, PharMed BPT) with weldability, low extractables, and gamma-sterilization compatibility, used as the standard fluid-path material for single-use biopharma manufacturing. Anticipates: thermoplastic-elastomer single-use tubing for biopharma where the same material supports peristaltic pumping (PharMed BPT for >1000 hour life) and sterile welding (C-Flex). Critical input to the entire single-use biopharma ecosystem; nearly every Cytiva, Sartorius, Pall, and Repligen single-use product uses Saint-Gobain tubing as the fluid path.
