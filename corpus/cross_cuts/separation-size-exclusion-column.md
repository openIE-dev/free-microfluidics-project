## DiaMed ID-System gel card (column agglutination technology) (1988)

- **id**: `diamed-id-system-gel-card`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: DiaMed AG (now Bio-Rad Laboratories)
- **disclosure**: Lapierre Y. et al., The gel test: a new way to detect red cell antigen-antibody reactions, Transfusion 30:109-113 (1990); DiaMed AG ID-System introduced 1988; foundational patent Lapierre EP0305337 / US5338689.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a microtube card containing a packed dextran-gel (or glass-bead) column through which red cells are centrifuged so that agglutinates are size-trapped at the column top and free cells pellet below - a centrifugal microfluidic size-separation immunoassay. Anticipates claims to (a) gel-column / glass-bead column agglutination cards for blood typing and antibody detection; (b) centrifugal red-cell separation as an agglutination endpoint; (c) automated reading of column reaction patterns. Foundational prior art for ortho-vision-gel-card, bio-rad-ih-1000, grifols-erytra-dg-gel.

## Bio-Rad IH-1000 / IH-500 immunohematology analyzer (2011)

- **id**: `bio-rad-ih-1000-immunohematology`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Bio-Rad Laboratories (DiaMed line)
- **disclosure**: Bio-Rad IH-1000 instrument operating instructions; CE mark ~2011, FDA clearance later; Bio-Rad acquired DiaMed 2007-2008 and automated the ID-System.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a walk-away analyzer automating dextran-gel column-agglutination cards by robotic reagent addition, incubation, dedicated card centrifugation, and camera-based grading. Anticipates claims to automated high-throughput gel-card immunohematology and to centrifugal-microfluidic gel-column red-cell separation under robotic control. Descends from diamed-id-system-gel-card.

## Grifols Erytra / Erytra Eflexis DG Gel immunohematology analyzer (2011)

- **id**: `grifols-erytra-dg-gel-analyzer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Grifols (Diagnostic Grifols S.A.)
- **disclosure**: Grifols Erytra Analyzer user manual; CE mark ~2011; Grifols Diagnostic Grifols (DG Gel) column-agglutination system.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an automated analyzer running an eight-column gel-matrix card with robotic reagent addition, incubation, integrated card centrifugation, and image-based grading. - **device class**: lab-on-chip
