## Mark, Haeberle, Roth, von Stetten & Zengerle 2010 — Microfluidic lab-on-a-chip platforms (2010-03)

- **id**: `mark-2010-microfluidic-platforms-review`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Daniel Mark, Stefan Haeberle, Günter Roth, Felix von Stetten, Roland Zengerle
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1039/B820557B; Chem. Soc. Rev. 39, 1153-1182 (2010)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Critical Chem Soc Rev cataloguing the seven dominant microfluidic platforms by 2010: capillary, pressure-driven, centrifugal, electrokinetic, acoustic, free-flow, and droplet/digital. For each it lists fabrication, fluidic operations achievable, and example applications — i.e., a public engineering manual. Anticipates: any post-2010 patent that claims a 'microfluidic platform' architecturally indistinguishable from one of these seven (e.g., 'a centrifugal disc with siphon valves and detection chamber') as obvious in view of the Mark 2010 enumeration. Particularly load-bearing for centrifugal and droplet platform genus claims.
