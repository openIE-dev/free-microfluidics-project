- **creator**: TSI Incorporated
- **disclosure**: TSI Incorporated product datasheet 'Aerodynamic Particle Sizer Spectrometer Model 3321' (2002)

- **prior art notes**: Discloses an aerosol spectrometer with an accelerating-nozzle microcapillary that imparts size-dependent velocity to particles, measured by a dual-laser time-of-flight detection volume.
