## ChargePoint AseptiSafe passive aseptic transfer valve (2007)

- **id**: `chargepoint-aseptisafe-rtp`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: ChargePoint Technology Ltd
- **disclosure**: ChargePoint Technology AseptiSafe valve launch (2007); https://www.thechargepoint.com/our-products/asepti-safe-bio/; GB2452778B; US8403006B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a passive split-butterfly aseptic transfer valve, a competitor architecture to the La Calhène DPTE for closed-system pharma material transfer between isolators and process vessels: two half-discs (alpha on isolator, beta on container) couple via bayonet and rotate together as a single seal, exposing only previously-paired surfaces. AseptiSafe bio is qualified for sterile aseptic CGT and biologic transfers. Anticipates: split-butterfly aseptic transfer valve architectures sharing the alpha-beta double-door pattern but differentiating via butterfly-disc geometry rather than DPTE bayonet-port geometry; specifically the architecture of a passive bayonet-engaged butterfly valve as the closed-system transfer interface for high-containment OEB5 powder and biologic-drug-substance handling.

## ChargePoint ChargeBag PE single-use bag transfer (2014)

- **id**: `chargepoint-chargebag-pe`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: ChargePoint Technology Ltd
- **disclosure**: ChargePoint ChargeBag PE product launch (2014); https://www.thechargepoint.com/our-products/chargebag-pe/; GB2519531B.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use disposable polyethylene bag pre-fitted with the AseptiSafe split-butterfly beta-port, gamma-irradiated as a sterile assembly, designed to dock onto a stainless-steel alpha port on an isolator wall for closed-system aseptic powder, slurry, or solution transfer. Anticipates: pre-fitted single-use bag-and-port assemblies for alpha-beta aseptic transfer in CGT and biologic processing; specifically the architectural pattern of integrating split-butterfly aseptic-transfer geometry with disposable single-use bags to eliminate the cleaning and sterilization burden of stainless-steel reusable transfer canisters.
