## Guardant Health Guardant360 CDx (cell-free DNA NGS liquid biopsy) (2014)

- **id**: `guardant360-cdx-cfdna-ngs-panel`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Guardant Health, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Lanman R.B. et al., Analytical and clinical validation of a digital sequencing panel for quantitative highly accurate evaluation of cell-free circulating tumor DNA, PLoS ONE 10:e0140712 (2015); Guardant360 launched 2014; FDA approval of Guardant360 CDx 2020 (P200010).
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses error-suppressed cfDNA NGS using molecular-barcode tagging, hybrid-capture enrichment, and clonal amplification on patterned-nanowell sequencing flow cells for tumor genotyping from plasma. Anticipates claims to barcode-corrected ultrasensitive cfDNA variant detection, comprehensive plasma genomic profiling panels, and companion-diagnostic use of liquid-biopsy NGS. Related to foundationone-liquid-cdx-cfdna and tempus-xf-liquid-biopsy; the flow-cell architecture descends from Illumina patterned-flow-cell prior art.

## Foundation Medicine FoundationOne Liquid CDx (cfDNA NGS companion diagnostic) (2016)

- **id**: `foundationone-liquid-cdx-cfdna`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Foundation Medicine, Inc. (Roche)
- **disclosure**: Clark T.A. et al., Analytical validation of a hybrid capture-based next-generation sequencing clinical assay for genomic profiling of cell-free circulating tumor DNA, J Mol Diagn 20:686-702 (2018) (FoundationACT); FoundationOne Liquid CDx FDA approval 2020 (P190032).
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses hybrid-capture cfDNA NGS over a large gene panel with clonal amplification on patterned-nanowell flow cells, reporting comprehensive genomic alterations plus MSI and blood TMB for companion-diagnostic use. Anticipates claims to large-panel cfDNA hybrid-capture liquid biopsy and to plasma-based companion diagnostics. Related to guardant360-cdx-cfdna-ngs-panel and foundationone-cdx-tissue-ngs.

## Foundation Medicine FoundationOne CDx (tissue comprehensive genomic profiling) (2017-11)

- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Foundation Medicine, Inc. (Roche)
- **disclosure**: FoundationOne CDx FDA approval 2017-11 (P170019); Frampton G.M. et al., Development and validation of a clinical cancer genomic profiling test based on massively parallel DNA sequencing, Nat Biotechnol 31:1023-1031 (2013) (FoundationOne predecessor).
