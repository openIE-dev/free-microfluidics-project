## Fluid Inclusion Petroleum Microfluidic Chip (FIPS) (2013)

- **id**: `fluid-inclusion-petroleum-fips-chip`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: CSIRO and Université de Lorraine consortium
- **disclosure**: Bourdet J. et al. 'Microfluidic chip for analyzing petroleum fluid inclusions: design and validation' Lab on a Chip 13:4456 (2013) doi:10.1039/c3lc50819b
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a fused-silica microfluidic chip designed to mimic natural petroleum fluid inclusions, allowing controlled migration of crude oil + water + gas at reservoir P-T and in-situ Raman characterization. Anticipates: (a) microfluidic analog of natural petroleum fluid inclusions; (b) high-PT fused-silica chips for petroleum geochemistry research; (c) coupling of microfluidic geochemistry to in-situ Raman. Does not disclose long-term inclusion sealing.
