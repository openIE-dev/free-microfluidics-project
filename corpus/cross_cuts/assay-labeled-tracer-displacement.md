## Radioimmunoassay (RIA) (1960)

- **id**: `yalow-berson-1960-radioimmunoassay`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Rosalyn Yalow, Solomon Berson (Veterans Administration Hospital, Bronx)
- **disclosure**: Yalow, R. S.; Berson, S. A. Immunoassay of endogenous plasma insulin in man. J. Clin. Invest. 1960, 39, 1157-1175. DOI: 10.1172/JCI104130
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: The foundational competitive-binding immunoassay. Discloses the core principle on which every downstream microfluidic immunoassay cartridge depends: a labeled tracer competing with analyte for a limited quantity of specific antibody, with bound/free separation read out as signal. Anticipates generic claims to competitive immunoassay architecture, labeled-tracer displacement readout, and standard-curve quantification of analyte concentration. The label has since migrated from radioisotope to enzyme (ELISA), fluorophore, and electrochemiluminescent tag, but the assay topology is unchanged. Any microfluidic device claiming novelty over competitive immunoassay as a category contends with this 1960 disclosure.
