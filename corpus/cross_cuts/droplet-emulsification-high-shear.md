# Cross-cut: `droplet-emulsification-high-shear`

**7 corpus entries disclose this subsystem.**

## Microfluidics Corporation Microfluidizer high-pressure homogenizer (1985-08-06)

- **id**: `microfluidics-corp-microfluidizer-high-shear-processor`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: mixer-component
- **creator**: Microfluidics Corporation (Microfluidics International / IDEX MPT)
- **disclosure**: US4533254A Apparatus for forming emulsions (Cook & Lagace, Biotechnology Development Corporation), granted 1985-08-06.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a fixed-geometry microchannel interaction chamber that divides a high-pressure feed into colliding microjets to homogenize and emulsify - the canonical industrial high-shear microfluidic processor. Anticipates broad claims to (a) sub-100 nm oil-in-water vaccine-adjuvant emulsion produced by repeated passes through a fixed-geometry microchannel chamber; (b) constant-shear scale-up by replicating chamber geometry; (c) liposome/nanoparticle size reduction by impinging-jet microfluidization. Direct prior art for any patent claiming squalene adjuvant manufacture (MF59, AS03 analogs) by microfluidization and for impinging-jet liposomal-nanoparticle process claims.

## SEPPIC Montanide ISA water-in-oil emulsion adjuvant (1993)

- **id**: `seppic-montanide-isa-water-in-oil-adjuvant`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: droplet-generator
- **creator**: SEPPIC (Air Liquide Healthcare)
- **disclosure**: Aucouturier J. et al., Montanide ISA 720 and 51: a new generation of water in oil emulsions as adjuvants for human vaccines, Expert Rev Vaccines (2002); SEPPIC Montanide ISA technical brochures, Air Liquide / SEPPIC, from ~1993.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses water-in-oil and water-in-oil-in-water vaccine emulsion adjuvants based on mannide monooleate surfactant in mineral oil or squalane, formed by emulsification (syringe extrusion, microfluidization, rotor-stator). Anticipates claims to mannide-monooleate W/O and W/O/W emulsion adjuvants of defined aqueous:oil ratio and to therapeutic cancer-vaccine, malaria-vaccine and veterinary-vaccine formulations using them. The syringe-extrusion / microfluidization emulsification step is anticipated by impinging-jet and extrusion microfluidic prior art.

## Seqirus MF59 squalene oil-in-water adjuvant (1995)

- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: droplet-generator
- **creator**: CSL Seqirus (formerly Chiron / Novartis Vaccines)
- **disclosure**: Ott G. et al., MF59 - Design and evaluation of a safe and potent adjuvant for human vaccines, in Powell & Newman (eds.), Vaccine Design: The Subunit and Adjuvant Approach, Plenum Press (1995), pp. 277-296.

- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: GSK (GlaxoSmithKline Biologicals)
