## Thermo Fisher KingFisher Magnetic Particle Processor (1996)

- **id**: `thermofisher-kingfisher-magnetic-bead-handler`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Thermo Fisher Scientific (originally Labsystems Oy, Finland)
- **disclosure**: Original Labsystems KingFisher launch 1996; ThermoFisher KingFisher Flex user manual N07669; US patent 6,448,092
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: KingFisher discloses a magnetic-tip-comb-based bead handler in which a comb of tips lined with internal magnets dips into successive wells of a deepwell plate, capturing beads on the outside of the tips and transferring them through wash and elution buffers without aspirating liquid. Anticipates: (i) the tip-comb magnet-transfer architecture, distinct from rod-in-cartridge designs (Maxwell RSC) and from open-magnet-array designs; (ii) high-throughput 96-well bead-handling for sequencing library prep, magnetic-bead immunoassays, and viral RNA extraction; (iii) the disposable-tip-comb consumable as a contamination-isolation primitive. Predecessor to MagMAX Express; foundation for many high-throughput COVID-19 extraction workflows.

## Thermo Fisher MagMAX Magnetic Bead Extraction System (2008)

- **id**: `thermofisher-magmax-extraction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Thermo Fisher Scientific (Applied Biosystems)
- **disclosure**: Applied Biosystems MagMAX product launch (2008); MagMAX Viral/Pathogen Nucleic Acid Isolation Kit user guide MAN0018073; US patent 8,062,847
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: MagMAX discloses a silica-coated paramagnetic bead chemistry and protocol set for nucleic acid isolation, productized as kit reagents compatible with multiple automated extractors. Anticipates: (i) silica-magnetic-bead extraction chemistry productization; (ii) protocol multiplexing across KingFisher, MagMAX Express, and manual workflows; (iii) the use of magnetic-bead nucleic acid extraction at pandemic-scale clinical throughput (2020 COVID-19 ramp). Builds on boom-1990-silica-magnetic-extraction foundation.

## Promega Maxwell RSC Magnetic Bead Nucleic Acid Extractor (2014)

- **id**: `promega-maxwell-rsc-magnetic-extraction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Promega Corporation
- **disclosure**: Promega Maxwell RSC launch 2014; Maxwell RSC technical manual TM411; US patent 7,754,148
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Maxwell RSC discloses a magnetic-rod-based nucleic acid extractor in which sealed pre-filled cartridges contain reagent wells; a moving magnet array transfers paramagnetic beads (with bound nucleic acids) sequentially through wash and elution wells. Anticipates: (i) magnetic-rod-driven bead-transfer extractors with sealed cartridges; (ii) pre-filled disposable extraction cartridges with reagent traceability; (iii) clinical-grade walk-away nucleic acid prep at 48-sample throughput. Adjacent to KingFisher (entry: distinct competitor) but with sealed pre-filled cartridges rather than open plates.

## Quanterix Simoa HD-X Platform (2017)

- **id**: `quanterix-simoa-hd-x-platform`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Quanterix Corporation
- **disclosure**: Quanterix Corp Simoa HD-X launch 2017; Rissin D. M. et al. Nature Biotechnology 28:595 (2010), doi:10.1038/nbt.1641; US patents 8,222,047 and 8,236,574
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Quanterix Simoa HD-X discloses a productized single-molecule array system in which paramagnetic beads coated with capture antibodies bind <1 target molecule each, are loaded into femtoliter microwells (etched in a fiber-optic bundle), sealed by an immiscible oil overlay, and read out optically as digital on/off well counts. Anticipates: (i) digital ELISA microwell-array platforms generally; (ii) femtoliter microwell formation by acid etching of fiber-optic bundles; (iii) oil-sealed microreactor arrays for single-molecule fluorogenic assays; (iv) the HD-X automated cartridge workflow. Builds on rissin-2010-quanterix-simoa academic ancestor entry but adds productized HD-X cartridge and instrument architecture.
