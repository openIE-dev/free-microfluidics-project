## Blackrock Microsystems Utah Intracortical Electrode Array (1991)

- **id**: `blackrock-utah-array`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: University of Utah / Bionic Technologies / Blackrock Microsystems / Blackrock Neurotech
- **disclosure**: Campbell et al., IEEE Trans. Biomed. Eng. 1991, 38, 758-768. DOI:10.1109/10.83588; Maynard et al., Electroencephalogr. Clin. Neurophysiol. 1997, 102, 228-239.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: The dominant chronic intracortical multielectrode array since the 1990s; foundational to the BrainGate clinical trials. Discloses: deep-reactive-ion-etched silicon needles in 10x10 grid with platinum-deposited tips; parylene-C conformal coating providing the saline/electronics boundary at the cortical insertion site; flexible cable bundle to skull-mounted percutaneous pedestal. Microfluidic-relevant elements: parylene encapsulation as a chronic saline barrier; the percutaneous pedestal as a fluidic seal between scalp and electronics. Anticipates: silicon multielectrode arrays for chronic cortical recording with parylene biocompatible encapsulation; the architectural pattern of high-density needle electrodes plus skull-anchored connector that has dominated the BMI field for 30+ years.

## Inscopix nVista Head-Mounted Miniature Calcium-Imaging Microscope (2011-09)

- **id**: `inscopix-nvista-miniscope`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Inscopix Inc. (Schnitzer lab Stanford spinout)
- **disclosure**: Ghosh et al., Nat. Methods 2011, 8, 871-878. DOI:10.1038/nmeth.1694; Inscopix nVista commercial launch 2014.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Head-mounted single-photon fluorescence microscope using an implanted GRIN (gradient-index) lens to access deep brain structures (striatum, hippocampus). Microfluidic-relevant content: the GRIN lens forms a chronic optical-fluidic boundary into the brain (similar role to electrode parylene); LED illumination thermal management uses a heat-spreading interface where saline / tissue contact mediates heat dissipation. Discloses: integrated 2-g microscope including LED, dichroic, GRIN coupling, CMOS sensor, and DAQ; chronic GRIN implantation with bone-cement skull mount; freely-behaving fluorescence imaging at single-cell resolution. Anticipates: head-mounted miniaturized fluorescence microscopes with chronic implanted optical access ports; nVoke pattern of co-located imaging + optogenetic actuation through a single GRIN port.

## SetPoint Medical Implantable Vagus Nerve Stimulator (2012-07-05)

- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: SetPoint Medical Corporation
- **disclosure**: Koopman et al., PNAS 2016, 113, 8284-8289. DOI:10.1073/pnas.1605635113; SetPoint Medical RESET-RA trial NCT04539964; FDA breakthrough designation 2020.

## Synchron Stentrode Endovascular Brain-Computer Interface (2016-02-08)

- **id**: `synchron-stentrode`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Synchron Inc.
- **disclosure**: Oxley et al., Nat. Biotechnol. 2016, 34, 320-327. DOI:10.1038/nbt.3428; FDA IDE approval 2021-07.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Microfluidic relevance is the vascular-deployment context: the Stentrode is a self-expanding stent platform with electrodes mounted on the strut, deployed via standard endovascular catheter through saline-flushed delivery sheaths. Discloses: catheter-based delivery of an electrode-bearing stent through sub-mm guidewire navigation in jugular -> sigmoid sinus -> superior sagittal sinus; saline irrigation of the delivery system; chronic blood-immersed electrode recording from cortex without breaching the dura; subcutaneous lead routing to a chest-mounted internal transmitter (BrainPort) and external receiver. Anticipates: vascularly-deployed neural interfaces using standard interventional radiology catheters; saline-irrigation-based delivery of permanent neural electrodes; the full architectural pattern of intracranial recording without craniotomy.

## Iota Biosciences Ultrasonic Neural Dust Implant (2016-08-03)

- **id**: `iota-biosciences-neural-dust`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Iota Biosciences (UC Berkeley spinout; Carmena/Maharbiz labs)
- **disclosure**: Seo et al., Neuron 2016, 91, 529-539. DOI:10.1016/j.neuron.2016.06.034; Piech et al., Nat. Biomed. Eng. 2020, 4, 207-222.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Sub-mm wireless neural implant powered and read out via external ultrasonic transducer. ## Neuropixels High-Density Silicon Probe (2017-11-09)

- **id**: `neuropixels-imec-hhmi`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: IMEC + HHMI Janelia + UCL + Allen Institute consortium
- **disclosure**: Jun et al., Nature 2017, 551, 232-236. DOI:10.1038/nature24636; Steinmetz et al., Science 2021, 372, eabf4588. DOI:10.1126/science.abf4588
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: CMOS-foundry-fabricated high-density silicon shank probe with on-shank multiplexing electronics, distributed by IMEC/HHMI at near-cost as a research enabler. Discloses: monolithic CMOS-process integration of electrodes with amplifiers, multiplexers, and ADCs on a single 1-cm Si shank narrow enough (70 um) for repeated insertion in mouse cortex with minimal damage; parylene encapsulation as the saline boundary. Anticipates: foundry-CMOS-integrated neural probes; the design choice of multiplexing 960+ sites onto 384 simultaneous channels via on-probe switches; standardization of the 384-channel/probe form factor that has shaped commercial successors (NeuralinK N1, Paradromics). Microfluidic-relevant element is parylene encapsulation as ECF barrier.

## DARPA RAM Replay Memory-Restoration Neural Implant (2018-03-28)

- **id**: `darpa-ram-replay-memory-implant`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Wake Forest School of Medicine + USC + DARPA Biological Technologies Office
- **disclosure**: Hampson et al., J. Neural Eng. 2018, 15, 036014. DOI:10.1088/1741-2552/aaaed7; DARPA RAM Replay program kickoff 2014.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: First-in-human demonstration of a closed-loop memory-pattern injection neural implant. Patient-specific pattern decoder (trained on hippocampal CA3 activity during memory encoding) drives a stimulator that injects the decoded pattern back into CA1 during recall. Microfluidic-relevant content: parylene encapsulation of the chronic Pt-Ir microwire array; titanium skull-port forming a percutaneous fluidic seal. Anticipates: closed-loop memory-restoration BMI using model-based pattern injection; chronic subdural multielectrode arrays connected to programmable IPG-style pulse generators; the architectural pattern of decoder-on-implant + stimulator-on-implant for neuroprosthetic memory.

## Rivnay-Lab Organic Iontronic Neural Interface (2018-09-27)

- **id**: `rivnay-iontronic-neural-interface`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Rivnay Lab (Northwestern University)
- **disclosure**: Rivnay et al., Nat. Rev. Mater. 2018, 3, 17086. DOI:10.1038/natrevmats.2017.86; Cea et al., Nat. Mater. 2020, 19, 679-686 (PEDOT:PSS injectable neural interface).
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Genuinely fluidic neural interface: the OECT mechanism uses cation injection from cerebrospinal fluid into a PEDOT:PSS channel to modulate hole density and thus channel current. The ionic conduction (interstitial K+/Na+ ions traversing into the conducting polymer) is the transduction mechanism. Discloses: organic electrochemical transistor front-ends fabricated on parylene-encapsulated flex substrates; ionic-electronic mixed conduction as the amplification mechanism; soft mechanical compliance matched to brain modulus to reduce gliosis. Anticipates: iontronic neural amplification using polymer/electrolyte interfaces; soft, mechanically-matched neural interfaces using PEDOT:PSS; the architectural pattern of distributed amplification at the electrode site eliminating noise on long flex cables. Genuinely a microfluidic-electronics hybrid.

## Neuralink R1 Surgical Robot for Threaded Microelectrode Insertion (2019-07-16)

- **id**: `neuralink-r1-surgical-robot`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Neuralink Corporation
- **disclosure**: Musk, E. & Neuralink (2019). An integrated brain-machine interface platform with thousands of channels. J. Med. Internet Res. 21:e16194. DOI:10.2196/16194; PRIME study FDA IDE approval 2023-05.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Borderline-microfluidic but included for the saline-bath / cortical-irrigation aspects: the R1 surgical robot inserts thin-film flex-thread electrodes into cortical tissue submerged in continuous saline irrigation, with a peristaltic micro-pump providing hemostatic flow at the insertion site. Discloses: precision needle threading with sub-50-um lateral accuracy in soft tissue while immersed in saline; closed-loop optical tracking of cortical surface topography; integrated micro-irrigation flow at the needle tip to clear blood at puncture; the entire architectural pattern of robotic, sub-100-um thread insertion at scale (1024+ threads per session). Anticipates: robotically-inserted high-channel-count cortical neural interfaces with saline-irrigated insertion sites; micro-irrigation as a hemostatic / clearing mechanism at neural insertion points; flex-PI thread electrodes formed via thin-film fab and robotically deployed.

- **device class**: other
