## GEA ConsiGma 25 Continuous Tableting Line (2010)

- **id**: `gea-consigma-25-continuous-tableting`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: GEA Pharma Systems (Belgium)
- **disclosure**: Vercruysse, J. et al. 'Continuous twin screw granulation: Influence of process variables on granule and tablet quality.' European Journal of Pharmaceutics and Biopharmaceutics 82, 205-211 (2012), doi:10.1016/j.ejpb.2012.05.010; GEA ConsiGma 25 product brochure, GEA Pharma Systems 2010-2012
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the GEA ConsiGma 25 continuous wet-granulation-and-drying line with integrated twin-screw granulator and segmented fluid-bed dryer. The dryer comprises six independent fluid-bed cells indexed by a rotary distributor that allows continuous operation while individual cells cycle through fill/dry/discharge phases. Anticipates: (a) segmented-rotary fluid-bed dryers for continuous granule processing; (b) integrated twin-screw wet granulator with in-line moisture PAT; (c) the architectural pattern of a granulator/dryer module physically separated from but synchronized with a downstream tablet press. Cite against later patents claiming segmented-rotary fluid-bed dryers for continuous pharmaceutical wet granulation.

## Pfizer ECO/ECOnTab Continuous Tableting Platform (2017)

- **id**: `pfizer-eco-econtab-continuous-tableting`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Pfizer Inc., Groton CT and Sandwich UK
- **disclosure**: Allison, G. et al. 'Regulatory and Quality Considerations for Continuous Manufacturing.' Journal of Pharmaceutical Sciences 104, 803-812 (2015), doi:10.1002/jps.24324; Pfizer continuous-manufacturing program, ISPE conference presentations 2017-2019
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Pfizer's ECO/ECOnTab continuous tableting platform integrating wet granulation, drying, and tableting in a sealed skid. The platform shares the GEA-Pfizer-Glatt collaboration heritage but introduces Pfizer-proprietary control software and PAT integration patterns. Anticipates: (a) two-tier scale (5 kg/h portable / 100 kg/h fixed) implementations of the same control software stack; (b) Daurismo-class continuous-manufacturing approvals using ECO; (c) integration of in-line viscosity and torque sensors as soft-sensors for real-time release. Cite against later patent claims for two-tier-scale continuous tableting platforms.
