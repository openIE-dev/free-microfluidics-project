## ApoCell ApoStream CTC Capture System (2012)

- **id**: `apocell-apostream-ctc-dep`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: ApoCell Inc. (acquired by Precision for Medicine)
- **disclosure**: Gupta V., Jafferji I., Garza M., et al., Biomicrofluidics 6:024133 (2012), doi:10.1063/1.4731647; ApoCell Inc. product literature; Precision for Medicine ApoStream technical brief 2018
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: ApoStream discloses a continuous-flow DEP field-flow fractionation chamber wherein a PCB interdigitated electrode array generates a non-uniform AC field that levitates erythrocytes (negative DEP) while CTCs (different polarizability) flow at a different streamline elevation, enabling label-free separation. Anticipates: (i) continuous-flow DEP-FFF CTC capture systems; (ii) PCB-electrode microfluidic devices for blood processing; (iii) antibody-independent rare-cell isolation by polarizability. Lineage descends from Gascoyne 2002 DEP cancer-cell foundation but adds productized cartridge and multi-mL/hr throughput.

## Tony Jun Huang Lab Tunable Acoustic Tweezers (2014-12)

- **id**: `huang-penn-state-2014-tsaw-acoustic-tweezers`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Tony Jun Huang group (Penn State, later Duke)
- **disclosure**: Ding X., Peng Z., Lin S.-C. S., et al. (Huang lab), PNAS 111(36):12992-12997 (2014), doi:10.1073/pnas.1413325111
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses tilted-angle standing surface acoustic wave (taSSAW) chip wherein two opposing IDTs generate a standing wave at an angle to the flow direction; particles experience a pressure-gradient force that translates them across streamlines as a function of size and compressibility. Anticipates: (i) SSAW-based label-free cell sorters with continuous outflow; (ii) acoustic separators tuned by transducer angle rather than frequency; (iii) integration of taSSAW with downstream sequencing inputs (e.g., CTC enrichment). Companion to Ding 2012 acoustic-tweezer entry but adds productizable separation geometry.
