## Malvern Panalytical Zetasizer Ultra LNP Sizing Cuvette (2018-05)

- **id**: `malvern-zetasizer-ultra-lnp`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Malvern Panalytical Ltd. (Spectris plc)
- **disclosure**: Malvern Panalytical Zetasizer Ultra launch 2018-05; product datasheet rev 2024; multiple LNP characterization application notes
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the disposable folded-capillary electrode cell (DTS1080) and DLS / MADLS measurement architecture used as the de-facto industry-standard LNP particle-size and zeta-potential release assay. Anticipates: disposable-cuvette LNP characterization workflows where the same cuvette serves DLS sizing and electrophoretic zeta-potential measurement; the folded-capillary geometry enables electrode-cell measurement without contamination between samples. Cited in essentially every mRNA-LNP CMC package; the Zetasizer Ultra MADLS variant adds multi-angle resolution distinguishing populations within the same DLS size range.
