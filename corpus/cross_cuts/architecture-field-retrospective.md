## Lab on a Chip 2018 — 30 years of microfluidics special issue (editorial + retrospective) (2018)

- **id**: `lab-chip-2018-30-years-microfluidics-special-issue`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Lab on a Chip editorial board (Aaron Wheeler et al.)
- **disclosure**: Lab Chip vol 18 — '30 Years of microfluidics' editorial and themed collection; doi:10.1039/C8LC90075B (editorial)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Special-issue editorial and themed collection in Lab on a Chip celebrating 30 years since the µTAS concept. Aggregates a citable retrospective of the entire field's published prior art. Anticipates: any post-2018 patent claim that re-frames a previously published microfluidic concept (paper-based, droplet, organ-on-chip, dmf, etc.) as novel; the retrospective volume serves as a single-citation umbrella that an examiner can use to reject 'novel microfluidic platform' claims as obvious in view of the field history.
