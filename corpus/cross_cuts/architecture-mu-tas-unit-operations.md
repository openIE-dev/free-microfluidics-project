## Auroux, Iossifidis, Reyes & Manz 2002 — Micro total analysis systems part 2 (analytical standard operations and applications) (2002-06-15)

- **id**: `auroux-2002-utas-review-anal-chem`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Pierre-Alain Auroux, Dimitri Iossifidis, Darwin R. Reyes, Andreas Manz
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1021/ac020239t; Anal. Chem. 74, 2637-2652 (2002)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Companion review to Reyes 2002 (already in the corpus); covers analytical standard operations on chip — sample injection, mixing, reaction, separation, and detection. Catalogues by 2002 the entire 'unit operations on chip' library: pneumatic and thermal valving, electrokinetic injection, T- and Y-mixers, electrophoretic separation, optical and electrochemical detection. Anticipates: any patent that combines two or more of these unit operations into an 'integrated microfluidic system' — the combinatorial space was already published. Specifically defeats novelty for 'on-chip injector + separator + detector' genus claims after 2002.
