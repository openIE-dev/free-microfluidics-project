- **prior art notes**: Hughes 2002 textbook and Markx 1999 review codify the field of dielectrophoresis (DEP) for biological microfluidics, providing element-by-element disclosure of: (a) electrode geometries (interdigitated, polynomial, castellated, parallel-bar); (b) DEP modalities (positive/negative DEP, traveling-wave DEP, electrorotation, DEP-FFF); (c) integration with microfluidic flow channels; (d) cell-electric-properties database. Anticipates broad downstream patent claims combining DEP electrode geometries with microfluidic flow for cell separation, trapping, and characterization.
