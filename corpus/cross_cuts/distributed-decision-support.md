## Conjoiner Nest Medical Chamber (Galiana's Chamber) (2000)

- **id**: `reynolds-conjoiner-nest-medical-galiana`
- **corpus**: fictional
- **device class**: fictional-laboratory
- **creator**: Alastair Reynolds
- **disclosure**: Alastair Reynolds, 'Revelation Space' (Gollancz, 2000); 'Glacial' (in 'Galactic North' collection, Gollancz, 2006, ISBN 0-575-07910-2)
- **ip status**: fictional
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a medical chamber whose telemetry is coupled in real-time to a distributed collective-cognition network of clinicians (the Conjoiner mesh), allowing 'crowd-sourced' diagnosis and treatment decisions for the patient inside the pod. Anticipates: networked clinical pods that publish full patient state to a remote panel of clinicians for collaborative diagnosis, including the architectural pattern of telemetry + actuator coupling between pod and remote clinician collective. Bears on tele-ICU systems and 'distributed clinical decision support' literature.
