## Janssen ConTabLine Continuous Tableting Platform (2018)

- **id**: `janssen-contabline-continuous-tableting-line`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Janssen Pharmaceuticals (Johnson & Johnson)
- **disclosure**: Vanhoorne, V. et al. 'Continuous Manufacturing of Solid Oral Dosage Forms.' Drug Discovery Today: Technologies 28, 27-37 (2018), doi:10.1016/j.ddtec.2018.03.003; Janssen ConTabLine technical disclosure ISPE Pharmaceutical Engineering 38(4), 2018
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Janssen's ConTabLine modular continuous-manufacturing skid combining wet granulation, drying, milling, and tableting on a single integrated control system with multi-point PAT monitoring. Anticipates: (a) the modular skid architecture for continuous tableting (later imitated by GEA ConsiGma and L.B. Bohle Kompress); (b) multi-PAT feedforward/feedback control across each unit operation in the line; (c) the design pattern of validating a single skid template that hosts multiple drug products with formulation-specific recipe parameters. Cite against later patents claiming integrated wet-granulation-to-tableting modular skids with PAT control loops.
