## Cerus INTERCEPT Blood System (amotosalen/UVA pathogen-reduction) (1997)

- **id**: `cerus-intercept-blood-system-pathogen-reduction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Cerus Corporation
- **disclosure**: Lin L. et al., Photochemical inactivation of viruses and bacteria in platelet concentrates by use of a novel psoralen and long-wavelength ultraviolet light, Transfusion 37:423-435 (1997); Cerus INTERCEPT Blood System; FDA approval (platelets/plasma) 2014.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a closed disposable set that photochemically inactivates pathogens in a blood product (amotosalen + UVA) and then passes the treated unit through a packed-bed adsorbent column to remove residual photoactive compound. Anticipates claims to psoralen/UVA pathogen reduction of platelets and plasma in defined-geometry illumination bags, packed-bed compound-adsorption devices for scrubbing photoproducts from blood, and closed-system pathogen-reduction processing sets.
