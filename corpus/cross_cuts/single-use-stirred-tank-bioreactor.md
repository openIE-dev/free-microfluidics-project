## Cytiva Xcellerex XDR-50/200/500/1000/2000 Single-Use Bioreactor Family (2008)

- **id**: `cytiva-xcellerex-xdr-extension`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Cytiva (formerly GE Healthcare / Xcellerex)
- **disclosure**: Xcellerex XDR launch 2008 (acquired by GE Healthcare 2012, now Cytiva); XDR datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Extends the existing cytiva-xcellerex-xdr entry by enumerating the full scaling family (XDR-10 through XDR-2000) and the cube-shaped bag with bottom-mounted impeller geometry. Anticipates: cube-bag bottom-impeller single-use bioreactor architectures across a 10L-2000L scaling family providing geometric similarity. Distinguished from Sartorius STR (cylindrical bag, top-mounted impeller) and Pall Allegro STR (different impeller geometry).

## Sartorius Biostat STR 50/200 Single-Use Stirred Tank Bioreactor (2009)

- **id**: `sartorius-biostat-str-50-200`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Sartorius Stedim Biotech
- **disclosure**: Sartorius Biostat STR launch 2009; STR Generation 3 launch 2017; Biostat STR datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use stirred tank bioreactor scaling family (50L-2000L) where the Flexsafe STR bag includes pre-installed impeller, sparger, and sensors, eliminating cleaning validation and reducing changeover time vs stainless-steel CHO bioreactors. Anticipates: scalable single-use stirred-tank bioreactor architectures with pre-installed impeller and sensors for clinical-to-commercial mAb and gene-therapy manufacturing; specifically the architectural pattern of identical bag geometry and impeller across the scaling family eliminating geometric scale-up reformulation. Direct competitor to Cytiva Xcellerex XDR and Pall Allegro STR. STR Generation 3 adds Hamilton Arc digital sensors and BioPAT integration.

## Pall Allegro STR 50/200/500/1000/2000 Single-Use Stirred Tank Bioreactor (2014)

- **id**: `pall-allegro-str-extension`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Pall Corporation (Danaher Corporation)
- **disclosure**: Pall Allegro STR 200 launch 2014; Allegro STR scaling-family expansion 2016-2020; Allegro STR datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use stirred tank bioreactor scaling family with cylindrical bag, bottom-mounted bidirectional impeller, and dual sparger configuration. Anticipates: cylindrical-bag bottom-impeller single-use bioreactor architectures with bidirectional impeller and dual sparger across 50L-2000L scaling family. Distinguished from Sartorius STR (cylindrical, top-impeller) and Cytiva Xcellerex XDR (cube, bottom-impeller) by impeller direction-reversibility.
