# Cross-cut: `separation-centrifugal`

**10 corpus entries disclose this subsystem.**

Earliest disclosure: 1988

Listed in chronological order. Each entry's `prior_art_notes` and
`disclosure_citation` constitute the citeable prior art material.

## DiaMed ID-System gel card (column agglutination technology) (1988)

- **id**: `diamed-id-system-gel-card`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: DiaMed AG (now Bio-Rad Laboratories)
- **disclosure**: Lapierre Y. et al., The gel test: a new way to detect red cell antigen-antibody reactions, Transfusion 30:109-113 (1990); DiaMed AG ID-System introduced 1988; foundational patent Lapierre EP0305337 / US5338689.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a microtube card containing a packed dextran-gel (or glass-bead) column through which red cells are centrifuged so that agglutinates are size-trapped at the column top and free cells pellet below - a centrifugal microfluidic size-separation immunoassay. Anticipates claims to (a) gel-column / glass-bead column agglutination cards for blood typing and antibody detection; (b) centrifugal red-cell separation as an agglutination endpoint; (c) automated reading of column reaction patterns. Foundational prior art for ortho-vision-gel-card, bio-rad-ih-1000, grifols-erytra-dg-gel.

## Fresenius Kabi Amicus Separator (apheresis) (1996)

- **id**: `fresenius-amicus-apheresis`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Fresenius Kabi (formerly Fenwal / Baxter Healthcare)
- **disclosure**: Fresenius Kabi (formerly Fenwal/Baxter) Amicus Separator operator manual; FDA clearance 1996 (platelet collection), later clearances for plasma, MNC, RBC and granulocyte collection.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a two-stage continuous-flow centrifugal apheresis set in which a downstream collection chamber operates as a fluidized particle bed that elutriates and traps platelets while plasma flows through, under optical interface control. Anticipates claims to fluidized-bed/elutriation platelet collection, two-stage centrifugal apheresis channels, and concurrent multi-component collection. Related to terumo-bct-spectra-optia-apheresis and terumo-bct-trima-accel-apheresis.
