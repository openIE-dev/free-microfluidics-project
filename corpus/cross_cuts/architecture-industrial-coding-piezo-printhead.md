## Trident ITW PixelJet Piezo Printhead (extended) (1995)

- **id**: `trident-itw-pixeljet-piezo-printhead`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Trident International (ITW Industrial Inkjet)
- **disclosure**: Trident International (Brookfield, CT, USA, ITW subsidiary) PixelJet 256 datasheet 1995; Trident PixelJet Print datasheet 2008; ITW Trident product line history
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Extension to wave 1 Trident UltraJet entry. Trident's PixelJet line targeted the higher-resolution, smaller-drop industrial coding niche left by the original UltraJet. Architectural primitives: PZT-shear-mode bar with machined channels (cf. Xaar shared-wall) bonded to a precision stainless orifice plate, with each PZT segment driving a single channel via shear deflection. Distinct from Xaar in that Trident did NOT share walls between adjacent channels - each channel has its own PZT segment, simplifying drive electronics at the cost of channel density. Anticipates: industrial coding piezo printheads using non-shared-wall shear-mode PZT actuation with field-replaceable orifice plate.
