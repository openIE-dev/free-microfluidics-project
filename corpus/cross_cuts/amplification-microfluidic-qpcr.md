- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Olink Proteomics AB
- **disclosure**: Lundberg M. et al., Homogeneous antibody-based proximity extension assays provide sensitive and specific detection of low-abundant proteins in human blood, Nucleic Acids Res 39:e102 (2011); Olink Bioscience / Olink Proteomics (now part of Thermo Fisher).
