## Paradromics Connexus Direct Data Interface (2020-06)

- **id**: `paradromics-connexus`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Paradromics Inc.
- **disclosure**: Sahasrabuddhe et al. (Paradromics) The Argo: a 65,536 channel recording system for high density neural recording in vivo. J. Neural Eng. 2021, 18, 015002. DOI:10.1088/1741-2552/abd0ce; FDA Breakthrough Device designation 2023-05.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Largely solid-state but included for the hermetic-fluidic-seal architecture, which is the implant-microfluidic boundary. Discloses: a hermetic titanium can with platinum-iridium microwire feedthroughs forming a sub-um helium-leak-rate barrier between brain (saline ECF) and electronics; biocompatible silicone overcoat at the cortical interface; 1600 microwires bundled into a deployable carrier. Anticipates: high-channel-count microwire neural implants with engineering-grade hermetic seals (qualified to MIL-STD vacuum/saline cycling); the brain-electronics interface as a microfluidic seal problem; bundled microwire arrays as a higher-channel-count alternative to silicon Utah arrays for chronic implantation.
