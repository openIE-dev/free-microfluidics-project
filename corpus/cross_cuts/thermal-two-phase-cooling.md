- **disclosure**: US5048602A Condenser for automotive air conditioning systems (Nippondenso); see also US5172761A (Showa Aluminum) heat exchanger with multiport flat tubes; SAE technical papers on parallel-flow automotive condensers, early 1990s.
- **ip status**: patented

## Bowers & Mudawar 1994 - High flux boiling in mini/micro-channel heat sinks (1994)

- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: cooling-substrate
- **creator**: Mark B. Bowers; Issam Mudawar
- **disclosure**: Bowers, M. B., Mudawar, I. (1994). 'High flux boiling in low flow rate, low pressure drop mini-channel and micro-channel heat sinks.' Int. J. Heat Mass Transfer 37(2): 321-332. doi:10.1016/0017-9310(94)90103-1

- **prior art notes**: Extends Tuckerman-Pease 1981 (already in corpus) into the two-phase microchannel cooling regime. Anticipates any patent that recites 'two-phase microchannel cold plate', 'boiling-augmented microchannel cooling', or 'critical heat flux limited microchannel heat sink'. Load-bearing for modern chip-cooling patents (Microsoft, IBM, Intel direct-liquid cooling claims).

## Kandlikar 2005 — Microchannel Boiling and Two-Phase Cooling Review (2005)

- **id**: `kandlikar-2005-microchannel-boiling-review`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: cooling-substrate
- **creator**: Satish G. Kandlikar
- **disclosure**: Kandlikar, S. G. (2005). 'High flux heat removal with microchannels — a roadmap of challenges and opportunities.' Heat Transfer Engineering 26(8): 5-14. doi:10.1080/01457630591003655. Companion: Annual Review of Heat Transfer 14, 233-278 (2005).
- **ip status**: public-domain

Together with Tuckerman-Pease 1981 anchors the entire microchannel-cooling prior art lineage.
