## Quake & Scherer 2000 — From micro- to nanofabrication with soft materials (2000-11-24)

- **id**: `quake-scherer-2000-from-micro-to-nano-soft-materials`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Stephen R. Quake, Axel Scherer
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1126/science.290.5496.1536; Science 290(5496) 1536-1540 (2000)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Science perspective laying out the case that soft-lithography in PDMS scales from micron to sub-100-nm features and is the dominant fabrication path for microfluidics, optoelectronics, and biotechnology. Disclosed before the Quake-group monolithic-membrane-valve and large-scale-integration patents matured into commercial Fluidigm products. Anticipates: genus claims to 'an elastomeric microfluidic device fabricated by replica molding from a master patterned by photolithography' and to 'multi-layer soft-lithography devices integrating control and flow layers' at the abstract architectural level. Particularly load-bearing because it timestamps the multi-layer-PDMS architecture in the Science archival record, independent of the Unger 2000 valve paper of the same year.
