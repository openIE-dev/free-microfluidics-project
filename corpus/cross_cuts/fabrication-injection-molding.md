## Becker & Locascio 2002 — Polymer microfluidic devices (review) (2002-04)

- **id**: `becker-locascio-2002-polymer-microfluidic-fabrication-review`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Holger Becker, Laurie E. Locascio
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1016/S0039-9140(01)00594-X; Talanta 56(2), 267-287 (2002)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Foundational review of polymer (PMMA, PC, COC, PS, PDMS) microfluidic fabrication: hot embossing, injection molding, laser ablation, solvent bonding, thermal bonding. Anticipates: any patent on a 'thermoplastic microfluidic device fabricated by hot-embossing or injection-molding with thermal or solvent bonding' as obvious in view of this 2002 review. Critical reference for commercial cartridge IP landscape (BioFire, Cepheid, Abbott ID Now, etc. all use thermoplastic processes documented here).

## Madou 2011 — Fundamentals of Microfabrication and Nanotechnology (3rd ed, 3-volume) (textbook) (2011-08-01)

- **id**: `madou-2011-fundamentals-microfabrication-3rd-edition`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Marc J. Madou
- **disclosure**: ISBN 978-0849331800 (vol 1) / 978-1420055115 (set); CRC Press, 3rd edition 2011 (1st ed 1997, 2nd ed 2002)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Three-volume engineering reference covering MEMS and microfluidic fabrication: photolithography, soft-lithography, hot embossing, injection molding, LIGA, deep RIE, glass etching, bonding (anodic, fusion, thermocompression, plasma), and integration. Volume III specifically covers manufacturing techniques for microfluidics. Anticipates: any patent on a 'method of fabricating a microfluidic device' that claims a process step (lithography, etch, mold, bond) that is documented in Madou's textbook before the patent priority date. Standard examiner reference for fabrication 102/103 rejections.

## NanoCellect WOLF Cell Sorter (2017)

- **id**: `nanocellect-wolf-microfluidic-sorter`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: NanoCellect Biomedical Inc.
- **disclosure**: NanoCellect Biomedical Inc. WOLF launch press release 2017; product datasheet WOLF G2; US patent 9,372,144
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: WOLF discloses a disposable thermoplastic microfluidic cartridge with a piezoelectric microvalve that diverts target droplets into collection wells; gentle pressures and short residence times preserve viability of sensitive cells (stem, primary T cells). Anticipates: (i) cartridge-based gentle cell sorters distinct from droplet-in-air FACS; (ii) piezo-microvalve-actuated sort decisions in microfluidic chips; (iii) sterile single-use sample paths for clinical-adjacent sorting. Distinguished from BD/Sony FACS by closed-cartridge fluidics rather than nozzle-and-deflection-plate architecture.
