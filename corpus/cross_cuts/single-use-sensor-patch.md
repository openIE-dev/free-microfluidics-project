## PreSens Precision Sensing Sensor Spot Patches (pH / DO / CO2) (1998)

- **id**: `presens-sensor-spot-patches`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: PreSens Precision Sensing GmbH
- **disclosure**: PreSens Precision Sensing GmbH founding 1997; SP-PSt3 oxygen sensor spot launch ~1998; product datasheets rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses single-use fluorophore-spot sensor patches adhered to the inside wall of a transparent bioreactor vessel, with external optical readout (LED excitation, photodiode emission detection through the vessel wall). Anticipates: non-invasive optical sensor patches for single-use bioreactor pH / DO / CO2 monitoring, eliminating the autoclave-and-recalibrate burden of traditional electrochemical probes; specifically the architectural pattern of pre-calibrated disposable sensor patches that ship inside the gamma-sterilized single-use vessel, with all electronics external. Foundational for the single-use bioreactor PAT category, OEM-integrated by virtually every single-use bioreactor vendor (Sartorius Flexsafe, Eppendorf BioBLU, Applikon AppliFlex) for pH / DO / CO2 monitoring.
