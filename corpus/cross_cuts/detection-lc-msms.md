## WADA Athlete Biological Passport Microfluidic Sample Handling Chain (2009)

- **id**: `wada-abp-microfluidic-sample-handling`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA)
- **disclosure**: World Anti-Doping Agency 'Athlete Biological Passport Operating Guidelines' v1.0 (December 2009); WADA International Standard for Laboratories (ISL)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the WADA-mandated Athlete Biological Passport workflow, including microfluidic-flow-cytometric blood module measurements (Sysmex XN-series, Beckman Coulter DxH) and microfluidic-LC-MS/MS steroidal module measurements with strict chain-of-custody. Anticipates as governance/standardization layer: (a) microfluidic instrument harmonization for forensic-grade longitudinal biomarker monitoring; (b) chain-of-custody requirements binding lab-on-chip measurements; (c) the regulatory framework that defines microfluidic-instrument equivalence in sport. Does not specify chip designs.
