## GRAIL Galleri (multi-cancer early detection methylation cfDNA test) (2020)

- **id**: `grail-galleri-mced-methylation`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: GRAIL, LLC
- **disclosure**: Liu M.C. et al. (CCGA Consortium), Sensitive and specific multi-cancer detection and localization using methylation signatures in cell-free DNA, Ann Oncol 31:745-759 (2020); Galleri launched 2021; GRAIL (formerly Illumina subsidiary).
