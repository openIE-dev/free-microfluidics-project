## Twist Bioscience vs Agilent Technologies Trade-Secret Litigation (2016-02)

- **id**: `twist-bioscience-vs-agilent-litigation`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Agilent Technologies / Twist Bioscience
- **disclosure**: Agilent Technologies, Inc. v. Twist Bioscience Corp., et al., Superior Court of California, San Francisco County, Case No. CGC-16-550483 (filed 2016-02); subsequent cross-actions; settlement reported 2024
- **ip status**: trade-secret
- **prior art notes**: Records the long-running California state court trade-secret case in which Agilent accused former employee Emily Leproust (Twist co-founder/CEO) of misappropriating high-density oligo array synthesis trade secrets when she founded Twist Bioscience. Material to defensive-publication scope: (a) the public docket disclosed substantial detail about Agilent's SurePrint inkjet array technology; (b) the cross-complaints disclosed details of Twist's silicon DRIE process; (c) the settlement (reported approximately 2024) reportedly transferred a one-time payment with no admission, leaving both estates intact. The proceeding clarified the scope of public-domain knowledge at the time Twist was founded (2013) and is therefore relevant to validity challenges on either side's later patents.
