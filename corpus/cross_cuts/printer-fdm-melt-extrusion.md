## Triastek MED (Melt Extrusion Deposition) 3D-Printing Platform (2020)

- **id**: `triastek-med-3d-printing-platform`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Triastek Inc. (Nanjing, China)
- **disclosure**: Triastek Inc. press releases 2020-2023 (T19, T20, T21, T22 IND filings); FDA IND clearance T19 (rivaroxaban 3D-printed) 2020-12; Goyanes, A. et al. 'Patient acceptability of 3D printed medicines.' International Journal of Pharmaceutics 530, 71-78 (2017), doi:10.1016/j.ijpharm.2017.07.064
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Triastek's MED platform combining hot-melt extrusion (HME) with FDM 3D printing in a continuous in-line process. The HME barrel produces API+polymer melt that is directly extruded through a printhead nozzle (no intermediate filament), depositing layered tablet geometries with engineered drug-release compartments. Anticipates: (a) direct HME-to-FDM continuous printing as an alternative to filament-based pharmaceutical FDM; (b) multi-compartment tablet geometries with spatially programmed drug-release kinetics; (c) the regulatory template for IND clearance of 3D-printed reformulations of approved APIs. Cite against later patents claiming direct-extrusion HME-FDM pharmaceutical 3D printers.
