- **id**: `sysmex-brewmaster-yeast-cell-counter`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Sysmex Partec GmbH (now Sysmex Corporation)
- **disclosure**: Sysmex Partec GmbH product brochure 'Brewmaster - Yeast Cell Counting and Viability for Breweries' (2009); ASBC method 'Yeast-3 Yeast Cell Count by Flow Cytometry' (2010)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a benchtop microfluidic flow cytometer dedicated to brewing yeast counting and viability, integrating a glass micro-flow cell with hydrodynamic focusing, optical excitation, and impedance pulse counting. Does not disclose multi-strain wild-yeast discrimination.
