## Krytox-PFPE surfactant for biocompatible droplet stabilization (2008)

- **id**: `abate-2010-surfactant-survey`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Weitz group, Harvard
- **disclosure**: Holtze, C.; Rowat, A. C.; Agresti, J. J.; Hutchison, J. B.; Angilè, F. E.; Schmitz, C. H. J.; Köster, S.; Duan, H.; Humphry, K. J.; Scanga, R. A.; Johnson, J. S.; Pisignano, D.; Weitz, D. A. Biocompatible surfactants for water-in-fluorocarbon emulsions. Lab Chip 2008, 8, 1632–1639. DOI: 10.1039/b806706f
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Disclosed PFPE-PEG triblock surfactants in fluorocarbon oil for biocompatible water-in-oil droplet stabilization. The chemistry that made droplet-format single-cell biology possible: previous surfactants either lysed cells or destabilized droplets within hours. Anticipates: PFPE-PEG surfactant as the de facto standard for single-cell droplet workflows, used in essentially every published Drop-seq, inDrops, ddPCR, and 10x Chromium experiment.
