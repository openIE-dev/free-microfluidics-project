## Fujifilm Dimatix Samba LFM Life-Sciences Printhead (2016)

- **id**: `fujifilm-dimatix-samba-lfm-life-sciences-printhead`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Fujifilm Dimatix Inc. (Lebanon, NH)
- **disclosure**: Fujifilm Dimatix Samba LFM datasheet 2016; Samba LM datasheet; Fujifilm Dimatix application note for life sciences
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a Samba-derived MEMS piezo printhead specifically engineered for life-sciences fluids: aqueous, cell-containing, and protein-bearing inks. Architectural primitives include biocompatible wetted-material selection (no copper or zinc in contact with bio fluid; PEEK/Parylene linings) and a low-shear flow path that minimizes turbulent stress and chamber dead zones to preserve cell viability through the printhead. Anticipates: MEMS piezo printheads engineered for life-sciences applications with biocompatible wetted-material set and shear-minimized fluid path. Important prior art for any bioprinter claim that uses a commercial graphic-arts printhead repurposed with a bio-compatible fluid path.
