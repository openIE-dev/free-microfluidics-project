## Sysmex Brewmaster Yeast Viability and Cell Counter (2009)

- **id**: `sysmex-brewmaster-yeast-cell-counter`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Sysmex Partec GmbH (now Sysmex Corporation)
- **disclosure**: Sysmex Partec GmbH product brochure 'Brewmaster - Yeast Cell Counting and Viability for Breweries' (2009); ASBC method 'Yeast-3 Yeast Cell Count by Flow Cytometry' (2010)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a benchtop microfluidic flow cytometer dedicated to brewing yeast counting and viability, integrating a glass micro-flow cell with hydrodynamic focusing, optical excitation, and impedance pulse counting. Anticipates: (a) microfluidic flow-cell architecture for yeast viability quantitation in breweries; (b) integrated stain-and-count workflow tailored to S. cerevisiae and S. pastorianus; (c) brewery-floor deployment of a lab-on-chip cytometer rather than off-line manual hemocytometer counts. Does not disclose multi-strain wild-yeast discrimination.

## ASBC Method Yeast-3: Flow Cytometric Yeast Count and Viability (2010)

- **id**: `asbc-yeast-3-flow-cytometry-method`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: American Society of Brewing Chemists (ASBC)
- **disclosure**: American Society of Brewing Chemists, Methods of Analysis, Yeast-3 'Yeast Cell Count and Viability by Flow Cytometry' (approved 2010, revised 2015)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the ASBC consensus protocol for using a microfluidic flow cytometer for yeast viability assessment in breweries, normalizing sample dilution, stain selection (PI/SYTO), gating, and reportable units. Anticipates: (a) standardized microfluidic flow cytometric workflow for brewing QC, vendor-neutral; (b) consensus interoperability of microfluidic instruments through a normative method document; (c) the practice of brewery harmonization on lab-on-chip cytometry. Does not disclose any specific cartridge geometry.

## Anchor Beer (Asia Pacific Breweries) Fermentation Microfluidic Monitoring System (2015)

- **id**: `anchor-beer-fermentation-microfluidic-monitoring`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Asia Pacific Breweries Singapore (Heineken Asia Pacific) with SIMTech
- **disclosure**: Asia Pacific Breweries technical poster, World Brewing Congress 2016 'Inline microfluidic monitoring of fermentation extract and ethanol at the Tuas Brewery'; Singapore Institute of Manufacturing Technology project 1421-FERM final report (2015)
- **ip status**: trade-secret
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a microfluidic side-stream sampler off a 1000 hL fermenter at the Tuas Brewery, automatically diluting wort, transferring it through a PDMS-glass micro-flow cell for NIR transmission spectroscopy, and reporting Plato extract and ethanol every five minutes. Anticipates: (a) side-stream microfluidic flow cells for continuous fermentation monitoring; (b) inline dilution-and-spectrometry on a single microchip device; (c) brewery deployment of a pressure-driven lab-on-chip subsystem to displace daily off-line laboratory analysis. Does not disclose chip-level CIP cleaning protocol.

## Heineken Continuous Quality Control Microfluidic Sampler (2019)

- **id**: `heineken-continuous-qc-microfluidic`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Heineken N.V.
- **disclosure**: Heineken N.V. sustainability report 2019 'Brewing a Better World' QC modernization section; TU Delft - Heineken project communications 2018-2020
- **ip status**: trade-secret
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a side-stream microfluidic optical flow cell that continuously measures iso-alpha-acid bitterness (IBU) and color (EBC) on the brewing line, replacing batch off-line spectrophotometry. Anticipates: (a) inline microfluidic optical bitterness assay; (b) integration of a thermoplastic optical cell with brewery SCADA; (c) the architectural pattern of a continuous-flow chip QC instrument on a major macro-brewery line. Does not disclose specific anti-fouling protocol.

## ABV Atomic Beer Hops Quantification Microfluidic Chip (2021)

- **id**: `abv-atomic-hops-quant-chip`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: ABV Atomic Inc.
- **disclosure**: ABV Atomic Inc. product website launch announcement (2021); Brewing Industry Guild trade article 'Hops bitterness on a chip' (Brew Magazine, October 2021)
- **ip status**: trade-secret
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use microfluidic cartridge for craft-brewery hops bitterness quantification using on-chip solid-phase extraction followed by UV absorbance detection at 275 nm. Anticipates: (a) point-of-brew microfluidic IBU assay; (b) the architectural pattern of an SPE-microfluidic UV chip for hops phytochemistry; (c) cartridge-based decentralization of a wet-lab method (ASBC Beer-23 isohumulones). Does not disclose iso-isomerization on chip.
