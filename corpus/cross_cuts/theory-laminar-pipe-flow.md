## Hagen 1839 — On the motion of water in narrow cylindrical tubes (Hagen-Poiseuille flow) (1839)

- **id**: `hagen-1839-pipe-flow-viscosity`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Gotthilf Heinrich Ludwig Hagen
- **disclosure**: G. Hagen, 'Über die Bewegung des Wassers in engen cylindrischen Röhren', Poggendorffs Annalen der Physik und Chemie 46, 423-442 (1839)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: First quantitative experimental description of laminar pressure-driven flow in narrow cylindrical tubes, predating Poiseuille's 1840 publication. Establishes the fourth-power dependence of volumetric flow on tube radius (Hagen-Poiseuille law). Foundational for ALL pressure-driven microfluidic theory: every channel-resistance calculation, every flow-controller specification, every droplet-generator design implicitly relies on this 1839 disclosure. Anticipates: any patent that recites 'flow rate determined by pressure drop and channel hydraulic resistance' as part of its operating principle — the relation is 187 years old.
