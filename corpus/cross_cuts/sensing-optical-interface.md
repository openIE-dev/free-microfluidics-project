# Cross-cut: `sensing-optical-interface`

**5 corpus entries disclose this subsystem.**

Listed in chronological order. Each entry's `prior_art_notes` and
`disclosure_citation` constitute the citeable prior art material.

## Fresenius Kabi Amicus Separator (apheresis) (1996)

- **id**: `fresenius-amicus-apheresis`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Fresenius Kabi (formerly Fenwal / Baxter Healthcare)
- **disclosure**: Fresenius Kabi (formerly Fenwal/Baxter) Amicus Separator operator manual; FDA clearance 1996 (platelet collection), later clearances for plasma, MNC, RBC and granulocyte collection.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a two-stage continuous-flow centrifugal apheresis set in which a downstream collection chamber operates as a fluidized particle bed that elutriates and traps platelets while plasma flows through, under optical interface control. Anticipates claims to fluidized-bed/elutriation platelet collection, two-stage centrifugal apheresis channels, and concurrent multi-component collection. Related to terumo-bct-spectra-optia-apheresis and terumo-bct-trima-accel-apheresis.

## Terumo BCT Trima Accel Automated Blood Collection System (2002)

- **id**: `terumo-bct-trima-accel-apheresis`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Terumo Blood and Cell Technologies
- **disclosure**: Terumo BCT Trima Accel System operator manual; FDA clearance 2002 onward (RBC, platelet, plasma, concurrent collections); CaridianBCT/Gambro/Terumo lineage.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a continuous-flow centrifugal apheresis set with an integrated fluidized-cell-bed leukoreduction chamber and optical interface control for collecting red cells, platelets and plasma in concurrent combinations. Anticipates claims to in-line fluidized-bed leukoreduction during apheresis, concurrent multicomponent collection scheduling, and reverse-flow chamber purging in centrifugal separators. Related to terumo-bct-spectra-optia-apheresis and fresenius-amicus-apheresis.

## Cytiva Sepax C-Pro / Sepax 2 cell-processing system (2002)

- **id**: `cytiva-sepax-c-pro-cell-processing`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Cytiva (formerly Biosafe SA, then GE Healthcare)
- **disclosure**: Biosafe SA / Cytiva Sepax 2 and Sepax C-Pro operator manuals; Sepax introduced by Biosafe ~2002 (CE mark), Sepax C-Pro launched ~2018; Cytiva acquired Biosafe 2019.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a closed automated cell processor using a syringe-piston-driven rotating separation chamber with an optical layer sensor that reads density-stratified blood/cell fractions so the target layer is aspirated programmatically. Anticipates claims to optically-controlled centrifugal cell separation in single-use chambers, programmable fraction collection by piston volume control, and closed cord-blood / cell-therapy processing kits. Related to fresenius-kabi-lovo-cell-washing as an alternative architecture.

## Terumo BCT Spectra Optia Apheresis System (2008)

- **id**: `terumo-bct-spectra-optia-apheresis`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Terumo Blood and Cell Technologies
- **disclosure**: Terumo BCT Spectra Optia Apheresis System operator manual; CE mark 2008, FDA clearances 2008 onward.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a continuous-flow centrifugal blood-separation channel with optical interface tracking and automated peristaltic draw of stratified fractions for therapeutic exchange and cell collection. Anticipates claims to continuous-flow apheresis with optical interface control, mononuclear-cell collection channels for cell-therapy starting material, and automated fraction-draw scheduling. Related to haemonetics-nexsys-pcs-apheresis and fresenius-amicus-apheresis.

## Haemonetics NexSys PCS plasmapheresis system (Persona / PCS2 lineage) (2019)

- **id**: `haemonetics-nexsys-pcs-apheresis`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Haemonetics Corporation
- **disclosure**: Haemonetics NexSys PCS System operator manual; FDA clearance 2019 (NexSys PCS with Persona); PCS2 predicate from the 1990s; Latham-bowl lineage from Allen Latham.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses discontinuous Latham-bowl centrifugal plasmapheresis with optical interface detection and donor-personalized collection-volume nomograms. Anticipates claims to Latham-bowl source-plasma collection, optical interface-triggered bowl emptying, and personalized-yield apheresis control. Foundational Latham-bowl prior art (US3145713) predates and anticipates much modern apheresis bowl design.
