## Cytiva Mobius MyWay Single-Use Mixer / MIX10 / MIX100 / MIX1000 / MIX2000 (2010)

- **id**: `cytiva-mobius-myway`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: mixer-component
- **creator**: EMD Millipore (now part of Merck KGaA / Cytiva — note this product line transferred under reorganizations)
- **disclosure**: EMD Millipore (now Cytiva) Mobius MIX brochure 2010; Mobius MIX 2000 launch 2017.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Single-use mixing bag system competitive with Sartorius FlexAct in biopharmaceutical buffer / media / harvest mixing. Discloses: bottom-mounted magnetically-coupled impeller within a welded polyethylene single-use bag (no shaft penetration eliminates contamination); 10 L to 2000 L scale range; integration with single-use sensors (pH, conductivity, temperature) via membrane-mounted single-use probes. Anticipates: bottom-magnetic-impeller single-use mixer architecture as the standard for large-volume biopharma mixing; the architectural choice of magnetic coupling vs shaft seal for sterility; integrated single-use sensor interfaces.
