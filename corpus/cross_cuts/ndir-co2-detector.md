## BlueSens BlueInOne CO2 Off-gas Analyzer (2012)

- **id**: `bluesens-blueinone-co2`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: BlueSens gas sensor GmbH
- **disclosure**: BlueSens BCP-CO2 product launch ~2012; BlueInOne CO2 product datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Extends the existing bluesens-blueinone-offgas entry by disclosing the CO2-specific BlueInOne variant (NDIR-based CO2 measurement). Anticipates: dedicated NDIR CO2 off-gas analyzers in compact bioreactor-mounted form factor; specifically the architectural pattern of separate single-gas analyzers (vs combined O2+CO2 in one cabinet) allowing flexible deployment to existing bioreactors that need CO2-only or OUR/CER-only monitoring. Distinct from the multi-gas BlueInOne FERM by being CO2-only and lower-cost.
