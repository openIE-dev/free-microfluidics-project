## Terumo TSCD-Q Sterile Tubing Welder (2018-09)

- **id**: `terumo-tscd-q-tubing-welder`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Terumo BCT, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Terumo TSCD-Q product launch 2018-09; product datasheet rev 2024; predecessor TSCD II launched ~2005
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a sterile tubing welder using a heated copper wafer to simultaneously cut and thermal-fuse two PVC tubing ends, maintaining the sterile fluid path through the weld operation. Anticipates: sterile-tubing-welder architectures for biopharma single-use manufacturing where two pre-sterilized fluid paths are connected without aseptic-isolator manipulation; specifically the architectural pattern of disposable copper wafer per weld eliminating wafer-to-wafer cross-contamination. Critical to CGT, mRNA, and vaccine manufacturing where dozens of weld operations connect single-use components into the process train. Competes with Cytiva BioWelder TC and Sartorius BioWelder.
