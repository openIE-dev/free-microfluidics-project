## Marangoni 1865 — On the expansion of a droplet of liquid floating on the surface of another liquid (surface-tension-driven flow) (1865)

- **id**: `marangoni-1865-surface-tension-flow`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Carlo Giuseppe Matteo Marangoni
- **disclosure**: C. Marangoni, 'Sull'espansione delle goccie d'un liquido galleggianti sulla superfície di altro liquido', Tipografia dei Fratelli Fusi, Pavia (1865); subsequent treatment Nuovo Cim. ser. 2, vol 5/6 (1871)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: First mathematical and experimental treatment of flows induced by surface-tension gradients (Marangoni effect / Marangoni convection). Foundational for: thermocapillary microfluidic actuation, surfactant-driven droplet motion, evaporation-driven assembly (coffee ring, tear-drop printing), and any droplet-microfluidic process where surfactants modulate interfacial flow. Anticipates: claims to 'thermocapillary microfluidic pump', 'surfactant-driven droplet steering', or 'Marangoni-driven micromixing' as their operating principle — physics is 161 years old.
