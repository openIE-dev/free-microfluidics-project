## Microfluidics Corporation Microfluidizer high-pressure homogenizer (1985-08-06)

- **id**: `microfluidics-corp-microfluidizer-high-shear-processor`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: mixer-component
- **creator**: Microfluidics Corporation (Microfluidics International / IDEX MPT)
- **disclosure**: US4533254A Apparatus for forming emulsions (Cook & Lagace, Biotechnology Development Corporation), granted 1985-08-06.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a fixed-geometry microchannel interaction chamber that divides a high-pressure feed into colliding microjets to homogenize and emulsify - the canonical industrial high-shear microfluidic processor. Anticipates broad claims to (a) sub-100 nm oil-in-water vaccine-adjuvant emulsion produced by repeated passes through a fixed-geometry microchannel chamber; (b) constant-shear scale-up by replicating chamber geometry; (c) liposome/nanoparticle size reduction by impinging-jet microfluidization. Direct prior art for any patent claiming squalene adjuvant manufacture (MF59, AS03 analogs) by microfluidization and for impinging-jet liposomal-nanoparticle process claims.
