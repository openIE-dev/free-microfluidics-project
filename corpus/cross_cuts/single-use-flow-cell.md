## Sensirion SLF3S-1300F Single-Use Liquid Flow Sensor (2019-03)

- **id**: `sensirion-slf3s-1300f-microflow`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Sensirion AG
- **disclosure**: Sensirion SLF3S-1300F product launch 2019-03; product datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use thermoplastic flow cell with a bonded CMOS thermal-mass-flow sensor chip, providing inline liquid-flow measurement in disposable biopharma process trains. Anticipates: single-use thermal-mass-flow sensor architectures for biopharma microflow measurement; specifically the architectural pattern of CMOS thermal-flow sensor chip bonded to a disposable molded flow cell, eliminating the need for separately-sterilized reusable flow meters. Used in mRNA-LNP manufacturing for precision flow control of aqueous and ethanol streams into LNP mixers.
