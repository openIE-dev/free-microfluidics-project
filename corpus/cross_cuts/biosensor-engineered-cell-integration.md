## iGEM TU Delft–Leiden 2014 — Paper Microfluidics for Electrace Biosensor (2014-10)

- **id**: `igem-tu-delft-2014-paper-microfluidics`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: iGEM TU Delft–Leiden 2014 team (Netherlands)
- **disclosure**: iGEM TU Delft-Leiden 2014 wiki https://2014.igem.org/Team:TU_Delft-Leiden/Project/Microfluidics (giga-jamboree timestamp 2014-10-30)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: iGEM team wikis are time-stamped public defensive disclosures published worldwide on jamboree dates, qualifying as printed publications under 102. This entry discloses a wax-printed Whatman paper device whose channels are designed to deliver and assay analytes against live Electrace biosensor strains. Element-by-element: (1) hydrophobic wax-printed channel patterning on Whatman cellulose chromatography paper; (2) multi-layer paper stack with controlled inter-layer flow; (3) reagent zones designed for engineered E. coli biosensor strains; (4) lateral-flow readout zone. Anticipates: claims directed to (a) integrated cellular biosensors on paper microfluidic substrates, (b) wax-printed multi-layer paper devices used for synthetic-biology readout, (c) the architectural pattern of paper microfluidic channels coupled with live engineered cells.
