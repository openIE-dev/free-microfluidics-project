## Korsch XL 200 / XL 800 Rotary Tablet Press (2008)

- **id**: `korsch-xl-rotary-tablet-press`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Korsch AG (Berlin, Germany)
- **disclosure**: Korsch AG product catalog 2008; Korsch XL 100/200/400/800 series technical brochure 2010-2018
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Korsch XL series rotary tablet press, the de facto industry-standard machine for continuous-manufacturing tableting lines. Anticipates: (a) integration of per-station piezoelectric force feedback in high-speed rotary presses; (b) PAT-port integration on the die table at the press outlet for NIR/Raman content-uniformity; (c) the modular die-table cassette for fast formulation changeover compatible with continuous-manufacturing flexibility requirements. Cite against later patents claiming high-speed rotary presses with per-station piezo force feedback and PAT outlet ports.
