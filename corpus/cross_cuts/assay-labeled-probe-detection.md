## Southern blot (DNA transfer and hybridization) (1975)

- **id**: `southern-1975-blot`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Edwin Southern (University of Edinburgh)
- **disclosure**: Southern, E. M. Detection of specific sequences among DNA fragments separated by gel electrophoresis. J. Mol. Biol. 1975, 98, 503-517. DOI: 10.1016/S0022-2836(75)80083-0
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: The original nucleic-acid transfer-and-hybridize protocol. Discloses capillary-driven transfer of biomolecules from a separation gel onto a binding membrane followed by probe hybridization for sequence-specific detection. Anticipates: membrane-immobilized target capture, labeled-probe hybridization detection, and (conceptually) the spotted DNA microarray. Microfluidic hybridization chambers, on-chip blotting devices, and integrated electrophoresis-plus-detection cartridges descend from this method. Eponymously named; the RNA, protein, and other variants are deliberately named after compass directions in homage.
