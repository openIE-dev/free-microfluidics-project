## IDEXX Colilert / Colilert-18 Reagent Snap-Pack (1989)

- **id**: `idexx-colilert-reagent-snap-pack`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: IDEXX Laboratories, Inc.
- **disclosure**: IDEXX Colilert reagent EPA approval 1989; Colilert-18 datasheet; US patent 4,925,789 (defined-substrate enzyme assay for coliforms and E. coli)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the canonical IDEXX defined-substrate water-quality reagent format: a sealed foil snap-pack of lyophilized MMO-MUG selective medium that is dissolved into a 100 mL water sample, distributed into a Quanti-Tray, sealed and incubated. Color and fluorescence after 18-24 h indicate coliform and E. coli presence. Anticipates: lyophilized defined-substrate reagents packaged as snap-pack consumables for water-quality testing; combined chromogenic + fluorogenic dual-target enzyme detection in a single reagent; and EPA-method-aligned snap-pack architectures distinct from but co-engineered with the Quanti-Tray microfluidic substrate.

## IDEXX Quanti-Tray and Quanti-Tray/2000 (1996)

- **id**: `idexx-quanti-tray-mpn-water`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: IDEXX Laboratories, Inc.
- **disclosure**: IDEXX Quanti-Tray patent issued 1996; IDEXX Quanti-Tray/2000 datasheet; US patent 5,518,892 (sealed-tray MPN format)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a foil-laminate tray with hydrophobic well dividers that partitions a 100 mL water sample into 49 (or 49+48) wells of two distinct volumes, after which the tray is heat-sealed and incubated. Combined with chromogenic/fluorogenic enzyme substrate reagents (Colilert), positive wells indicate target organisms; combinatorial well counts yield MPN. Anticipates: foil-laminate sealed-tray most-probable-number quantitation as the dominant water-quality microfluidic primitive; heat-seal closure of distributed sample compartments to prevent cross-contamination; and pour-and-seal architectures that approximate Poisson partitioning without active fluidics.

## Charm Sciences PALCAM Listeria Rapid Test (2005)

- **id**: `charm-palcam-listeria-rapid`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Charm Sciences, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Charm Sciences PALCAM rapid test product datasheet 2005; AOAC RI validation reports
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a strip-format selective enrichment cartridge with esculin-based chromogenic indicator chemistry for Listeria, designed as a one-step swab-to-result format for food-plant environmental monitoring. Anticipates: combined enrichment + chromogenic detection in a single sealed pouch; environmental swab cartridges that integrate sample collection, enrichment, and visual detection without intermediate pipetting; and the architectural pattern of selective-broth-impregnated substrates in food-environment surveillance.
