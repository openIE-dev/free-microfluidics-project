## La Calhène DPTE double-port aseptic transfer system (1962)

- **id**: `la-calhene-dpte-double-port`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Getinge La Calhène (formerly La Calhène)
- **disclosure**: La Calhène (now Getinge La Calhène) DPTE introduction circa 1962 for nuclear glove-box transfer; FR2257326A1 (1974); https://www.getingelacalhene.com/products/dpte/.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the foundational alpha-beta double-port aseptic transfer system: a fixed alpha port on an isolator wall and a removable beta port on a transfer container, with bayonet-coupling that captures both door faces against each other and rotates them as a single sealed unit so that the previously-exposed external surfaces are isolated inside the joint after coupling. Anticipates: essentially every modern closed-system sterile transfer port (RTP, beta-bag, charge-bag) used in CGT, vaccine, mRNA, and aseptic-fill operations. Foundational prior art for: alpha-beta double-door coupling geometry; bayonet rotation as a means of capturing exterior surfaces between mated doors; flexible-bag transfer containers using the same port; and pharma-grade adoption of nuclear-industry biocontainment ports. ANY claim broadly directed to alpha-beta double-port aseptic transfer is anticipated by the 1960s-1970s La Calhène DPTE prior art.
