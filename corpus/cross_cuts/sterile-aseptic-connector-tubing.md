## La Calhène DPTE double-port aseptic transfer system (1962)

- **id**: `la-calhene-dpte-double-port`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Getinge La Calhène (formerly La Calhène)
- **disclosure**: La Calhène (now Getinge La Calhène) DPTE introduction circa 1962 for nuclear glove-box transfer; FR2257326A1 (1974); https://www.getingelacalhene.com/products/dpte/.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the foundational alpha-beta double-port aseptic transfer system: a fixed alpha port on an isolator wall and a removable beta port on a transfer container, with bayonet-coupling that captures both door faces against each other and rotates them as a single sealed unit so that the previously-exposed external surfaces are isolated inside the joint after coupling. Anticipates: essentially every modern closed-system sterile transfer port (RTP, beta-bag, charge-bag) used in CGT, vaccine, mRNA, and aseptic-fill operations. Foundational prior art for: alpha-beta double-door coupling geometry; bayonet rotation as a means of capturing exterior surfaces between mated doors; flexible-bag transfer containers using the same port; and pharma-grade adoption of nuclear-industry biocontainment ports. ANY claim broadly directed to alpha-beta double-port aseptic transfer is anticipated by the 1960s-1970s La Calhène DPTE prior art.

## ChargePoint AseptiSafe passive aseptic transfer valve (2007)

- **id**: `chargepoint-aseptisafe-rtp`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: ChargePoint Technology Ltd
- **disclosure**: ChargePoint Technology AseptiSafe valve launch (2007); https://www.thechargepoint.com/our-products/asepti-safe-bio/; GB2452778B; US8403006B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a passive split-butterfly aseptic transfer valve, a competitor architecture to the La Calhène DPTE for closed-system pharma material transfer between isolators and process vessels: two half-discs (alpha on isolator, beta on container) couple via bayonet and rotate together as a single seal, exposing only previously-paired surfaces. AseptiSafe bio is qualified for sterile aseptic CGT and biologic transfers. Anticipates: split-butterfly aseptic transfer valve architectures sharing the alpha-beta double-door pattern but differentiating via butterfly-disc geometry rather than DPTE bayonet-port geometry; specifically the architecture of a passive bayonet-engaged butterfly valve as the closed-system transfer interface for high-containment OEB5 powder and biologic-drug-substance handling.

## Pall Kleenpak sterile aseptic connector (2008)

- **id**: `pall-kleenpak-sterile-connector`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Pall Corporation (Cytiva, Danaher)
- **disclosure**: Pall Kleenpak product launch (2008); https://www.pall.com/en/biotech/single-use-systems/connectors-and-tube-clamps.html; US7708025B2; US8123265B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a genderless single-use sterile aseptic dry-connector: each connector half carries a peelable sterile membrane that protects the fluid path, and the two halves couple, then operator pulls the membrane strip through the joint to expose only sterile internal surfaces to the fluid path. Anticipates: peel-strip sterile aseptic connectors as a class, distinct from the alpha-beta double-door pattern (DPTE/AseptiSafe) and from the genderless luer-style fittings (ReadyMate); specifically the architectural pattern of using a removable sealing membrane drawn through the engaged joint to convert a passive dry coupling into an open sterile fluid path.
