## Agilent Femto Pulse high-sensitivity capillary electrophoresis (2015)

- **id**: `agilent-femto-pulse`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Agilent Technologies (acquired Advanced Analytical Technologies Inc.)
- **disclosure**: Advanced Analytical Femto Pulse launch (2015); acquired by Agilent (2018); https://www.agilent.com/en/product/automated-electrophoresis/femto-pulse-system; US9476846B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a pulsed-field capillary electrophoresis platform using a 12-capillary fused-silica array with intercalating-dye fluorescence detection, achieving 5-fg/µL double-stranded-DNA sensitivity and 250 kb fragment separation range. Anticipates: high-sensitivity capillary electrophoresis platforms for AAV genome-integrity assessment, low-input mRNA QC, and high-molecular-weight DNA characterization; specifically the architectural pattern of pulsed-field switching applied to fused-silica capillary arrays as an alternative to slab-gel pulsed-field electrophoresis or microfluidic-chip CE for very-large or very-low-quantity nucleic acids relevant to CGT manufacturing release testing.
