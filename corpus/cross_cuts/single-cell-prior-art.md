## 10x Genomics vs Parse Biosciences Patent Litigation (2023-08)

- **id**: `tenx-vs-parse-biosciences-litigation`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: 10x Genomics / Parse Biosciences
- **disclosure**: 10x Genomics, Inc. v. Parse Biosciences, Inc., U.S. District Court for the District of Delaware, Case No. 1:23-cv-00930 (filed 2023-08-23); related counterclaims and IPR petitions
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Records the August 2023 D. Del. infringement action in which 10x Genomics asserted multiple GEM/Drop-seq-lineage patents against Parse's Evercode split-pool products. Material because: (a) the case raises the question whether instrument-free combinatorial indexing infringes patents directed to compartmentalized droplet-based single-cell workflows; (b) Parse's defenses and counterclaims drew on Rosenberg 2018 SPLiT-seq, Cao et al. 2017 sci-RNA-seq, and prior University of Washington academic disclosures as 102 prior art against 10x's claims; (c) the docket exposed claim-construction theory pertinent to whether 'partition' must be a physical droplet or can be a fixed cell. Useful for invalidity contentions and as evidence of state of the art at relevant priority dates.
