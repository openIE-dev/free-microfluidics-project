## Quake & Scherer 2000 — From micro- to nanofabrication with soft materials (2000-11-24)

- **id**: `quake-scherer-2000-from-micro-to-nano-soft-materials`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Stephen R. Quake, Axel Scherer
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1126/science.290.5496.1536; Science 290(5496) 1536-1540 (2000)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Science perspective laying out the case that soft-lithography in PDMS scales from micron to sub-100-nm features and is the dominant fabrication path for microfluidics, optoelectronics, and biotechnology. Disclosed before the Quake-group monolithic-membrane-valve and large-scale-integration patents matured into commercial Fluidigm products. Anticipates: genus claims to 'an elastomeric microfluidic device fabricated by replica molding from a master patterned by photolithography' and to 'multi-layer soft-lithography devices integrating control and flow layers' at the abstract architectural level. Particularly load-bearing because it timestamps the multi-layer-PDMS architecture in the Science archival record, independent of the Unger 2000 valve paper of the same year.

## Beebe, Mensing & Walker 2002 — Physics and applications of microfluidics in biology (2002-08)

- **id**: `beebe-mensing-walker-2002-physics-applications-review`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: David J. Beebe, Glennys A. Mensing, Glenn M. Walker
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1146/annurev.bioeng.4.112601.125916; Annu. Rev. Biomed. Eng. 4, 261-286 (2002)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Comprehensive Annual Reviews chapter cataloguing the physics governing microfluidics for biology: laminar flow, diffusional mixing, electrokinetic phenomena, capillarity, two-phase flow, and surface chemistry; with applied examples in cell handling, immunoassays, PCR, and DNA analysis. By 2002 disclosure of these as integrated 'microfluidic platforms for biological assay' Anticipates: any later patent claim that recites a generic 'microfluidic device for performing a biological assay comprising laminar flow channels, mixers, and detection regions'. Specifically anticipates 'soft-lithography PDMS device for cell-based assay' and 'electroosmotically driven biochemical analysis' as obvious combinations. Very high-density bibliographic entry; cited 1000+ times in subsequent prior-art landscapes.

## Qin, Xia & Whitesides 2010 / Nature Reviews Materials 2017 — soft lithography 20-year retrospective (2010-03-04)

- **id**: `nature-reviews-materials-2017-soft-litho-anniversary`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Dong Qin, Younan Xia, George M. Whitesides
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1038/nprot.2009.234; Nat. Protoc. 5, 491-502 (2010); plus Nature Reviews Materials retrospective volume 2 (2017) doi:10.1038/natrevmats.2017.16 'Three decades of microfluidics'
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Comprehensive Nature Protocols + Nature Reviews Materials retrospectives that aggregate the soft-lithography toolkit (replica molding, microcontact printing, micromolding in capillaries, solvent-assisted micromolding) into a single citable disclosure. Anticipates: any patent claiming a fabrication process consisting of 'replica-molding a PDMS device against a photolithographically defined master' as obvious in view of the reviewed methods. Standard examiner reference for soft-litho 102/103.
