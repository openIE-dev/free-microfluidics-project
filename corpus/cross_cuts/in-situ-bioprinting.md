## Advanced Solutions BioAssemblyBot 6-Axis Bioprinter (2014)

- **id**: `advanced-solutions-bioassemblybot`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Advanced Solutions Life Sciences, LLC
- **disclosure**: Advanced Solutions Life Sciences (Louisville, KY) BioAssemblyBot launch 2014; BAB-400 datasheet 2018
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a bioprinter architecture using a 6-axis articulated robot arm rather than 3-axis Cartesian gantry, allowing the printhead to approach the build target from any angle. Anticipates: 6-axis-arm-mounted bioprinters capable of printing onto non-planar substrates including curved organ surfaces and wound beds. The architectural primitive is (6-axis articulated arm + interchangeable printhead end-effector + 8-position tool changer + non-planar deposition target) defining the in-situ bioprinting category that 3-axis Cartesian bioprinters cannot address.
