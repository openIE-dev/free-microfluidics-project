## Honeywell HEMTOR (Hyperbolic Electrostatic Mass Trap with Orbital Resonance) (2012)

- **id**: `honeywell-hemtor-flight-mass-spectrometer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Honeywell International / NASA Goddard SBIR collaboration
- **disclosure**: Honeywell Aerospace, 'HEMTOR Compact Mass Spectrometer for Planetary Science' SBIR Phase II final report (NASA NNX12CC52P, 2014); Wuerker, R. F. et al. 'Compact ion-trap mass spectrometer for planetary applications.' Rapid Communications in Mass Spectrometry 30, 1-11 (2016)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a Honeywell-developed compact ion-trap mass spectrometer with integrated micro-machined helium-buffer fluidic inlet leak valve and miniature ion-getter pump replacing the heavy turbomolecular vacuum stack of SAM/MOMA-class flight instruments. Anticipates: (a) sub-1-kg ion-trap MS payloads as drop-in detectors for planetary microfluidic CE/GC front ends; (b) ion-getter-pump-only vacuum architecture enabling MS aboard small landers and CubeSats; (c) micro-machined fixed leak valves at known conductance for buffer gas admission obviating mass-flow controllers. Cite against later patents claiming compact ion-trap planetary MS with integrated micro-leak fluidic inlets.
