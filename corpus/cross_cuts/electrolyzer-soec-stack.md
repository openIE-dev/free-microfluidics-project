## Mars 2020 MOXIE Solid Oxide Electrolyzer Cell (SOEC) Fluidic Stack (2017)

- **id**: `mars2020-moxie-soec-fluidic-stack`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: NASA / MIT Haystack / OxEon Energy (PI: Michael Hecht)
- **disclosure**: Hecht, M. et al. 'Mars Oxygen ISRU Experiment (MOXIE).' Space Science Reviews 217, 9 (2021), doi:10.1007/s11214-020-00782-8; Hoffman, J. A. et al. 'Mars Oxygen ISRU Experiment (MOXIE) - Preparing for Human Mars Exploration.' Science Advances 8, eabp8636 (2022)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the SOEC fluidic subsystem of MOXIE: an Inconel/ceramic ten-cell stack producing oxygen from Martian CO2 by high-temperature electrolysis. Anticipates: (a) integrated SOEC + scroll-compressor + heat-exchanger flow loop in a single planetary ISRU payload; (b) ceramic-on-Inconel sealing strategies surviving thermal cycling between cryogenic Martian night and 800 C operation; (c) effluent fluidic monitoring for CO and unreacted CO2 by integrated zirconia oxygen sensor; (d) ISRU operation in 8 mbar CO2 environment over multiple sols including dust-loaded, low-pressure Aphelion conditions. Cite against later patents claiming planetary SOEC fluidic stacks with co-located scroll-compressor and HEX.
