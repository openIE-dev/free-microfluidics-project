# Cross-cut: `architecture-photochemical-treatment`

## MacoPharma THERAFLEX pathogen-reduction systems (UVC platelets / MB-Plasma) (1995)

- **id**: `macopharma-theraflex-pathogen-reduction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: MacoPharma
- **disclosure**: Mohr H. et al., Photodynamic virus inactivation of blood components, Immunol Invest 24:73-85 (1995); THERAFLEX MB-Plasma in routine use from the 1990s; THERAFLEX UV-Platelets developed 2000s; MacoPharma product literature.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a methylene-blue + visible-light photodynamic plasma-treatment set with a downstream membrane filter that removes the dye and photoproducts, and a photosensitizer-free 254 nm UVC platelet-treatment set using a thin agitated illumination bag. Anticipates claims to methylene-blue plasma pathogen reduction with membrane dye removal and to photosensitizer-free UVC platelet pathogen reduction. Related to cerus-intercept-blood-system-pathogen-reduction and terumo-mirasol-prt-pathogen-reduction.

## Cerus INTERCEPT Blood System (amotosalen/UVA pathogen-reduction) (1997)

- **id**: `cerus-intercept-blood-system-pathogen-reduction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Cerus Corporation
- **disclosure**: Lin L. et al., Photochemical inactivation of viruses and bacteria in platelet concentrates by use of a novel psoralen and long-wavelength ultraviolet light, Transfusion 37:423-435 (1997); Cerus INTERCEPT Blood System; FDA approval (platelets/plasma) 2014.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a closed disposable set that photochemically inactivates pathogens in a blood product (amotosalen + UVA) and then passes the treated unit through a packed-bed adsorbent column to remove residual photoactive compound. Anticipates claims to psoralen/UVA pathogen reduction of platelets and plasma in defined-geometry illumination bags, packed-bed compound-adsorption devices for scrubbing photoproducts from blood, and closed-system pathogen-reduction processing sets.

## Terumo BCT Mirasol PRT System (riboflavin + UV pathogen reduction) (2006)

- **id**: `terumo-mirasol-prt-pathogen-reduction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Terumo Blood and Cell Technologies (Terumo BCT)
- **disclosure**: Goodrich R.P. et al., The Mirasol PRT system for pathogen reduction of platelets and plasma: an overview, Transfus Apher Sci 35:5-17 (2006); Terumo BCT (formerly Navigant/Gambro BCT); CE mark 2007.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a closed disposable set that pathogen-reduces a blood product by adding riboflavin and exposing the unit, agitated as a thin layer in a UV-transparent bag, to broad UV light with no post-treatment removal step. Anticipates claims to riboflavin/UV pathogen-reduction of platelets, plasma and whole blood, to thin-film agitated illumination geometries for uniform photon dosing, and to no-removal photosensitizer pathogen-reduction processing sets. Related to cerus-intercept-blood-system-pathogen-reduction.
