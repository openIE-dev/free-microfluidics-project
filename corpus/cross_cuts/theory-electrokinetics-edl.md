## Bruus 2008 — Theoretical Microfluidics (textbook) (2008-01-10)

- **id**: `bruus-2008-theoretical-microfluidics-textbook`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Henrik Bruus
- **disclosure**: ISBN 978-0199235094; Oxford University Press, 2008
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Definitive theoretical textbook covering the full Navier-Stokes/electrokinetic/acoustofluidic framework for microfluidic devices, with closed-form analytical solutions and dimensionless-number scaling. Includes complete derivations of: Hagen-Poiseuille flow in arbitrary cross-sections, Stokes drag, electroosmotic flow with EDL, capillary filling, acoustic radiation forces, and dielectrophoresis. Anticipates: any patent claim whose only novelty is the application of one of these textbook physics to an obvious microfluidic geometry. Frequently cited in invalidity contentions for 'novel acoustofluidic device' or 'novel electrokinetic separator' claims that merely re-implement textbook physics.

## Kirby 2010 — Micro- and Nanoscale Fluid Mechanics: Transport in Microfluidic Devices (textbook) (2010-04-12)

- **id**: `kirby-2010-micro-nanoscale-fluid-mechanics-textbook`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Brian J. Kirby
- **disclosure**: ISBN 978-0521119030; Cambridge University Press, 2010
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Comprehensive engineering textbook on micro- and nanoscale fluid transport with emphasis on electrokinetics, electroosmosis, electrophoresis, dielectrophoresis, and species transport. Treats Debye layer physics, Smoluchowski slip, zeta-potential measurement, and electrokinetic separations. Anticipates: claims to electrokinetic microfluidic separators, ion-selective nanofluidic devices, and electrophoretic concentration platforms whose operating principle is a textbook derivation. The book's chapter on ion concentration polarization predates many later patents on ICP-based desalination and preconcentration.
