## Mars 2020 MEDA (Mars Environmental Dynamics Analyzer) Fluidic Dust Filter (2020)

- **id**: `mars2020-meda-fluidic-dust-filter`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Centro de Astrobiologia (CAB-INTA-CSIC) / NASA JPL (PI: Jose Antonio Rodriguez-Manfredi)
- **disclosure**: Rodriguez-Manfredi, J. A. et al. 'The Mars Environmental Dynamics Analyzer, MEDA. A Suite of Environmental Sensors for the Mars 2020 Mission.' Space Science Reviews 217, 48 (2021), doi:10.1007/s11214-021-00816-9; INTA-CAB MEDA payload technical documentation 2018-2020
- **ip status**: unknown
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the MEDA atmospheric inlet and dust-filtration subsystem aboard Perseverance: a multi-stage fluidic architecture combining a cyclonic pre-separator, baffled labyrinth, and hydrophobic PTFE membrane that protects ECMWF-class meteorological sensors from Mars dust loading without active actuation. Anticipates: (a) passive multi-stage cyclone-plus-membrane dust filtration as a Mars-rover sensor-protection front-end; (b) thermally controlled inlet ducts that prevent CO2 frost in cold Mars night operations; (c) the architectural trade preferring passive filtration over active brushes/wipers on multi-year planetary meteorology stations. Cite against later patents claiming Mars-rated cyclone-plus-hydrophobic-membrane dust filters.
