## Mars Express SPICAM UV/IR Atmospheric Spectrometer (2003)

- **id**: `mars-express-spicam-uv-ir-spectrometer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Service d'Aeronomie / IKI / ESA (PI: Jean-Loup Bertaux)
- **disclosure**: Bertaux, J.-L. et al. 'SPICAM on Mars Express: Observing modes and overview of UV spectrometer data and scientific results.' Journal of Geophysical Research 111, E10S90 (2006), doi:10.1029/2006JE002690; ESA SP-1291 Mars Express mission documentation
- **ip status**: unknown
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the SPICAM payload aboard Mars Express, the precursor to NOMAD/ACS architectures, with vacuum-sealed UV+IR spectrometer optical bench and integrated UV calibration lamp. Anticipates: (a) integrated UV+near-IR planetary atmospheric spectrometer in a single co-aligned payload; (b) sealed UV pen-ray calibration lamp used cyclically inside an evacuated optical bench across multi-year mission; (c) operational template (occultation/nadir/limb modes) later inherited by NOMAD and ACS. Cite against later patent claims for combined UV+NIR planetary spectrometer benches with internal pen-ray calibration.
