## Eppendorf DASGIP Parallel Bioreactor System (2009)

- **id**: `eppendorf-dasgip-parallel-bioreactor`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Eppendorf AG (formerly DASGIP AG, Germany)
- **disclosure**: Eppendorf DASGIP Parallel Bioreactor system product brochure 2009-2018 (DASGIP acquired by Eppendorf 2012); Lattermann, C. and Buchs, J. 'Microscale and miniscale fermentation and screening.' Current Opinion in Biotechnology 35, 1-6 (2015)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the DASGIP (now Eppendorf) parallel bioreactor architecture: 4/8/16-fold cabinets each providing independent control of pH/DO/temperature/agitation/gas-mix per vessel, with vessels ranging from 100 mL benchtop to 2.5 L pilot. Anticipates: (a) modular-cabinet parallel bioreactor architecture allowing field-upgrade from 4 to 8 to 16 vessels by adding cabinets; (b) per-vessel independent gas-mix and feed control as the DoE-enabling foundational architecture; (c) the DASGIP control software stack (DASware) as the precursor template for vendor-agnostic parallel-bioreactor data acquisition. Cite against later patents claiming modular-cabinet parallel bioreactor systems with per-vessel independent control.
