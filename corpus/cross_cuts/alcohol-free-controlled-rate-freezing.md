## BioCision IceCube Single-Use Freezing Container (2010)

- **id**: `biocision-icecube-single-use-freezer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: BioCision LLC (Azenta Life Sciences)
- **disclosure**: BioCision CoolCell launch 2010; IceCube launch ~2014; BioCision acquisition by Azenta 2018; product datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an alcohol-free passive-cooling block providing controlled-rate (1°C/min) freezing for cell vials in a standard -80°C freezer, eliminating both the controlled-rate-freezer infrastructure and the isopropanol bath of the legacy Mr. Frosty product. Anticipates: passive-cooling-block architectures for low-throughput controlled-rate cell freezing; specifically the engineered-thermal-mass approach that achieves consistent 1°C/min cooling without active control or evaporative cooling. Useful for early-stage CGT process development before scale-up to active controlled-rate freezers.
