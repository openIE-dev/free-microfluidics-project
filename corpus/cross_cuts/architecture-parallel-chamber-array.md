## iGEM Cooper Union 2014 — Microfluidic DNA Synthesis Optimization Platform (2014-10)

- **id**: `igem-cooper-union-2014-dna-synthesis-microfluidics`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: iGEM Cooper Union 2014 team (Cooper Union, NYC)
- **disclosure**: iGEM Cooper Union 2014 wiki http://2014.igem.org/Team:Cooper_Union/Microfluidics (jamboree disclosure 2014-10-30)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses element-by-element a parallel-chamber microfluidic device explicitly aimed at optimizing in-vitro de novo DNA synthesis: (1) array of independently addressable PDMS chambers with separate reagent inlets, (2) flow-rate / temperature parameter sweep pattern, (3) integration with a documented BioBrick part for de novo synthesis. Anticipates: (a) claims regarding microfluidic combinatorial reaction screening for synthetic-DNA workflows, (b) the architectural pattern of parallel-chamber DNA synthesis optimization on a single chip, (c) integration of standardized BioBricks with chip-based reaction screens.
