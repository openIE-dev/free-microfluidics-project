## Tony Jun Huang Lab Tunable Acoustic Tweezers (2014-12)

- **id**: `huang-penn-state-2014-tsaw-acoustic-tweezers`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Tony Jun Huang group (Penn State, later Duke)
- **disclosure**: Ding X., Peng Z., Lin S.-C. S., et al. (Huang lab), PNAS 111(36):12992-12997 (2014), doi:10.1073/pnas.1413325111
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses tilted-angle standing surface acoustic wave (taSSAW) chip wherein two opposing IDTs generate a standing wave at an angle to the flow direction; particles experience a pressure-gradient force that translates them across streamlines as a function of size and compressibility. Anticipates: (i) SSAW-based label-free cell sorters with continuous outflow; (ii) acoustic separators tuned by transducer angle rather than frequency; (iii) integration of taSSAW with downstream sequencing inputs (e.g., CTC enrichment). Companion to Ding 2012 acoustic-tweezer entry but adds productizable separation geometry.
