## Buehner / Hill 1977 IBM Thermal Inkjet Research Disclosure (1977)

- **id**: `buehner-hill-1977-ibm-thermal-inkjet-research`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: W.G. Buehner, J.D. Hill, T.H. Williams, J.W. Woods (IBM Research)
- **disclosure**: W.G. Buehner, J.D. Hill, T.H. Williams, J.W. Woods, 'Application of Ink Jet Technology to a Word Processing Output Printer', IBM Journal of Research and Development, 21(1):2-9, January 1977, doi:10.1147/rd.211.0002
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Academic anchor: IBM's 1977 IBM J. R&D paper by Buehner, Hill, Williams, Woods is one of the earliest comprehensive published comparisons of inkjet drop-ejection mechanisms (thermal, piezo, electrostatic) for office output applications. The paper is foundational prior art for office inkjet because it explicitly compared the candidate ejection mechanisms and characterized their tradeoffs (drop volume, frequency, reliability, cost). Anticipates: design-of-experiments approaches to inkjet printhead architecture, including multi-mechanism comparison frameworks. The architectural primitive disclosed is the systematic enumeration of the (mechanism x nozzle-density x frequency x drop-volume) design space for office inkjet, predating commercial product by over a decade. Useful as foundational academic prior art that pre-establishes obviousness of mechanism selection.
