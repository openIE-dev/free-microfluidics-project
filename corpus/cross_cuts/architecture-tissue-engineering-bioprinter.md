## T&R Biofab IB3D Bioprinter Printhead (2018)

- **id**: `tnr-biofab-ib3d-bioprinter-head`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: T&R Biofab Co., Ltd.
- **disclosure**: T&R Biofab Co., Ltd. (Korea) IB3D launch 2018; T&R Biofab IB3D printhead datasheet 2019
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Korean bioprinter platform extending wave 1 entry. Architectural primitives include: (1) multi-modal printhead combining pneumatic bioink extrusion with screw-driven thermoplastic extrusion, allowing simultaneous deposition of soft cell-laden hydrogel and stiff PCL scaffold; (2) heated build plate maintaining 37C for cell viability during multi-hour print. Anticipates: bioprinters combining thermoplastic scaffold extrusion with bioink extrusion in a single registered build, with active build-plate temperature control for cell viability.
