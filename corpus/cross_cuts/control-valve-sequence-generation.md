## Fluigi — Microfluidic Device Synthesis CAD (Densmore lab) (2014-12)

- **id**: `huang-densmore-2014-fluigi-microfluidic-cad`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Haiyao Huang, Douglas Densmore (Boston University CIDAR Lab)
- **disclosure**: Huang H, Densmore D. Fluigi: Microfluidic Device Synthesis for Synthetic Biology. ACM Journal on Emerging Technologies in Computing Systems 11(3): article 26 (2014). doi:10.1145/2660773
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a complete microfluidic CAD synthesis pipeline analogous to electronic design automation. Element-by-element: (1) a netlist representation for microfluidic components with channels, valves, mixers, chambers, and ports; (2) placement and routing algorithms tailored to Quake-style MLSI chips; (3) automatic generation of valve actuation sequences for compiled functional behaviour; (4) simulation hooks for cellular genetic circuits compartmentalized on-chip. Anticipates: (a) software claims directed to automatic microfluidic chip layout from high-level descriptions; (b) compilation of valve-actuation control sequences from a behavioural specification; (c) the architectural pattern of EDA-style place-and-route applied to flow-and-control layer microfluidics. Implementations are publicly hosted (BU-EC552/Fluigi GitHub).
