## Walsworth Lab NV-Diamond Wide-Field Magnetic Imaging Microscope (2013-04-19)

- **id**: `walsworth-nv-magnetometer`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: nanofluidic-chip
- **creator**: Walsworth Lab (Harvard, then University of Maryland)
- **disclosure**: Le Sage et al. (Walsworth lab), Nature 2013, 496, 486-489. DOI:10.1038/nature12072 (NV-diamond magnetic imaging of magnetotactic bacteria); Glenn et al., Geochem. Geophys. Geosyst. 2017, 18, 3254-3267.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: NV-center diamond wide-field magnetic imaging microscope. Microfluidic-relevant content: the diamond substrate is overlaid with a PDMS microfluidic sample chamber holding live cells or sample fluid above the NV-implanted surface; the proximity (~10 nm) of NV centers to the sample provides single-bacterium magnetic resolution. Discloses: surface-implanted NV-center array on CVD diamond at controlled depth; PDMS microfluidic chamber for live-cell or geological-sample loading directly on the diamond surface; wide-field optical readout with frequency-modulated microwave manipulation; sub-uT field sensitivity at sub-um spatial resolution. Anticipates: NV-diamond-microfluidic-chamber hybrid devices for magnetic imaging of biological systems; the architectural pattern of diamond-as-sensor-substrate + PDMS-microfluidic-as-sample-cell that has dominated the magnetic-imaging-of-cells subfield.
