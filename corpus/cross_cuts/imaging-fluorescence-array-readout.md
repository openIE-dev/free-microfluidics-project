## Quanterix Simoa HD-X Platform (2017)

- **id**: `quanterix-simoa-hd-x-platform`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Quanterix Corporation
- **disclosure**: Quanterix Corp Simoa HD-X launch 2017; Rissin D. M. et al. Nature Biotechnology 28:595 (2010), doi:10.1038/nbt.1641; US patents 8,222,047 and 8,236,574
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Quanterix Simoa HD-X discloses a productized single-molecule array system in which paramagnetic beads coated with capture antibodies bind <1 target molecule each, are loaded into femtoliter microwells (etched in a fiber-optic bundle), sealed by an immiscible oil overlay, and read out optically as digital on/off well counts. Anticipates: (i) digital ELISA microwell-array platforms generally; (ii) femtoliter microwell formation by acid etching of fiber-optic bundles; (iii) oil-sealed microreactor arrays for single-molecule fluorogenic assays; (iv) the HD-X automated cartridge workflow. Builds on rissin-2010-quanterix-simoa academic ancestor entry but adds productized HD-X cartridge and instrument architecture.
