# Cross-cut: `separation-magnetic-bead`

## Luminex xMAP bead-multiplex platform (Luminex 100/200, FLEXMAP 3D, MAGPIX) (1997)

- **id**: `luminex-xmap-bead-multiplex-platform`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Luminex Corporation (now part of DiaSorin)
- **disclosure**: Fulton R.J. et al., Advanced multiplexed analysis with the FlowMetrix system, Clin Chem 43:1749-1756 (1997); Luminex Corporation xMAP technology; foundational patent Chandler et al. US5736330.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses spectrally-barcoded microspheres each bearing a distinct capture probe, read by single-file sheath-flow laser interrogation (or CCD imaging of settled magnetic beads) to multiplex up to 500 analytes per well. Anticipates broad claims to fluorescence-coded bead multiplex assays, sheath-flow optical bead classification, magnetic-bead settling-and-imaging readout, and multiplexed immunoassay/nucleic-acid panels on coded microspheres. Foundational prior art for bio-rad-bioplex-2200-multiplex and bio-techne-ella-simple-plex (and many others).

## Werfen / Diagast QWALYS (Erythrocyte Magnetized Technology blood grouping) (2003)

- **id**: `werfen-diagast-qwalys-emt-blood-grouping`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Diagast (Werfen group)
- **disclosure**: Diagast / Werfen QWALYS system literature; Erythrocyte Magnetized Technology (EMT) introduced by Diagast in the early 2000s; founding patents on magnetized red cells.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses immunoagglutination assays in which red cells are coated with paramagnetic ferrofluid so a magnetic field, not centrifugation, sediments them and reveals agglutination by sedimentation-pattern morphology, automated on an imaging analyzer. Anticipates claims to magnetic-particle-mediated red-cell sedimentation blood grouping and to centrifuge-free immunohematology automation. Magnetic-separation alternative to diamed-id-system-gel-card.

## Bio-Rad BioPlex 2200 multiplex immunoassay analyzer (2004)

- **id**: `bio-rad-bioplex-2200-multiplex`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Bio-Rad Laboratories
- **disclosure**: Bio-Rad BioPlex 2200 System operator manual; FDA clearance 2004 onward (ANA Screen, ToRC, syphilis, vasculitis, celiac panels); built on Luminex xMAP technology.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an automated random-access analyzer that performs magnetic-bead xMAP multiplex immunoassays - bead capture, magnetic wash, reporter addition, single-file flow-cytometric readout - in disposable vessels with continuous bead/reagent fluidics. Anticipates claims to automated magnetic-bead multiplex serology analyzers and to consolidated multi-analyte autoimmune/infectious panels from one aspiration. Descends from luminex-xmap-bead-multiplex-platform.

## Grifols Procleix Panther NAT blood-donor screening system (2014)

- **id**: `grifols-procleix-panther-nat-blood-screening`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Grifols Diagnostic Solutions (assay) / Hologic (Panther instrument)
- **disclosure**: Grifols / Hologic Procleix Panther System operator manual; CE mark 2014, FDA approval of Procleix Ultrio Elite assay on Panther 2015-2017.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a random-access blood-donor screening platform that performs magnetic-bead target capture, isothermal transcription-mediated amplification, and real-time chemiluminescent hybridization-protection detection in sealed single-use vessels on a continuous-load track. Anticipates claims to integrated TMA-based NAT blood screening, dual-kinetic chemiluminescent readout, and consolidated random-access infectious-disease screening of plasma. Related to hologic-panther-fusion-cartridge (same instrument family).

## Abbott Alinity s blood-donor screening system (2018)

- **id**: `abbott-alinity-s-blood-screening`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Abbott Laboratories (Diagnostics Division)
- **disclosure**: Abbott Alinity s System operator manual; CE mark 2018; Abbott Diagnostics Alinity family product literature.
- **ip status**: patented

## Seer Proteograph (engineered-nanoparticle protein-corona proteomics) (2020)

- **id**: `seer-proteograph-nanoparticle-protein-corona`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Seer, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Blume J.E. et al., Rapid, deep and precise profiling of the plasma proteome with multi-nanoparticle protein corona, Nat Commun 11:3662 (2020); Seer, Inc. Proteograph platform; commercial launch 2021.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a multi-nanoparticle protein-corona plasma-proteomics workflow in which engineered magnetic nanoparticles each form a characteristic protein corona, captured/washed/digested by an automated magnetic-bead microfluidic cartridge instrument, with peptides analyzed by LC-MS/MS. Anticipates claims to nanoparticle-corona dynamic-range compression for proteomics, panels of physicochemically distinct corona nanoparticles, and automated on-bead corona digestion cartridges. Related to olink-proximity-extension-assay-proteomics as a complementary modality.
