## Tesla 4680 Battery Cell Electrolyte Injection Microfluidic System (2020)

- **id**: `tesla-4680-electrolyte-injection`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: dispenser-pipettor
- **creator**: Tesla Inc.
- **disclosure**: Tesla Inc Battery Day presentation (September 2020); US20210320375A1 'Methods of and systems for filling battery cells'
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a vacuum-assisted electrolyte injection system for the 4680 tab-less cylindrical battery cell, using a microfluidic needle interface to dispense calibrated electrolyte volumes through a small fill port under controlled atmosphere. Anticipates: (a) vacuum-assist + microfluidic-needle electrolyte fill for high-energy cells; (b) the architectural pattern of pressure-cycled microfluidic dispense for battery manufacturing; (c) tab-less large-format cell electrolyte filling protocol. Patent and Battery Day anchors.
