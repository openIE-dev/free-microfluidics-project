## Applied Biosystems 3500xL Genetic Analyzer (CE Capillary Array) (2010)

- **id**: `thermofisher-3500xl-genetic-analyzer-ce`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Thermo Fisher Scientific (Applied Biosystems)
- **disclosure**: Applied Biosystems 3500/3500xL Genetic Analyzer product launch 2010; Thermo Fisher 3500xL user guide; US patent 5,587,128 (multi-capillary array electrophoresis)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the second-generation multi-capillary genetic analyzer architecture used as the workhorse Sanger / STR fragment-analysis platform: a 24-capillary fused-silica array with replenishable POP-7 separation polymer, electrokinetic injection from a 96-well plate, and post-column laser-induced fluorescence detection. Anticipates: capillary-array CE as the dominant forensic STR readout platform across multi-vendor STR kits; the architectural pattern of a refillable polymer + capillary array + 96-well-plate autosampler driving both Sanger and forensic workflows on shared hardware; and the multi-dye optical decomposition primitive that defines modern STR fragment analysis.
