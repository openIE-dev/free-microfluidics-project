## ASBC Method Yeast-3: Flow Cytometric Yeast Count and Viability (2010)

- **id**: `asbc-yeast-3-flow-cytometry-method`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: American Society of Brewing Chemists (ASBC)
- **disclosure**: American Society of Brewing Chemists, Methods of Analysis, Yeast-3 'Yeast Cell Count and Viability by Flow Cytometry' (approved 2010, revised 2015)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the ASBC consensus protocol for using a microfluidic flow cytometer for yeast viability assessment in breweries, normalizing sample dilution, stain selection (PI/SYTO), gating, and reportable units. Anticipates: (a) standardized microfluidic flow cytometric workflow for brewing QC, vendor-neutral; (b) consensus interoperability of microfluidic instruments through a normative method document; (c) the practice of brewery harmonization on lab-on-chip cytometry.
