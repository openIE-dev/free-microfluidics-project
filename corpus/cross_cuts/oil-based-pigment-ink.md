## RISO ComColor GD-Series Fast Cut-Sheet Inkjet (2014)

- **id**: `riso-comcolor-gd-series-fast-cut-sheet-inkjet`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: RISO Kagaku Corporation
- **disclosure**: RISO Kagaku Corporation ComColor GD9630 launch announcement 2014; ComColor GL series launch 2020; RISO ComColor GD datasheet
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a high-speed cut-sheet inkjet using a stationary line printhead bar (no scanning carriage) and a distinctive oil-based pigment ink that dries by absorption alone, eliminating the fuser stage characteristic of electrophotography and the dryer/UV characteristic of aqueous/UV inkjet. Anticipates: cut-sheet office inkjet printers using oil-based pigment ink with no thermal or UV fixing, which together produce a very low energy footprint per page. Architectural primitive: (line printhead bar + oil-based pigment ink + no-fix output) as a complete category distinct from aqueous, UV, and toner.
