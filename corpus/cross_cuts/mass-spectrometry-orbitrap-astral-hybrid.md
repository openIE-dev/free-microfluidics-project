## Thermo Fisher Orbitrap Astral mass spectrometer (single-cell proteomics) (2023)

- **id**: `thermo-orbitrap-astral-sc-proteomics`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Thermo Fisher Scientific
- **disclosure**: Thermo Fisher Orbitrap Astral launch ASMS 2023; https://www.thermofisher.com/order/catalog/product/BRE725540; US11631581B2; US10593530B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a hybrid mass-spectrometer architecture combining a high-resolution Orbitrap analyzer with a novel Asymmetric Track Lossless (Astral) analyzer: ion packets injected into the Astral execute multiple high-energy traversals of an asymmetric reflectron track, achieving high-resolution TOF mass measurements at 200 Hz MS/MS rates with ion-utilization efficiency above 70%. The architecture enables single-cell proteomics with thousands of protein IDs per cell at low input. Anticipates: Orbitrap-Astral hybrid analyzer architectures as a class for single-cell proteomics; specifically the architectural pattern of using a separate high-rate TOF analyzer downstream of an Orbitrap survey scan to maximize ion utilization in single-cell-input MS workflows; foundational prior art for claims asserting hybrid Orbitrap+TOF instrument designs targeting single-cell or low-input proteomics.
