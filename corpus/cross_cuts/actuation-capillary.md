## Alere Triage TOX Drug Screen Microfluidic Cartridge (2003)

- **id**: `alere-triage-tox-drug-screen`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Biosite Diagnostics (now Abbott)
- **disclosure**: Biosite Diagnostics (later Alere/Abbott) product datasheet 'Triage TOX Drug Screen' (2003); US6271040B1 'Diagnostic device'
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use thermoplastic urine cartridge that performs nine-class drug-of-abuse immunoassays using fluorescence-labeled particles in capillary-driven microfluidic channels, read by a benchtop Triage MeterPro. Anticipates: (a) ED-grade microfluidic drug-screen cartridge; (b) the architectural pattern of capillary microfluidic + fluorescent-particle immunoassay; (c) 15-minute clinical drug screen on a single cartridge. Strong patent anchor.

## Dräger DrugTest 5000 Oral Fluid Drug Screen Microfluidic Cartridge (2009)

- **id**: `drager-drugtest-5000-oral-fluid-cartridge`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Drägerwerk AG & Co. KGaA
- **disclosure**: Drägerwerk AG product brochure 'DrugTest 5000' (2009); US7943386B2 'Lateral flow drug test cartridge with optical reader'
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use oral-fluid drug-screen cartridge integrating saliva collection, capillary microfluidic transport, lateral-flow immunoassay strips for THC, COC, OPI, AMP, MET, METH, BZD, KET, and an optical reader providing roadside positive/negative readings. Anticipates: (a) integrated roadside oral-fluid drug screen on a single cartridge; (b) the architectural pattern of capillary-collector + multi-strip lateral flow + optical reader for forensic toxicology; (c) eight-class drug multiplex on a microfluidic cartridge. Strong primary patent and product anchor.
