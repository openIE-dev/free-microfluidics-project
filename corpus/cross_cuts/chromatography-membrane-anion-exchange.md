## Sartorius Sartobind Q Membrane Chromatography (1994)

- **id**: `sartorius-sartobind-q-membrane`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Sartorius Stedim Biotech
- **disclosure**: Sartobind membrane chromatography launch 1994 (Sartorius); product datasheets including Sartobind Q nano (2008), Sartobind STIC PA (2012), and Sartobind Rapid A (2020)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a membrane-format chromatography media where the Q (quaternary amine) ligand is grafted onto regenerated cellulose or PES membrane sheets stacked in a disposable housing, allowing convective flow-through operation rather than packed-bed diffusion-limited operation. Anticipates: single-use membrane-chromatography architectures for biopharma polishing; specifically the disposable-membrane-stack architecture eliminates column-packing, enables 10-100x higher loading flow rates, and is dominant for flow-through impurity-removal applications (host cell DNA, virus, leached Protein A). Increasingly applied to AAV polishing as alternative to bead-based Capto Q.

## Pall Mustang Q XT Membrane Chromatography Capsule (2002)

- **id**: `pall-mustang-q-xt`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Pall Corporation (Danaher Corporation)
- **disclosure**: Pall Mustang Q product launch 2002; Mustang Q XT 2014; product datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a multi-layer PES membrane stack with covalently bound quaternary-ammonium Q ligand in a disposable injection-molded capsule housing for convective flow-through chromatography. Anticipates: scalable single-use membrane chromatography architectures from research (5mL Acrodisc) through process scale (5L XT capsule) using geometrically equivalent membrane chemistry; specifically the Pall scaling family supports linear scale-up by varying capsule size while keeping membrane chemistry and bed depth constant. Direct competitor to Sartorius Sartobind in membrane chromatography for biopharma polishing. XT variant addresses the elevated-temperature sanitization needed for vaccine and CGT manufacturing.
