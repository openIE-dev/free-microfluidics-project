## IDEXX Quanti-Tray and Quanti-Tray/2000 (1996)

- **id**: `idexx-quanti-tray-mpn-water`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: IDEXX Laboratories, Inc.
- **disclosure**: IDEXX Quanti-Tray patent issued 1996; IDEXX Quanti-Tray/2000 datasheet; US patent 5,518,892 (sealed-tray MPN format)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a foil-laminate tray with hydrophobic well dividers that partitions a 100 mL water sample into 49 (or 49+48) wells of two distinct volumes, after which the tray is heat-sealed and incubated. Combined with chromogenic/fluorogenic enzyme substrate reagents (Colilert), positive wells indicate target organisms; combinatorial well counts yield MPN. Anticipates: foil-laminate sealed-tray most-probable-number quantitation as the dominant water-quality microfluidic primitive; heat-seal closure of distributed sample compartments to prevent cross-contamination; and pour-and-seal architectures that approximate Poisson partitioning without active fluidics.
