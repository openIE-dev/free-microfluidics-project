## Bruker Microflex MALDI-TOF Benchtop Mass Spectrometer (2003)

- **id**: `bruker-microflex-maldi-tof`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Bruker Daltonics
- **disclosure**: Bruker Daltonics Microflex datasheet 2003; FDA clearance for MALDI Biotyper CA System K124067 2013-11-22.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Benchtop linear MALDI-TOF that became the dominant US/EU clinical microbial-identification platform. Microfluidic-relevant content: the MALDI target plate functions as a passive picoliter-array spotting substrate, with droplet-based sample + matrix delivery (typically 1 uL spotted manually or by liquid handler) and on-plate co-crystallization. Discloses: stainless-steel polished target plates accepting 96/384/1536 spotted samples; nitrogen laser desorption ionization at 337 nm; linear TOF analyzer producing intact-protein mass spectra; Biotyper spectral matching to a clinical-microbial reference database. Anticipates: clinical-microbiology MALDI-TOF for sub-minute species ID; on-plate matrix co-crystallization as a sample interface to MS; spotted picoliter-droplet arrays as the MALDI sample format.
