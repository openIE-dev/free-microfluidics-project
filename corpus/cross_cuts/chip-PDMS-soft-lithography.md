## iGEM EPF Lausanne 2014 — BioPad Microfluidic Touch-Responsive Chip (2014-10)

- **id**: `igem-epfl-2014-microfluidic-biopad`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: iGEM EPF Lausanne 2014 team (EPFL, Switzerland)
- **disclosure**: iGEM EPF Lausanne 2014 wiki https://2014.igem.org/Team:EPF_Lausanne/Microfluidics (jamboree disclosure 2014-10-30)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Element-by-element discloses: (1) PDMS chip containing matrix of cellular chambers; (2) integrated on-chip waste loop performing chemical decontamination of effluent before exit; (3) touch-input layer mapping user interactions to chamber actuation; (4) optical readout pipeline. Anticipates: (a) claims for microfluidic chips with integrated waste-stream decontamination loops, (b) the architectural pattern of biocomputing trackpads using engineered cells in PDMS chambers, (c) touch-input bio-interface architectures.

- **creator**: iGEM Cooper Union 2014 team (Cooper Union, NYC)
- **disclosure**: iGEM Cooper Union 2014 wiki http://2014.igem.org/Team:Cooper_Union/Microfluidics (jamboree disclosure 2014-10-30)

## iGEM EPFL 2018 — CRISPR-Cas12a ctDNA Detection Microfluidic Chip (2018-10)

- **id**: `igem-epfl-2018-crispr-ctdna-microfluidics`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: iGEM EPFL 2018 team (EPF Lausanne, Switzerland)
- **disclosure**: iGEM EPFL 2018 wiki https://2018.igem.org/Team:EPFL/Notebook-Detection-ctDNA (jamboree disclosure 2018-10-25)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Element-by-element discloses a microfluidic CRISPR diagnostic targeting circulating tumor DNA: (1) on-chip PCR amplification chamber, (2) downstream Cas12a + crRNA reaction chamber containing fluorescent ssDNA reporter, (3) optical readout window measuring fluorescence increase upon collateral ssDNA cleavage. Anticipates: (a) claims directed to combined PCR + Cas12a-microfluidic ctDNA assays, (b) one-pot CRISPR + amplification cartridge workflows for cancer liquid biopsy, (c) integrated fluorescence-based collateral-cleavage readout in disposable cartridges.
