## Thermo Fisher MagMAX Magnetic Bead Extraction System (2008)

- **id**: `thermofisher-magmax-extraction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Thermo Fisher Scientific (Applied Biosystems)
- **disclosure**: Applied Biosystems MagMAX product launch (2008); MagMAX Viral/Pathogen Nucleic Acid Isolation Kit user guide MAN0018073; US patent 8,062,847
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: MagMAX discloses a silica-coated paramagnetic bead chemistry and protocol set for nucleic acid isolation, productized as kit reagents compatible with multiple automated extractors. Anticipates: (i) silica-magnetic-bead extraction chemistry productization; (ii) protocol multiplexing across KingFisher, MagMAX Express, and manual workflows; (iii) the use of magnetic-bead nucleic acid extraction at pandemic-scale clinical throughput (2020 COVID-19 ramp). Builds on boom-1990-silica-magnetic-extraction foundation.

## Promega Maxwell RSC Magnetic Bead Nucleic Acid Extractor (2014)

- **id**: `promega-maxwell-rsc-magnetic-extraction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Promega Corporation
- **disclosure**: Promega Maxwell RSC launch 2014; Maxwell RSC technical manual TM411; US patent 7,754,148
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Maxwell RSC discloses a magnetic-rod-based nucleic acid extractor in which sealed pre-filled cartridges contain reagent wells; a moving magnet array transfers paramagnetic beads (with bound nucleic acids) sequentially through wash and elution wells. Anticipates: (i) magnetic-rod-driven bead-transfer extractors with sealed cartridges; (ii) pre-filled disposable extraction cartridges with reagent traceability; (iii) clinical-grade walk-away nucleic acid prep at 48-sample throughput. Adjacent to KingFisher (entry: distinct competitor) but with sealed pre-filled cartridges rather than open plates.
