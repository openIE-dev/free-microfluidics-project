## Stirling Ultracold SU780XLE / ULT25NEU ultra-low freezer (2010)

- **id**: `stirling-ultracold-su780`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Stirling Ultracold (Global Cooling Inc.)
- **disclosure**: Stirling Ultracold (Global Cooling, Inc.) SU780XLE product page; https://stirlingultracold.com/products/su780xle/; US7028503B2; US7305838B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an upright ULT -86 °C laboratory freezer that replaces conventional cascade vapor-compression refrigeration with a free-piston Stirling-cycle cryocooler, eliminating compressor oil, HFC refrigerants, and most refrigeration-loop service requirements. The portable ULT25NEU benchtop variant deployed in COVID-19 mRNA vaccine cold-chain is the same Stirling cryocooler in a portable form factor. Anticipates: free-piston Stirling-cycle ULT freezers as a class; specifically the architectural pattern of using a closed Stirling cryocooler (no working-fluid loop, no compressor oil) for pharmaceutical -80 °C storage, including portable point-of-administration vaccine freezers; also anticipates the deployment pattern of portable -80 °C freezers for mRNA vaccine cold-chain at point-of-use.
