## Waters ACQUITY UPLC Ultra-Performance Liquid Chromatography System (2004-04-19)

- **id**: `waters-acquity-uplc`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Waters Corporation
- **disclosure**: Swartz, J. Liq. Chromatogr. Relat. Technol. 2005, 28, 1253-1263. DOI:10.1081/JLC-200053046; Waters Corporation product launch April 2004; ACQUITY UPLC operator's guide 71500031203.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Founding instrument of the UHPLC (ultra-high pressure LC) category. Discloses: precision-machined sub-2-um particle columns operating at 15,000 psi system pressures (vs 6000 psi prior HPLC); BEH particle chemistry providing the mechanical strength to withstand the ultra-high pressure; complete system including pump, autosampler, column oven, and detector all rated for the 15 kpsi pressure regime. Anticipates: the entire UHPLC category as the dominant analytical-LC architecture from 2004 onward; sub-2-um particle column chemistry; high-pressure system design for sub-minute peak widths.
