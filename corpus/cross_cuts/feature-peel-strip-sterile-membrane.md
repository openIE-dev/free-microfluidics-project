## Pall Kleenpak sterile aseptic connector (2008)

- **id**: `pall-kleenpak-sterile-connector`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Pall Corporation (Cytiva, Danaher)
- **disclosure**: Pall Kleenpak product launch (2008); https://www.pall.com/en/biotech/single-use-systems/connectors-and-tube-clamps.html; US7708025B2; US8123265B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a genderless single-use sterile aseptic dry-connector: each connector half carries a peelable sterile membrane that protects the fluid path, and the two halves couple, then operator pulls the membrane strip through the joint to expose only sterile internal surfaces to the fluid path. Anticipates: peel-strip sterile aseptic connectors as a class, distinct from the alpha-beta double-door pattern (DPTE/AseptiSafe) and from the genderless luer-style fittings (ReadyMate); specifically the architectural pattern of using a removable sealing membrane drawn through the engaged joint to convert a passive dry coupling into an open sterile fluid path.

- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Watson-Marlow Fluid Technology Group (Spirax Sarco)
- **disclosure**: Watson-Marlow Fluid Technology Group AsepticSU launch 2018; https://www.wmftg.com/product/asepticsu/; US10870056B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a genderless single-use sterile aseptic connector with peel-strip membrane (architecturally similar to Pall Kleenpak Presto) integrated with the Watson-Marlow tube-welder ecosystem to provide closed-system bag-to-bag and bag-to-skid fluid transfer paths. Anticipates: vendor-coordinated systems comprising sterile connectors plus tube welders plus bag manifolds offered as a unified single-use ecosystem; specifically the integration of peel-strip aseptic connectors with TPE tube-welder-compatible terminations as a single-vendor solution.
