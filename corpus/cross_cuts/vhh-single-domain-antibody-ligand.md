## Thermo Fisher POROS CaptureSelect AAVX Affinity Resin (2017-06)

- **id**: `thermofisher-poros-aavx`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc. (acquired Life Technologies POROS line)
- **disclosure**: Thermo Fisher POROS AAVX launch press release 2017-06; product datasheet rev 2024; US9,879,062B2
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a perfusion-bead chromatography resin with a camelid VHH single-domain antibody ligand engineered to bind a conserved AAV capsid epitope across multiple serotypes (AAV1-9), providing a single affinity-capture step for AAV gene-therapy manufacturing. Anticipates: pan-serotype AAV affinity-capture workflows in viral-vector manufacturing trains; specifically the architectural pattern of using a single VHH-based affinity column for the AAV capture step regardless of serotype, eliminating per-serotype column qualification. The perfusion-bead architecture supports the high-flow rates required for clarified-harvest loading. POROS AAVX is the dominant commercial AAV capture resin and is referenced in essentially every modern AAV-CGT process.
