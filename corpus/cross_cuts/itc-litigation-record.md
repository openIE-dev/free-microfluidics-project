## PacBio vs Oxford Nanopore Multi-Jurisdiction Litigation (US ITC + UK Patents Court) (2017)

- **id**: `pacbio-vs-ont-itc-uk-multi-jurisdiction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Pacific Biosciences / Oxford Nanopore Technologies
- **disclosure**: Pacific Biosciences v. Oxford Nanopore Technologies, U.S. International Trade Commission Investigation No. 337-TA-1057 (2017); England and Wales Patents Court parallel action; D. Del. cases
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Records the parallel ITC Section 337 investigation and UK Patents Court action between PacBio and Oxford Nanopore over nanopore sequencing methods. Material because: (a) the ITC's exclusion-order remedy raised the stakes substantially compared to district-court damages, motivating thorough public-record technical disclosures; (b) the UK Patents Court found PacBio's UK equivalents invalid for insufficiency, providing an authoritative judicial reading of nanopore-array patent scope; (c) the case exposed the 102 prior art including academic nanopore work (Deamer/Branton 1996, Akeson group early work) that may be useful as anchors for future defensive contentions. Outcome (2019-2020): exclusion order initially issued then reconsidered; UK action favored ONT.
