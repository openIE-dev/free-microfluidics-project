## Plateau 1873 — Statique expérimentale et théorique des liquides soumis aux seules forces moléculaires (1873)

- **id**: `plateau-1873-soap-films-capillarity`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Joseph Antoine Ferdinand Plateau
- **disclosure**: J.A.F. Plateau, 'Statique expérimentale et théorique des liquides soumis aux seules forces moléculaires', Gauthier-Villars, Paris (2 vol., 1873)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Foundational treatise on capillarity, soap films, minimal surfaces, and droplet/jet stability. Establishes Plateau's laws (geometry of soap-film junctions and Plateau borders) and the Plateau-Rayleigh instability criterion for jets. Foundational for: any droplet-microfluidic process exploiting jet break-up (flow-focusing, T-junction, co-flow), interfacial geometry (Plateau borders in foams), and minimal-surface microfabrication. Anticipates: claims to droplet generators that recite jet-break-up as their operating principle; the criterion is 153 years old.

## Tabeling 2005 — Introduction to Microfluidics (textbook) (2005-09-22)

- **id**: `tabeling-2005-introduction-microfluidics-textbook`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Patrick Tabeling
- **disclosure**: ISBN 978-0198568643; Oxford University Press, 2005
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Foundational graduate textbook by ESPCI's Patrick Tabeling. Systematically covers low-Re hydrodynamics, diffusion, capillarity, electrokinetics, two-phase flow, and surface chemistry as applied to microfluidic devices; with worked engineering examples. As a textbook, the engineering descriptions of the underlying physics and unit operations are unencumbered prior art (copyright attaches only to the specific text). Anticipates: any patent claim that recites a generic microfluidic device whose operation is governed by one of the textbook's worked physics — particularly electroosmotic flow, capillary filling, droplet generation by flow focusing, and pressure-driven Hagen-Poiseuille flow at micron scale. A standard examiner reference.
