- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Arkray, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Arkray Spotchem EZ SP-4430 product launch 2003; Arkray Spotchem operator's manual; US patent 5,234,665 (dry chemistry strip), US patent 6,632,675
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Arkray's flagship Spotchem dry-chemistry tray architecture: a thermoplastic tray holding up to six layered dry-reagent strips, each with spreading layer, reagent layer and indicator film, read by reflectance after capillary plasma application.
