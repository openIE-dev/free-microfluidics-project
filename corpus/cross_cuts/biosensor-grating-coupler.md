## Tiefenthaler & Lukosz IOS-1 Optical Waveguide Sensor (1989)

- **id**: `tiefenthaler-lukosz-1989-IOS-1-grating`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Kurt Tiefenthaler & Walter Lukosz (ETH Zurich; Artificial Sensing Instruments ASI)
- **disclosure**: Tiefenthaler K., Lukosz W., Journal of the Optical Society of America B 6:209 (1989), doi:10.1364/JOSAB.6.000209
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: DRAFT: Tiefenthaler & Lukosz 1989 discloses the input/output grating-coupler waveguide biosensor architecture, with TE and TM mode interrogation enabling simultaneous solution refractive index and adlayer thickness measurement, productized by ASI as IOS-1. Anticipates: (i) dual-polarization grating-coupler biosensor architectures; (ii) sol-gel high-index waveguide layers on glass for cost-effective biosensor mass production; (iii) commercial real-time biosensor instrumentation predating SPR-based BIAcore. Sister entry to lukosz-1991-grating-coupler-biosensor; lineage feed to imec-photonic-biosensor-lab-on-chip.

## Lukosz Grating-Coupler Optical Biosensor (PIC ancestor) (1991)

- **id**: `lukosz-1991-grating-coupler-biosensor`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Walter Lukosz (ETH Zurich)
- **disclosure**: Lukosz W., Biosensors and Bioelectronics 6(3):215-225 (1991), doi:10.1016/0956-5663(91)80006-J
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Lukosz 1991 discloses the integrated-optical grating coupler biosensor: a SiN waveguide on glass with input/output grating couplers covered by a microfluidic flow cell, where evanescent-field interaction with surface-bound analyte shifts the resonant coupling angle. Anticipates: (i) all subsequent grating-coupler PIC biosensors; (ii) the use of SiN as the bio-compatible PIC waveguide material; (iii) microfluidic flow cells over PIC biosensors as a standard architectural pattern. Academic ancestor of LioniX/Imec/MERIDIAN PIC biosensor commercial efforts.
