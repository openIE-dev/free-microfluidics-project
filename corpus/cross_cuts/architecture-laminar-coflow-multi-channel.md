## LUMICKS C-Trap Optical Tweezer + Microfluidic Chamber (2014)

- **id**: `lumicks-c-trap-optical-tweezer-microfluidic`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: LUMICKS BV (spinout VU Amsterdam, Wuite/Peterman groups)
- **disclosure**: LUMICKS BV product launch 2014; product page https://lumicks.com/c-trap/; Wuite group VU Amsterdam foundational publications including Forget A. L., Kowalczykowski S. C. Nature 482:423 (2012)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: LUMICKS C-Trap discloses an integrated single-molecule platform combining dual-trap optical tweezers, confocal fluorescence imaging, and a 5-channel laminar microfluidic flow cell (u-Flux) that allows the trapped molecule to be translated between distinct buffer reagent streams without mixing. Anticipates: (i) combined optical-tweezer + microfluidic-flow-cell instruments; (ii) the 5-channel laminar coflow chamber design with non-mixing reagent zones for single-molecule reaction triggering; (iii) integration of confocal fluorescence excitation/detection with optical trapping in a single sample plane. Academic foundation: Forget & Kowalczykowski 2012 Nature.
