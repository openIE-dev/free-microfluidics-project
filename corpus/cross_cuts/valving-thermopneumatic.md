## Burns, Mastrangelo et al. 1998 — An integrated nanoliter DNA analysis device (1998-10-16)

- **id**: `burns-1998-integrated-dna-analysis-device`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Mark A. Burns, Brian N. Johnson, Sundaresh N. Brahmasandra, Kalyan Handique, James R. Webster, Madhavi Krishnan, Timothy S. Sammarco, Piu M. Man, Darren Jones, Dylan Heldsinger, Carlos H. Mastrangelo, David T. Burke
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1126/science.282.5388.484; Science 282(5388) 484-487 (1998)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Landmark Science paper: first end-to-end integrated DNA analysis cartridge combining metering, mixing, thermal cycling (PCR), electrophoretic separation, and on-chip fluorescence detection in a single silicon-glass device with on-chip air-pressure pumping and thermo-pneumatic valving. By 1998 disclosure-genus, anticipates virtually every later sample-to-answer molecular diagnostic cartridge patent that claims 'integration of metering + amplification + separation + detection on a single substrate'. Defeats novelty for the architectural genus of POC molecular cartridges (BioFire, Cepheid GeneXpert, Abbott ID Now all descend from this disclosure).
