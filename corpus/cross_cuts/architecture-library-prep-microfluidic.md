## Smithsonian Center for Conservation Genomics Microfluidic Workflow (2015)

- **id**: `smithsonian-conservation-genomics-microfluidic`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Smithsonian Institution Center for Conservation Genomics
- **disclosure**: Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History 'Center for Conservation Genomics technical workflows' (2015); Maldonado J.E. et al. 'Conservation genomics with microfluidic library prep' Conservation Genetics Resources 7:633 (2015) doi:10.1007/s12686-015-0488-5
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Smithsonian CCG adoption of microfluidic NGS library prep (Fluidigm Access Array, Bio-Rad ddSEQ) for low-input non-invasive samples (hair, scat, eDNA) from endangered species. Anticipates: (a) institutional adoption of microfluidic NGS prep for conservation genomics; (b) low-input microfluidic library protocols for non-invasive samples; (c) workflow integration with national-level biorepository. Does not specify specific cartridge designs.
