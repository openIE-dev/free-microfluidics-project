- **disclosure**: Fette Compacting FE Series product brochure (Fette Compacting GmbH 2012); FE 35/55/75 technical specifications 2014-2020

- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Fette Compacting FE series rotary tablet press with integrated 'Sense' PAT system. Anticipates: (a) removable die-table cassette as a changeover-time-reduction strategy compatible with continuous-manufacturing flexibility; (b) integrated NIR + Raman + machine-vision PAT trio at the press outlet; (c) full OEB-5 containment integration in high-throughput rotary presses.
