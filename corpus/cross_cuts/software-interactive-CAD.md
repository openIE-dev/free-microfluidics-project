## 3DuF — Interactive Design Environment for Continuous-Flow Microfluidic Devices (2019-06-25)

- **id**: `sanka-2019-3duf-interactive-design`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Radhakrishna Sanka, Densmore lab (Boston University CIDAR Lab)
- **disclosure**: Sanka R, Lippai J, Samarasekera D, Nemsick S, Densmore D. 3DμF — Interactive Design Environment for Continuous Flow Microfluidic Devices. Scientific Reports 9: 9166 (2019). doi:10.1038/s41598-019-45623-z
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses element-by-element a modern open web-based microfluidic CAD editor: (1) parameterized library of fluidic primitives (channels, mixers, droplet generators, chambers, valves), (2) interactive canvas with snapping, layering and routing aids, (3) export pipeline producing SVG photomasks and STL volumes for direct fabrication, (4) plugin architecture for design-rule checking and integration with downstream simulation. Anticipates claims directed to: (a) browser-served interactive design environments for fluidic chips; (b) parameterized snap-to-grid microfluidic primitives with consistent fluidic-resistance metadata; (c) one-click export to multiple fabrication targets (laser cutting, soft lithography, multi-material 3D printing).
