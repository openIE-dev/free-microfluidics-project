## Agilent 4200 / 4150 / 5200 TapeStation automated electrophoresis (2009)

- **id**: `agilent-tapestation-4200`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Agilent Technologies (acquired Lab901)
- **disclosure**: Lab901 ScreenTape system acquired by Agilent (2011); TapeStation 2200 launch 2011, 4200 launch 2015; https://www.agilent.com/en/product/automated-electrophoresis/tapestation-systems; US7387717B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a laminated thin-film electrophoresis cassette system: ScreenTape cassettes contain pre-cast polyacrylamide gel separation channels laminated within a film substrate, with 16 lanes per cassette, sealed at manufacture; the TapeStation instrument robotically pierces each lane in turn, loads sample, applies voltage, performs fluorescence detection, and computes RINe (RIN-equivalent) scores. Anticipates: laminated-film electrophoresis cassette architectures as a class distinct from glass-chip electrophoresis; specifically the architecture of sealed pre-cast polyacrylamide cassettes pierced robotically at the time of analysis as a low-cost alternative to wet-etched glass chips; relevant prior art for any disposable nucleic-acid QC cassette using laminated polymer-film substrates.
