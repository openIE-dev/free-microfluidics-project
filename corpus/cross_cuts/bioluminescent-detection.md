## Hygiena Innovate ATP Hygiene Monitoring System (2018)

- **id**: `hygiena-innovate-atp-luminometer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Hygiena, LLC
- **disclosure**: Hygiena Innovate / EnSURE Touch product launch 2018; UltraSnap swab datasheet; US patent 6,524,795 (snap-valve ATP swab cartridge)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Hygiena's flagship ATP swab-cartridge architecture: a polypropylene tube with an upper bulb containing liquid-stable luciferin-luciferase, a lower swab pre-moistened with extractant, and a snap-valve that joins the two. Pressing the bulb mixes reagent with swab in a single sealed cartridge read in a benchtop luminometer. Anticipates: snap-valve mixing of liquid reagent with sample-loaded swab in a single sealed disposable; cloud-managed multi-site ATP monitoring ecosystems; and the architectural pattern of swab + reagent in one cartridge as a defining primitive of food-plant hygiene programs.
