## Rivnay-Lab Organic Iontronic Neural Interface (2018-09-27)

- **id**: `rivnay-iontronic-neural-interface`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Rivnay Lab (Northwestern University)
- **disclosure**: Rivnay et al., Nat. Rev. Mater. 2018, 3, 17086. DOI:10.1038/natrevmats.2017.86; Cea et al., Nat. Mater. 2020, 19, 679-686 (PEDOT:PSS injectable neural interface).
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Genuinely fluidic neural interface: the OECT mechanism uses cation injection from cerebrospinal fluid into a PEDOT:PSS channel to modulate hole density and thus channel current. The ionic conduction (interstitial K+/Na+ ions traversing into the conducting polymer) is the transduction mechanism. Discloses: organic electrochemical transistor front-ends fabricated on parylene-encapsulated flex substrates; ionic-electronic mixed conduction as the amplification mechanism; soft mechanical compliance matched to brain modulus to reduce gliosis. Anticipates: iontronic neural amplification using polymer/electrolyte interfaces; soft, mechanically-matched neural interfaces using PEDOT:PSS; the architectural pattern of distributed amplification at the electrode site eliminating noise on long flex cables. Genuinely a microfluidic-electronics hybrid.
