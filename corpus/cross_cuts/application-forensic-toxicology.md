## Randox Toxicology BioChip Array Drug Screen Multiplex (2009)

- **id**: `randox-toxicology-biochip-multiplex`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Randox Laboratories Ltd (Toxicology Division)
- **disclosure**: Randox Toxicology Ltd product brochure 'Evidence Investigator Drugs of Abuse Array' (2009); US7785862B2 'BioChip Array Technology'
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a microarray-on-microfluidic-chip platform with up to 44 spotted antibodies on a single 9 mm biochip surface, performing simultaneous immunoassays for drugs of abuse and metabolites with chemiluminescent imaging. Anticipates: (a) microarray multiplexing on a microfluidic chip for forensic toxicology; (b) the architectural pattern of biochip + chemiluminescence imager as a multiplex drugs-of-abuse panel; (c) 44-plex single-chip workflow. Strong patent anchor.
