## Romer Labs FluoroQuant Mycotoxin Quantitation System (2008)

- **id**: `romer-labs-fluoroquant-mycotoxin-quant`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Romer Labs Diagnostic GmbH
- **disclosure**: Romer Labs FluoroQuant product launch 2008; FluoroQuant Aflatoxin datasheet; AOAC Official Method 2008.02 reference
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an immunoaffinity-column-cleanup-plus-fluorometric quantitation cartridge approach: sample extract is passed through an antibody-loaded SPE column, eluted with methanol, derivatized, and quantified in a benchtop filter fluorometer with a fixed-volume cuvette. Anticipates: immunoaffinity SPE cartridge formats coupled with benchtop fluorometric readout for grain mycotoxin compliance testing; and the cartridge-plus-derivatization-plus-cuvette workflow as the analytical primitive for AOAC-recognized aflatoxin quantitation.
