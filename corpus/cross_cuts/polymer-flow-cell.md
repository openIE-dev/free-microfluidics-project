## Element Biosciences AVITI Avidite Loopable Polymerase Chemistry Patent Family (2022)

- **id**: `element-aviti-avidite-chemistry-patent-family`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Element Biosciences, Inc.
- **disclosure**: US11242512B2 'Compositions and methods for nucleic acid sequencing' (priority 2018); US11505782B2; WO2020076976A1; Element Biosciences AVITI launch 2022
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses avidity sequencing: instead of single-molecule reversible-terminator nucleotides (Illumina SBS), Element uses multivalent polymer-bound nucleotide conjugates ('avidites') that bind strongly to clonal clusters carrying many copies of the same template, decoupling base identification (driven by avidity) from chain extension (driven by separate unlabeled nucleotide). Anticipates: (a) any sequencing-by-avidity workflow using polymer-conjugated multivalent nucleotide labels; (b) the architectural separation of label-binding step from chain-extension step within one cycle; (c) the use of dyes presented as polymer-tethered clusters for high-density flow-cell imaging.
