## IKA ElectraSyn 2.0 Electrochemistry Research Instrument (2017)

- **id**: `ika-electrasyn-2-electrochemistry`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: IKA-Werke GmbH & Co. KG (Germany) in collaboration with Baran Group at Scripps
- **disclosure**: IKA ElectraSyn 2.0 product brochure 2017-2020; Kingston, C. et al. 'A Survival Guide for the Electro-curious.' Accounts of Chemical Research 53, 72-83 (2020), doi:10.1021/acs.accounts.9b00539
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the IKA ElectraSyn 2.0 - a compact, low-cost ($2k) benchtop potentiostat/galvanostat with integrated screw-in batch electrochemistry cells, plus an optional ElectraSyn flow cell accessory. Designed by IKA in collaboration with Phil Baran's group at Scripps to lower the access barrier for organic chemists to electrochemistry. Anticipates: (a) low-cost benchtop integrated potentiostat-cell instruments for organic chemistry electrochemistry; (b) screw-in interchangeable electrode architecture enabling rapid material screening; (c) modular flow-cell accessory converting batch instrument into a continuous-flow electrosynthesis platform; (d) the architectural pattern of vendor + academic-lab co-design driving electrochemistry adoption. Cite against later patents claiming compact benchtop potentiostat-cell integrated electrochemistry research instruments.
