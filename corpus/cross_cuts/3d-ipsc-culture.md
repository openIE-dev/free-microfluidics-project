## Inventia Life Science RASTRUM iPSC Bioprinting Variant (2022)

- **id**: `inventia-rastrum-ipsc-variant`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Inventia Life Science Pty Ltd.
- **disclosure**: Inventia RASTRUM iPSC application notes 2022; product datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Extends the existing inventia-rastrum entry by disclosing the iPSC-specific bioprinting application with defined hydrogel bioinks supporting 3D iPSC culture and organoid manufacturing. Anticipates: drop-on-demand bioprinting architectures for iPSC encapsulation in defined hydrogels; specifically supports the manufacturing transition from 2D iPSC culture (which suffers from spontaneous differentiation) to 3D bioprinted iPSC culture (which preserves pluripotency longer and enables direct progression to organoid differentiation).
