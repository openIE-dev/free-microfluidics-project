- **disclosure**: FabRx Ltd. M3DIMAKER product brochure 2020; Awad, A.; Trenfield, S. J.; Goyanes, A.; Gaisford, S.; Basit, A. W. '3D printing: Principles and pharmaceutical applications of selective laser sintering.' International Journal of Pharmaceutics 586, 119594 (2020), doi:10.1016/j.ijpharm.2020.119594

- **prior art notes**: Discloses the FabRx M3DIMAKER pharmaceutical 3D printer: a multi-modality printer (FDM/SSE/DPE/SLS) housed in a GMP-compatible enclosure with HEPA-filtered laminar-flow build chamber. ## AM4Pharma Semisolid 3D Pharmaceutical Printer (2020)

- **id**: `am4pharma-semisolid-3d-printer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: AM4Pharma Oy (University of Helsinki spinout)
- **disclosure**: Khatri, P.; Shah, M. K.; Vora, N. 'Formulation strategies for solid oral dosage form using 3D printing technology.' Journal of Drug Delivery Science and Technology 46, 148-155 (2018), doi:10.1016/j.jddst.2018.05.009; AM4Pharma Oy company technical brochure (Univ Helsinki spinout) 2020
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the AM4Pharma semisolid extrusion 3D pharmaceutical printer for heat-sensitive APIs. Unlike FDM-based printers, the SSE platform extrudes pre-mixed pharmaceutical pastes at room temperature, enabling printing of biologics, peptides, and proteins that would denature in HME. Anticipates: (a) room-temperature SSE 3D printers as a heat-sensitive-API alternative to FDM/HME pharmaceutical printing; (b) hydrogel-paste API formulations as printable feedstocks for SSE; (c) the small-batch personalized-medicine business model targeting orphan and rare diseases. Cite against later patents claiming room-temperature SSE pharmaceutical 3D printers.
