## Voxeljet VX Binder-Jetting Sand and Polymer Printer (1999)

- **id**: `voxeljet-binder-jet-printer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Voxeljet AG
- **disclosure**: Voxeljet AG (Friedberg, Germany, originally Voxeljet GmbH) founded 1999, technology spun out of TU Munich; VX1000 datasheet 2010; foundational Ederer / Hochsmann patents from late 1990s
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an industrial binder-jetting architecture descended from the foundational MIT 3DP patents (Sachs/Cima/Williams) but scaled to very large build volumes for foundry/casting applications. Architectural primitives: (1) stitched piezo printhead bar across the full build width (multi-meter); (2) recoater-blade powder spreading; (3) furan or aqueous binder for sand consolidation; (4) PMMA binder for polymer pattern fabrication; (5) Voxeljet-pioneered continuous build process where the build platform descends through a fixed printhead and parts emerge from the rear. Anticipates: industrial-scale binder-jetting systems with multi-meter build volumes, stitched printhead bars, and continuous-build variants. Critical prior art for foundry-direct mold-and-core 3D printing claims.
