## SetPoint Medical Implantable Vagus Nerve Stimulator (2012-07-05)

- **id**: `setpoint-medical-vagus-stimulator`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: SetPoint Medical Corporation
- **disclosure**: Koopman et al., PNAS 2016, 113, 8284-8289. DOI:10.1073/pnas.1605635113; SetPoint Medical RESET-RA trial NCT04539964; FDA breakthrough designation 2020.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Bioelectronic medicine implant in the architectural lineage of Cyberonics VNS, with a smaller form factor and a chronic-rechargeable battery in a hermetic Ti can. Microfluidic-relevant content: silicone encapsulation of the cuff electrode as the chronic neural-fluidic interface; titanium feedthroughs as the can-fluidic seal. Discloses: cervical vagus cuff electrode chronic implantation; closed-loop pulse generator; documented anti-inflammatory effect via splenic cholinergic pathway. Anticipates: bioelectronic medicine implants targeting non-neurological inflammatory disease (RA, Crohn's, lupus); the architectural pattern of cuff electrode + battery-IPG + transcutaneous-recharge as a neuro-immune therapeutic platform.
