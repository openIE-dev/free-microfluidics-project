## Magnetic-activated cell sorting (MACS) (1990)

- **id**: `miltenyi-1990-macs-magnetic-cell-sorting`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Miltenyi et al., founder of Miltenyi Biotec
- **disclosure**: Miltenyi, S.; Müller, W.; Weichel, W.; Radbruch, A. High gradient magnetic cell separation with MACS. Cytometry 1990, 11, 231–238. DOI: 10.1002/cyto.990110203
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Disclosed high-gradient magnetic cell separation using ferromagnetic-bead-coated antibodies in a column packed with steel matrix in a strong external field. Industrially the foundational immunomagnetic cell separation chemistry, although the column form is non-microfluidic. Critical context for microfluidic immunomagnetic separation: every CTC-iChip and IsoFlux-style architecture is the chip-scale descendant of the MACS column.

- **creator**: Veridex / Immunicon (now Menarini Silicon Biosystems)
