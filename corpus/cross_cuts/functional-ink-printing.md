## Optomec Aerosol Jet Printed Electronics System (2004)

- **id**: `optomec-aerosol-jet-printer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Optomec Inc. (technology originated at Sciperio under Maskless Mesoscale Materials Deposition / M3D program)
- **disclosure**: Optomec acquisition of Aerosol Jet (M3D) technology 2004 from Sciperio; Optomec Aerosol Jet datasheet 2008; foundational US patent 7,938,341
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an aerosol jet (M3D) deposition architecture fundamentally distinct from inkjet drop-on-demand: ink is first atomized into a mist (1-5 micron droplets) by ultrasonic or pneumatic atomizer, the mist is entrained in a carrier-gas stream, then the stream is aerodynamically focused by a coaxial sheath gas through a converging nozzle to a 10-micron-wide deposition focus. This focusing happens in-flight without contact and the working distance can be 1-5mm, allowing deposition onto curved and 3D surfaces. Anticipates: any aerosol-based functional-ink deposition system using coaxial sheath-gas aerodynamic focusing of an atomized-ink stream. The architectural primitive is (atomizer + aerosol entrainment + coaxial sheath-gas focusing nozzle + non-contact 1-5mm working distance), which defines the entire aerosol-jet category as separate from piezo or thermal inkjet drop-on-demand. Critical prior art for printed-electronics / conformal-electronics / 3D-substrate-printing claims.
