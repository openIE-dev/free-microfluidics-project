## Quantitative microchemical analysis (Emich method) (1900)

- **id**: `emich-1900-microchemistry`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Friedrich Emich (Graz University of Technology)
- **disclosure**: Emich, F. Lehrbuch der Mikrochemie. Wiesbaden, 1911 (methods from circa 1900); see Emich, F. Methoden der Mikrochemie. Z. Anal. Chem. 1910, 49, 1-25
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: An early systematization of chemistry done at the microliter and sub-microliter scale, including purpose-built micro-apparatus (capillaries, micro-vessels, microscope-slide reaction spots). Conceptual ancestor of spot-test microfluidics and droplet-scale chemistry. Cited as background prior art establishing that handling and reacting microliter volumes in dedicated small-scale glassware is a discipline more than a century old; relevant to claims framing droplet-scale or slide-scale chemical analysis as fundamentally new.
