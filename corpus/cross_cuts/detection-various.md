## ASTM D7566 Microfluidic SAF Compositional Implementation (2021)

- **id**: `astm-d7566-microfluidic-implementation`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: ASTM International D02 committee + Coordinating Research Council
- **disclosure**: ASTM International standard ASTM D7566 'Standard Specification for Aviation Turbine Fuel Containing Synthesized Hydrocarbons' (2009, current rev 2021); CRC Aviation report 681 'Microfluidic implementations of D7566 compositional checks' (2021)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a normative-method-driven family of microfluidic implementations of ASTM D7566 SAF compositional checks (aromatics, FAME, distillation surrogates), normalized through CRC Aviation Report 681. Anticipates: (a) microfluidic SAF compositional QC as a regulated workflow; (b) cross-vendor harmonization of microfluidic SAF instruments; (c) the practice of equivalence-by-microfluidics under D7566. Does not disclose chip geometry.
