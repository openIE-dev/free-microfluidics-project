## Singular Genomics G4 Flow Cell and SBX Chemistry Patent Family (2021)

- **id**: `singular-genomics-g4-flow-cell-patent-family`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Singular Genomics Systems, Inc.
- **disclosure**: US11512336B2 'Sequencing systems and methods using glass-substrate flow cells' (priority 2019); US11459604B2; Singular Genomics G4 launch 2022
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Singular's G4 flow-cell architecture: four independently addressable glass flow cells per cartridge, each with patterned nanoclusters and on-cartridge fluidics manifold supporting independent run-start times. Anticipates: (a) sequencer architectures with independent per-flow-cell run scheduling; (b) glass-substrate flow cells with patterned cluster sites at sub-micrometer pitch; (c) the cartridge-as-replaceable-fluidics-manifold pattern that lets a single optics path service four cells. Material to claims by Illumina patents requiring a single run-clock for the entire instrument.
