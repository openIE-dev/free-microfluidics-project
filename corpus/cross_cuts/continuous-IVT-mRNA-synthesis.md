## Moderna mRNA Continuous-Process Manufacturing Platform (2020-12-18)

- **id**: `moderna-mrna-continuous-process`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Moderna Inc.
- **disclosure**: Moderna Inc. SEC 10-K 2020; Schoenmaker et al., Int. J. Pharm. 2021, 601, 120586 DOI:10.1016/j.ijpharm.2021.120586 (mRNA-LNP review covering Moderna platform); Moderna Spikevax EMA EPAR 2021-01.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Moderna's mRNA-LNP manufacturing platform integrates continuous in-vitro transcription, capping, mRNA purification, and microfluidic LNP encapsulation in a GMP cleanroom skid stack. Public technical disclosure is limited (proprietary), but the patent portfolio and EMA EPAR filings disclose the architectural pattern. Discloses: continuous IVT module with template DNA addition + NTPs + T7 polymerase; co-transcriptional or post-transcriptional 5' capping; mRNA tangential-flow filtration purification; microfluidic LNP encapsulation step (mixer geometry not fully disclosed); continuous downstream concentration + diafiltration. Anticipates: continuous-process mRNA-LNP manufacturing at clinical-scale; the integrated workflow of IVT -> cap -> purify -> LNP-encapsulate -> formulate as a standard architectural template; LNP encapsulation as the mass-throughput-limiting unit operation.
