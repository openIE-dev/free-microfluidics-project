## DARPA RAM Replay Memory-Restoration Neural Implant (2018-03-28)

- **id**: `darpa-ram-replay-memory-implant`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Wake Forest School of Medicine + USC + DARPA Biological Technologies Office
- **disclosure**: Hampson et al., J. Neural Eng. 2018, 15, 036014. DOI:10.1088/1741-2552/aaaed7; DARPA RAM Replay program kickoff 2014.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: First-in-human demonstration of a closed-loop memory-pattern injection neural implant. Patient-specific pattern decoder (trained on hippocampal CA3 activity during memory encoding) drives a stimulator that injects the decoded pattern back into CA1 during recall. Microfluidic-relevant content: parylene encapsulation of the chronic Pt-Ir microwire array; titanium skull-port forming a percutaneous fluidic seal. Anticipates: closed-loop memory-restoration BMI using model-based pattern injection; chronic subdural multielectrode arrays connected to programmable IPG-style pulse generators; the architectural pattern of decoder-on-implant + stimulator-on-implant for neuroprosthetic memory.
