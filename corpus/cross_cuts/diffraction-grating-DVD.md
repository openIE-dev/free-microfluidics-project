## Public Lab Foldable Papercraft Mini-Spectrometer (2014)

- **id**: `publiclab-foldable-spectrometer-2014`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Jeffrey Warren and Public Lab community (Boston, USA)
- **disclosure**: Warren J et al. Public Lab Foldable Spectrometry Starter Kit. https://publiclab.org/wiki/foldable-spec (2014; v2 2017-11-30 https://publiclab.org/notes/warren/11-30-2017/build-a-papercraft-spectrometer-for-your-phone-version-2-0); GitHub https://github.com/publiclab/papercraft-spectrometer
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Discloses element-by-element a smartphone spectrometer constructed from folded cardstock and a DVD-derived diffraction grating, coupled with the Spectral Workbench cloud-hosted spectrum-extraction software. Anticipates: (a) paper-form foldable smartphone spectrometers (claims regarding low-cost foldable optical instruments coupled to phone cameras), (b) cloud-hosted spectral analysis paired with phone-camera-acquired spectra, (c) educational kits combining DVD diffraction with smartphone capture as prior art against subsequent commercial DIY spectrometer claims.

## Public Lab Papercraft Spectrometer v2.0 (2017-11-30)

- **id**: `publiclab-papercraft-spectrometer-v2`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Jeffrey Warren and Public Lab community
- **disclosure**: Warren J. Build a papercraft spectrometer for your phone — version 2.0. Public Lab research note 2017-11-30. https://publiclab.org/notes/warren/11-30-2017/build-a-papercraft-spectrometer-for-your-phone-version-2-0 ; repo https://github.com/publiclab/papercraft-spectrometer

Anticipates: claims around single-sheet folded smartphone spectrometers and improved phone-alignment optical patterns.
