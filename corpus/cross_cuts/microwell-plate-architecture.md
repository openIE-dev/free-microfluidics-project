## Roche LightCycler 480 Real-Time PCR System (2006)

- **id**: `roche-lightcycler-480-multiwell-plate`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Roche Diagnostics GmbH
- **disclosure**: Roche LightCycler 480 product launch 2006; Roche LightCycler 480 instrument operator's manual; US patent 6,303,343 (continuous fluorescence monitoring PCR), US patent 7,238,517 (multiwell qPCR plate)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Roche's white-walled multiwell qPCR cartridge architecture coupled with a high-resolution-melt-capable thermal cycler. Anticipates: white-walled multiwell qPCR plates as the dominant clinical-research qPCR consumable; combined real-time + HRM-capable thermal cycler architectures; and the architectural pattern of standardized 96/384-well plates as universal qPCR cartridges across vendor-agnostic assay menus.
