## Maxwell 1867 — On the dynamical theory of gases (kinetic theory of viscosity) (1867)

- **id**: `maxwell-1867-viscosity-kinetic-theory`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: James Clerk Maxwell
- **disclosure**: J.C. Maxwell, 'On the dynamical theory of gases', Phil. Trans. Roy. Soc. London 157, 49-88 (1867)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Derivation of gas viscosity from kinetic theory; predicts viscosity is independent of pressure and proportional to sqrt(T). Foundational for: gas-flow microfluidics (gas chromatography on chip, micro gas sensors), Knudsen-number scaling at low pressure, and slip-flow corrections. Anticipates: claims that microscale gas-flow devices exhibit 'unexpected' viscosity behavior as novel — the kinetic-theory framework is 159 years old. Also load-bearing for any breath-analysis lab-on-chip claiming gas-phase transport effects.
