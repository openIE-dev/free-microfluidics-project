## CNSA Tianwen-1 Zhurong Rover Fluidic Instruments (2021)

- **id**: `tianwen1-zhurong-rover-instruments`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: China National Space Administration (CNSA) / China Academy of Space Technology
- **disclosure**: Wan, W. X. et al. 'China's first mission to Mars.' Nature Astronomy 4, 721 (2020), doi:10.1038/s41550-020-1148-6; Zou, Y. et al. 'Scientific objectives and payloads of Tianwen-1, China's first Mars exploration mission.' Advances in Space Research 67, 812-823 (2021)
- **ip status**: unknown
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Zhurong rover instrument suite including MarSCoDe LIBS+SWIR. The instrument hosts a sealed nine-target onboard calibration carousel rotated under stepper-motor control to expose reference materials (basalt, talc, etc.) to the LIBS focus for periodic in-flight wavelength/composition calibration. Anticipates: (a) sealed multi-target calibration carousels for planetary LIBS instruments; (b) co-located LIBS+SWIR optical-channel fluidics where the LIBS plasma plume is observed by both UV and SWIR spectrometers via shared evacuated optical-bench; (c) magnetometer/meteorology shared sensor head with sealed Pt100 temperature probe and CMOS pressure transducer. Cite against later patents claiming on-board calibration-target carousels for planetary LIBS rovers.
