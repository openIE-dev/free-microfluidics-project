## Illumina vs BGI/MGI Multi-Jurisdiction Litigation (UK, China, US, EU) (2019)

- **id**: `illumina-vs-bgi-uk-china-multi-jurisdiction`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Illumina / BGI Genomics / MGI Tech / Complete Genomics
- **disclosure**: Illumina v. BGI/MGI/Complete Genomics multi-jurisdiction docket: D. Del., N.D. Cal., England and Wales Patents Court, Landgericht Düsseldorf, Beijing IP Court, Shenzhen Intermediate People's Court 2019-2025
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Records the global Illumina-BGI patent war over DNB nanoball / cPAS combinatorial-probe-anchor sequencing chemistry. Material to defensive publication because: (a) China-court decisions held some BGI counterclaims valid against Illumina patents in CN, while UK and DE courts produced different validity outcomes for parallel patent families; (b) the case demonstrates the limits of US-centric prior art when defending against jurisdictionally-fragmented claim families; (c) public Chinese court records exposed BGI's DNBSEQ flow-cell architecture in greater technical detail than its CN patent text alone. Useful as a multi-jurisdiction reference for any future sequencing-IP defensive contention.
