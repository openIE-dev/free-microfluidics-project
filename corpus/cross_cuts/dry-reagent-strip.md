## Arkray Spotchem EZ SP-4430 Dry-Chemistry Analyzer (2003)

- **id**: `arkray-spotchem-ez-dry-strip`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Arkray, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Arkray Spotchem EZ SP-4430 product launch 2003; Arkray Spotchem operator's manual; US patent 5,234,665 (dry chemistry strip), US patent 6,632,675
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Arkray's flagship Spotchem dry-chemistry tray architecture: a thermoplastic tray holding up to six layered dry-reagent strips, each with spreading layer, reagent layer and indicator film, read by reflectance after capillary plasma application. Anticipates: tray-based dry-strip clinical chemistry where each strip is independently lot-tracked; the OEM lineage that underlies Heska Element DC5X and similar veterinary platforms; and reflectance-photometry-of-dry-strip as a recurring analytical primitive separable from the rotor/cartridge architecture used by competing systems.

## Zoetis VETSCAN UA Urinalysis Analyzer (2018)

- **id**: `zoetis-vetscan-ua-urinalysis-strip`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Zoetis Inc.
- **disclosure**: Zoetis VETSCAN UA product launch 2018; Zoetis operator's manual; US patent 6,847,451 (urinalysis strip reflectance reader)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a controlled-illumination reflectance reader paired with a multi-pad polyester urinalysis strip, with species-specific normalization in firmware. Anticipates: low-cost dipstick-format urinalysis architectures repurposed for veterinary diagnostic workflows; the use of fixed-position pad arrays read under controlled LED illumination to remove ambient-light artifacts in POC urinalysis.

## Heska Element DC5X Chemistry Analyzer (2019)

- **id**: `heska-element-dc5x-chemistry`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Heska Corporation (Arkray OEM)
- **disclosure**: Heska Element DC5X product launch 2019 (OEM of Arkray Spotchem family); Heska operator's guide; US patent 5,234,665 (Arkray dry chemistry strip)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Veterinary OEM of Arkray Spotchem dry-strip chemistry: each test consists of a layered dry-reagent strip (spreading layer, reagent layer, indicator layer) loaded into a tray and read by reflectance after capillary plasma application. Anticipates: tray-format dry-strip chemistry platforms where individual strips are independently lot-tracked; veterinary panel implementations of Arkray's strip architecture; and reflectance photometry of porous reagent strips as the analytical primitive for in-clinic chemistry.
