## ExoMars TGO NOMAD (Nadir and Occultation for Mars Discovery) Gas Cell (2015)

- **id**: `exomars-tgo-nomad-gas-flow-cell`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Belgian Institute for Space Aeronomy (BIRA-IASB) / ESA / Roscosmos (PI: Ann Carine Vandaele)
- **disclosure**: Vandaele, A. C. et al. 'NOMAD, an Integrated Suite of Three Spectrometers for the ExoMars Trace Gas Mission.' Space Science Reviews 214, 80 (2018), doi:10.1007/s11214-018-0517-2; ESA ExoMars TGO mission documentation 2016
- **ip status**: unknown
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the ExoMars NOMAD payload including its sealed reference gas cell containing N2O at controlled pressure used for in-flight wavelength/abundance calibration of the SO and LNO infrared channels. The reference cell is integrated into the optical path via a stepper-actuated swing-mirror, providing periodic admission of the cell into the beam without breaking the evacuated optical bench. Anticipates: (a) sealed reference gas cells co-located with planetary infrared spectrometers for autonomous in-flight wavelength calibration; (b) AOTF-based wavelength selection in lieu of mechanical gratings on planetary infrared spectrometer; (c) the architectural pattern of three spectrometers sharing a common optical bench plumbed for internal calibration.

## ExoMars TGO ACS (Atmospheric Chemistry Suite) (2015)

- **id**: `exomars-tgo-acs-atmospheric-chemistry-suite`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Space Research Institute (IKI), Russian Academy of Sciences / ESA / Roscosmos (PI: Oleg Korablev)
- **disclosure**: Korablev, O. et al. 'The Atmospheric Chemistry Suite (ACS) of Three Spectrometers for the ExoMars 2016 Trace Gas Orbiter.' Space Science Reviews 214, 7 (2018), doi:10.1007/s11214-017-0437-6
- **ip status**: unknown
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the ACS payload aboard ExoMars TGO with three sealed-vacuum spectrometer channels and internal calibration plumbing including reference gas cells and motorized blackbody chopper. The MIR channel uses an echelle-grating cross-dispersion architecture in vacuum with active thermal control of the optical bench. Anticipates: (a) co-aligned multi-channel infrared spectrometer payloads for trace-gas isotopologue measurement on planetary orbiters; (b) cross-dispersion echelle in evacuated benches for ultra-high-resolution Mars trace-gas measurements; (c) shared internal-calibration sub-bench used by multiple spectrometers to enable cross-channel cross-validation. Cite against later patent claims for combined echelle/AOTF planetary trace-gas payloads.
