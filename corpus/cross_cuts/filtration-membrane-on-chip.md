## IDS Jet Fuel Particulate Microfluidic Cartridge (2015)

- **id**: `ids-jet-fuel-particulate-cartridge`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: International Drug Surveillance / IDS Inc
- **disclosure**: IDS Inc product datasheet 'Particulate and Free Water Jet Fuel Cartridge' (2015)
- **ip status**: trade-secret
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use thermoplastic microfluidic cartridge for combined free-water and particulate-contamination QC of Jet A and Jet A-1, with an integrated 0.8 micrometer membrane and a colorimetric water indicator. Anticipates: (a) tarmac-deployable jet-fuel QC cartridge with integrated membrane filtration; (b) ASTM D5452-equivalent cartridge format; (c) the architectural pattern of a hybrid mechanical-filter / colorimetric-water microfluidic device for fuel. Does not disclose membrane wettability tuning.
