## ChromaTan Multimix Continuous Chromatography System (2014)

- **id**: `chromatan-multimix-continuous-chromatography`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: ChromaTan Corporation
- **disclosure**: ChromaTan Corporation founding 2014; Multimix product literature 2018
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a multi-column periodic-counter-current chromatography skid where 4-8 columns cycle through load / wash / elute / regenerate phases under valve control, providing continuous output and >2x resin utilization vs single-column batch. Anticipates: multi-column periodic-counter-current continuous chromatography architectures for biopharma manufacturing; specifically smaller-column-with-cycling rather than larger-column-with-batch architectural choice. Competes with BioSMB (Sartorius) and Akta PCC (Cytiva) in the continuous-chromatography segment.
