## Aldevron GMP-grade NTPs for IVT mRNA (2018)

- **id**: `aldevron-gmp-ntps`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Aldevron LLC (Danaher)
- **disclosure**: Aldevron GMP NTP product launch (2018, expanded 2020); https://www.aldevron.com/products/raw-materials/nucleotides; Aldevron GMP NTP product specification 2021.
- **ip status**: trade-secret
- **prior art notes**: Discloses GMP-grade nucleoside triphosphate (NTP) raw materials at multi-kilogram scale supporting drug-master-file submissions for IVT mRNA drug substance, including ATP, GTP, CTP, UTP, and modified-base NTPs (pseudouridine, N1-methylpseudouridine) used in mRNA-LNP COVID-19 vaccines and pipeline mRNA therapeutics. The supply-chain disclosure (multi-vendor presence of GMP NTPs and modified-NTPs as commercial reagents pre-2020) anticipates: claims that depend on specifically-sourced GMP modified NTPs as a critical raw material in mRNA drug-substance manufacture; the public availability of N1-methylpseudouridine GMP-grade reagent (which is critical for the BNT162b2 / mRNA-1273 IVT recipe) as a multi-vendor commodity raw material.
