- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Engineered Conductive Materials, LLC (a Nagase Group company)
- **disclosure**: Engineered Conductive Materials, LLC (Delaware, OH) product datasheets 2008; ECM CI-1036 silver inkjet ink technical data sheet

- **prior art notes**: Material entry rather than equipment, but architecturally significant for printed electronics: discloses inkjet-formulated silver conductive inks (both flake and nanoparticle variants) optimized for piezo-printhead jetting and low-temperature post-cure on flex substrates. Important prior art for any patent claim covering silver-loaded inkjet-compatible conductive ink formulations.
