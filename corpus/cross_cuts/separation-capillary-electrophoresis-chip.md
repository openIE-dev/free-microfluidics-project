## Manz et al. 1992 — Planar chips technology for miniaturization of separation systems (first electrophoretic separation on chip) (1992)

- **id**: `manz-1992-electrophoretic-separation-on-chip`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Andreas Manz, Daniel J. Harrison, Elisabeth Verpoorte, Carlo S. Effenhauser, Albert Paulus, Hans Lüdi, H. Michael Widmer
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1016/0021-9673(92)80293-4; J. Chromatogr. 593, 253-258 (1992)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: First demonstration of an electrophoretic separation performed on a planar microfabricated chip. Predates Harrison 1992/1993 Anal. Chem. coverage of capillary electrophoresis on chip. Anticipates: any patent claim to 'a microfluidic chip comprising microfabricated channels in a planar substrate for electrophoretic separation of analytes' filed after 1992. Foundational disclosure for the entire chip-CE industry.

- **id**: `manz-becker-1998-microsystem-technology-textbook`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Andreas Manz, Holger Becker (eds.)
- **disclosure**: ISBN 978-3540655558; Springer Topics in Current Chemistry vol 194, 1998
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Edited Springer volume by the originators of µTAS/lab-on-a-chip; chapters cover microreaction technology, capillary electrophoresis chips, miniaturized analysis, and integrated bio-detection.
