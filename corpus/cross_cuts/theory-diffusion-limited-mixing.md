## Stone & Kim 2001 — Microfluidics: basic issues, applications, and challenges (2001-06)

- **id**: `stone-kim-2001-microfluidics-basic-issues`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Howard A. Stone, Sam Kim
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1002/aic.690470602; AIChE Journal 47(6) 1250-1254 (2001)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Editorial-review framing of the nascent microfluidics field at the chemical-engineering scale. Discloses the canonical taxonomy of microfluidic challenges (low Reynolds, diffusion-limited mixing, surface-tension dominance, manufacturability constraints, integration with off-chip macroworld) before any 'microfluidics platform' patents on those general architectures issued. Anticipates: claims that abstract over a 'microfluidic platform comprising channels of <500 µm characteristic dimension exploiting laminar flow, surface tension, and capillarity for chemical or biological assay' — i.e., genus-level claims naming low-Re flow as the operative principle. Establishes the engineering-principles framing that a competent examiner uses to reject overly broad microfluidic genus claims as obvious in view of a published research agenda.
