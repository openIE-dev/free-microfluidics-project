## Plateau 1873 — Statique expérimentale et théorique des liquides soumis aux seules forces moléculaires (1873)

- **id**: `plateau-1873-soap-films-capillarity`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Joseph Antoine Ferdinand Plateau
- **disclosure**: J.A.F. Plateau, 'Statique expérimentale et théorique des liquides soumis aux seules forces moléculaires', Gauthier-Villars, Paris (2 vol., 1873)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Foundational treatise on capillarity, soap films, minimal surfaces, and droplet/jet stability. Establishes Plateau's laws (geometry of soap-film junctions and Plateau borders) and the Plateau-Rayleigh instability criterion for jets. Foundational for: any droplet-microfluidic process exploiting jet break-up (flow-focusing, T-junction, co-flow), interfacial geometry (Plateau borders in foams), and minimal-surface microfabrication. Anticipates: claims to droplet generators that recite jet-break-up as their operating principle; the criterion is 153 years old.

## Rayleigh 1879 — On the instability of jets (Plateau-Rayleigh instability) (1879)

- **id**: `rayleigh-1879-jet-instability`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: John William Strutt, 3rd Baron Rayleigh
- **disclosure**: Lord Rayleigh, 'On the instability of jets', Proc. London Math. Soc. s1-10, 4-13 (1879); doi:10.1112/plms/s1-10.1.4
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Quantitative linear-stability analysis showing that a cylindrical liquid jet is unstable to perturbations of wavelength greater than its circumference (most-unstable wavelength ≈ 9.02 r), leading to break-up into droplets. Anticipates: every droplet-microfluidic generator (T-junction, flow-focusing, co-flow), every continuous-inkjet printhead, every drop-on-demand piezo printhead, every microfluidic emulsion generator. The criterion is the operating physics of these devices and is 147 years old. Particularly load-bearing for inkjet/drop-generator patents claiming 'a method of producing droplets of controlled size by perturbing a liquid jet'.
