## Mettler-Toledo InPro 6000 Series Polarographic / Optical DO Sensors (2008)

- **id**: `mettler-toledo-inpro-6000-series`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Mettler-Toledo GmbH (Process Analytics)
- **disclosure**: Mettler-Toledo InPro 6800 launch ~2008; InPro 6970i optical DO launch 2017; product datasheets rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Extends the existing mettler-toledo-inpro-6800-do entry by disclosing the broader InPro 6000 series including the InPro 6970i optical / luminescence-based DO sensor (alternative to polarographic 6800/6850i). Anticipates: dual-technology DO sensor families (polarographic + optical / luminescence) sharing a common digital protocol (ISM) and form factor (PG-13.5), allowing customers to switch sensing chemistries without changing the bioreactor headplate or transmitter. The optical 6970i is competitive with PreSens patches for accuracy + drift; the polarographic 6800 retains broad CIP/SIP compatibility advantages.
