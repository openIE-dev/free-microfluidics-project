## Trasis miniAllInOne Compact PET Tracer Synthesizer (2014)

- **id**: `trasis-mini-allinone`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Trasis SA
- **disclosure**: Trasis SA product launch 2014; Lemaire et al., J. Nucl. Med. 2014, 55(Suppl 1), 1208 abstract.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Compact academic-scale PET tracer synthesizer competing with GE FASTlab and IBA Synthera. Single-use cassette architecture similar to GE FASTlab but at ~3x smaller form factor for university-scale or rare-tracer R&D. Discloses: pre-packed disposable cassette with ~10-15 valves, reaction vessel, purification cartridge stack, and reagent reservoirs; syringe-driven cassette interface; extensible chemistry library covering F-18, C-11, Ga-68 isotopes; software-controlled multi-step radiolabeling sequences. Anticipates: compact bench-scale single-use cassette PET radiosynthesis for academic and rare-tracer development; the architectural pattern of trace-element-clean disposables enabling rapid switching between tracer chemistries without cross-contamination.
