- **id**: `whoi-itp-microfluidic-chemistry`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution (WHOI)
- **disclosure**: Krishfield R. et al. 'Ice-Tethered Profiler observations under Arctic sea ice with chemistry option' WHOI Technical Report 2018-01 (2018); Beaufort Gyre Exploration Project ITP-V documentation
- **ip status**: open-permissive
