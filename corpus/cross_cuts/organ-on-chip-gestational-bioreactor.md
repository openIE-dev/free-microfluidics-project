- **corpus**: fictional
- **device class**: fictional-laboratory

- **disclosure**: Lois McMaster Bujold, 'Shards of Honor' (Baen Books, 1986, ISBN 0-671-65644-8); central to 'Barrayar' (Baen, 1991, ISBN 0-671-72083-9); 'Mirror Dance' (Baen, 1994); 'Cetaganda' (Baen, 1996); detailed in 'A Civil Campaign' (Baen, 1999)
- **ip status**: fictional
- **prior art notes**: Discloses ex-vivo gestation of a human embryo to term inside an instrumented perfused chamber, as a mass-produced civilian biotech product. Bujold's 1986 disclosure substantially predates the 2017 Partridge et al. (CHOP) Biobag publication.
