## SonoSep Technologies Acoustic Cell Separator (2002)

- **id**: `sonosep-acoustic-cell-separation`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: SonoSep Technologies Inc. (Vancouver BC, James Piret group spinout)
- **disclosure**: Trampler F., Sonderhoff S. A., Pui P. W. S., Kilburn D. G., Piret J. M., Bio/Technology 12:281-284 (1994); SonoSep Technologies Inc. product literature, Vancouver BC; US patent 5,626,767
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: SonoSep discloses a bulk-acoustic-wave resonant retention chamber inserted into the perfusion loop of a bioreactor; cells aggregate at pressure nodes and settle back into the bioreactor while spent medium is harvested. Anticipates: (i) BAW-based bioreactor perfusion devices; (ii) acoustic separators scaled to multi-liter clinical-grade biomanufacturing; (iii) the combination of acoustic resonance with gravitational sedimentation for membrane-free clarification. Predates AcouSort blood-prep products by ~15 years for the bioreactor application.

## Free-flow acoustophoresis for cell separation (2007)

- **id**: `laurell-2007-acoustophoresis`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Laurell group, Lund University
- **disclosure**: Petersson, F.; Åberg, L.; Swärd-Nilsson, A.-M.; Laurell, T. Free flow acoustophoresis: microfluidic-based mode of particle and cell separation. Anal. Chem. 2007, 79, 5117–5123. DOI: 10.1021/ac070444e
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Foundational disclosure of free-flow acoustophoresis: continuous-flow particle separation in a microchannel by transverse acoustic radiation force from a half-wavelength bulk acoustic standing wave, with sample inlet and multiple outlets at distinct lateral positions. Anticipates: silicon-channel BAW resonator architecture, label-free continuous acoustic separation by particle compressibility / density contrast, and the entire bulk-acoustic-wave acoustofluidics field commercialized by AcouSort and used in dozens of academic CTC and exosome platforms.

## Standing surface acoustic wave (SSAW) acoustic tweezers (2012)

- **id**: `ding-2012-saw-acoustic-tweezers`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: T. J. Huang group, Penn State / Duke
- **disclosure**: Ding, X.; Lin, S.-C. S.; Kiraly, B.; Yue, H.; Li, S.; Chiang, I.-K.; Shi, J.; Benkovic, S. J.; Huang, T. J. On-chip manipulation of single microparticles, cells, and organisms using surface acoustic waves. Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 2012, 109, 11105–11109. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1209288109
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Disclosed SSAW (standing surface acoustic wave) micromanipulation of cells and microparticles in a PDMS channel atop a lithium niobate substrate with paired IDTs. Anticipates: IDT-on-LiNbO3 SSAW architecture for sub-mm patterning of pressure nodes in solution, individually addressable cell trapping by SAW phase shifting, and the SAW microfluidics paradigm that competes with Laurell-style BAW. Underlies most subsequent SAW-based cell-separation papers.

## AcouSort BAW acoustofluidic platform (2013)

- **id**: `acousort-acoustofluidic-platform`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: AcouSort AB (Lund Sweden, spinoff from Laurell group)
- **disclosure**: AcouSort AB. https://acousort.com
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Commercial bulk-acoustic-wave (BAW) acoustofluidic platform: silicon-microchannel BAW resonator for label-free continuous cell separation. Anticipates: BAW-acoustofluidic platform commercialization at clinical scale.

## AcouSort AcouTrap (2018)

- **id**: `acousort-acoutrap-cell-retention`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: AcouSort AB
- **disclosure**: AcouSort AB AcouTrap product datasheet (2018); AcouSort AB IPO prospectus 2019; product page https://acousort.com/products/acoutrap/
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: AcouTrap discloses a silicon-glass etched microchannel coupled to a piezoelectric transducer driven near the half-wavelength resonance of the channel cross-section, generating a single transverse pressure node that traps suspended cells against continuous flow. Anticipates: (i) any subsequent claim to a flow-through cell retention unit using bulk acoustic standing waves in a microfabricated chip; (ii) buffer-exchange architectures wherein medium is swept past a stationary acoustically-trapped cell pellet; (iii) the specific combination of transducer impedance matching to a silicon resonator for biological cell retention without centrifugation. Distinguished from droplet acoustic trapping (Hou 2012) by use of a continuous channel rather than discrete droplets.

## AcouSort AcouSep (2019)

- **id**: `acousort-acousep-blood-separator`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: AcouSort AB
- **disclosure**: AcouSort AB AcouSep product datasheet (2019); product page https://acousort.com/products/acousep/
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: AcouSep discloses a silicon-glass acoustophoretic chip with a single channel and trifurcated outlet, where erythrocytes (high acoustic contrast) migrate to the central pressure node and platelet-rich plasma exits via side outlets. Anticipates: (i) continuous-flow free-flow acoustophoretic blood plasma separators with multi-outlet harvesting; (ii) the use of acoustic-radiation-force differential focusing to fractionate cells by size and compressibility without filters or centrifuge; (iii) coupling such chips to clinical-flow front-ends for continuous plasma harvest. Builds on Laurell 2007 acoustophoresis foundation but adds productized chip-holder, transducer assembly, and disposable interface.

## AcouSort AcouWash (2020)

- **id**: `acousort-acouwash-buffer-exchange`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: AcouSort AB
- **disclosure**: AcouSort AB AcouWash 2 datasheet (2020); product page https://acousort.com/products/acouwash/
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: AcouWash discloses a parallel laminar coflow chip in which cells in dirty buffer enter via one inlet, are translated by acoustic radiation force across a co-flowing clean buffer stream, and exit through a clean outlet. Anticipates: (i) continuous-flow microfluidic buffer-exchange architectures using acoustic radiation force as the translation mechanism; (ii) parallel-stream wash protocols replacing centrifuge-based washes for shear-sensitive cells (T cells, NK cells); (iii) integration of acoustic translation modules upstream of single-cell sequencing or cell-therapy fill stations.
