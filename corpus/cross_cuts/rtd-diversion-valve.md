## Janssen Prezista Continuous Direct Compression Manufacturing Line (2016)

- **id**: `janssen-prezista-continuous-direct-compression-line`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Janssen Pharmaceuticals (Johnson & Johnson) Puerto Rico / Belgium
- **disclosure**: FDA Approval, Prezista (darunavir) Supplement: Continuous Manufacturing, NDA 021976/S-049, approved 2016-04-12; Lee, S. L. et al. 'Modernizing Pharmaceutical Manufacturing: from Batch to Continuous Production.' Journal of Pharmaceutical Innovation 10, 191-199 (2015), doi:10.1007/s12247-015-9215-8
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Janssen continuous direct-compression line approved by the FDA in 2016 for Prezista (darunavir), the first FDA approval of a switch from batch to continuous for an already-approved drug. The line architecture includes: (a) loss-in-weight feeders metering API + excipients into a continuous blender; (b) in-line NIR PAT spectrometers measuring blend uniformity at multiple points; (c) rotary tablet press (Korsch XL series) integrated with PAT-driven control loop; (d) residence-time-distribution-based diversion valve sending out-of-spec material to waste while in-spec material continues. Anticipates: (a) the regulatory and architectural template for continuous direct-compression of solid oral dosage forms; (b) RTD-gated diversion as a real-time release strategy substitute for batch sampling; (c) feed-forward NIR-driven control loops in pharmaceutical manufacturing.
