## Politecnico di Milano MOSFET E-Tongue Microfluidic Array for Wine Tasting (2017)

- **id**: `polimi-mosfet-etongue-wine`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Politecnico di Milano (Cetó, Pioggia, et al.)
- **disclosure**: Cetó X. et al. 'Voltammetric electronic tongue for wine discrimination using polypyrrole-modified MOSFET sensors in a microfluidic chamber', Sensors and Actuators B 247:1170-1177 (2017) doi:10.1016/j.snb.2017.03.078
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an integrated MOSFET-array electronic tongue beneath a PDMS microfluidic flow chamber, with polypyrrole-functionalized gates trained for wine discrimination. Anticipates: (a) heterogeneous CMOS+PDMS microfluidic e-tongue; (b) microfluidic chamber-array architecture coupled to a multi-MOSFET sensing back-end; (c) wine-region or wine-style discrimination as a microfluidic application. Does not disclose long-term sensor drift compensation.
