## IonSense DART (Direct Analysis in Real Time) Ion Source (2005-03-15)

- **id**: `ionsense-dart`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: IonSense Inc. (acquired by Bruker 2022)
- **disclosure**: Cody, Laramee & Durst, Anal. Chem. 2005, 77, 2297-2302. DOI:10.1021/ac050162j; IonSense Inc. founded 2005; DART SVP commercial launch 2007.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Ambient ionization source generating a heated metastable-He gas stream that ionizes solid/liquid analytes touched into the plume. Microfluidic-relevant content: precise heated gas-flow channel acting as a controlled-pressure microfluidic delivery to the ionization region; the gas-flow geometry IS the ionization mechanism. Discloses: glow-discharge-driven generation of metastable-state He atoms; heated transfer line delivering metastable gas to a sampling region; coupling to TOF or QToF MS via the standard atmospheric-pressure inlet. Anticipates: ambient-ionization MS with no sample prep, no chromatography, and no liquid solvent; the architectural pattern of touch-and-MS for forensic drug screening, food-contaminant detection; gas-flow microfluidic delivery as the ionization mechanism.
