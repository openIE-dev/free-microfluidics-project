## Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA) (1971)

- **id**: `engvall-perlmann-1971-elisa`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Eva Engvall, Peter Perlmann (Stockholm University); independently Van Weemen and Schuurs (Organon)
- **disclosure**: Engvall, E.; Perlmann, P. Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA). Quantitative assay of immunoglobulin G. Immunochemistry 1971, 8, 871-874. DOI: 10.1016/0019-2791(71)90454-X
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Replaced the radioisotope of RIA with an enzyme conjugate (alkaline phosphatase, horseradish peroxidase) generating a colorimetric or fluorogenic product. Discloses the solid-phase sandwich and competitive ELISA topology that virtually every lateral-flow strip, microfluidic immunoassay chip, and bead-based multiplex assay implements at smaller scale. Anticipates: enzyme-amplified immunoassay readout, solid-phase antibody immobilization with washing steps, chromogenic/fluorogenic substrate conversion as the detection step. Microfluidic ELISA-on-chip devices (digital microfluidic ELISA, droplet ELISA, paper ELISA) are miniaturizations of this 1971 method and cannot claim the assay chemistry as novel.

## SUMA Ultramicroanalytical System (1987)

- **id**: `cigb-suma-ultramicroanalytical-system`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Centro de Inmunoensayo (Cuban Center of Immunoassay), Havana
- **disclosure**: Ramirez, M.; et al. SUMA technology and its applications in screening programs. Biotecnologia Aplicada (Cuba) circa 1990s; see also Cuban Center of Immunoassay (Centro de Inmunoensayo) product documentation for the SUMA PR-521 plate reader and UltraMicroELISA plates
- **ip status**: trade-secret
- **prior art notes**: An early and large-scale realization of volume-reduced solid-phase immunoassay: the conventional 96-well ELISA shrunk roughly twenty-fold in sample volume with a fluorogenic readout, deployed as a national screening infrastructure from 1987 onward. Discloses: micro-well-array immunoassay plates at the ~10 microliter scale, fluorogenic-substrate detection optimized for low-volume wells, and integrated reader/plate/reagent system design for mass screening. Relevant prior art against later claims to miniaturized multiwell immunoassay formats, fluorogenic micro-ELISA, and integrated low-volume immunoassay screening systems. Implements the Engvall-Perlmann ELISA chemistry and Yalow-Berson immunoassay topology at reduced scale.
