## Twist Bioscience + Western Digital DNA Storage Demonstration (DNA Data Storage Alliance) (2023-09-26)

- **id**: `twist-western-digital-dna-storage`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Twist Bioscience + Western Digital + DNA Data Storage Alliance
- **disclosure**: DNA Data Storage Alliance whitepaper Preserving Our Digital Legacy: An Introduction to DNA Data Storage 2023-06; SNIA / DNA Data Storage Alliance announcement of joint Twist-WD demonstration 2023-09.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Industry-consortium demonstration of DNA storage interoperability. Twist Bioscience's 9600-well MEMS silicon DNA synthesis chip serves as the write substrate (the wells are micro-reactors for in-situ phosphoramidite synthesis); Western Digital provides the storage-system data-path layer (encoding, addressing, integrity check); commercial nanopore sequencing provides readback. Anticipates: standardized DNA data storage interfaces and addressing schemes; integration of MEMS DNA synthesis chips with industry-standard storage software stacks; the architectural pattern of separate write-substrate (silicon microwell array) + storage-system layer + read-substrate (nanopore) as the canonical DNA storage architecture.
