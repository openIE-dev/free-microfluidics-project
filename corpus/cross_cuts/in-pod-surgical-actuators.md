## Niven Ringworld Autodoc (1970) (1970-10)

- **id**: `niven-1970-ringworld-autodoc`
- **corpus**: fictional
- **device class**: fictional-laboratory
- **creator**: Larry Niven
- **disclosure**: Larry Niven, 'Ringworld' (Ballantine Books, October 1970, ISBN 0-345-02046-4)
- **ip status**: fictional
- **prior art notes**: Among the earliest explicit literary disclosures (1970) of a self-contained, user-operable autodoc that diagnoses and treats a patient via a single immersion in a perfused chamber, with on-board drug compounding and surgical actuation. Element-by-element discloses (a) immersion bath with full-body sensor coverage, (b) on-board drug library and compounding fluidics, (c) actuated surgical end-effectors operating under fluid, (d) closed-loop diagnose-treat-monitor cycle without physician. Anticipates the entire 'medical pod' / consumer autodoc design space across decades of subsequent depictions and contemporary closed-loop infusion / monitoring system R&D. Niven's 1970 publication is one of the earliest defensible disclosure dates for a complete autodoc.

## Tau Earth Caste Medical Pod (Aun'Va Cocoon) (2001)

- **id**: `warhammer-40k-tau-earth-caste-medical-pod`
- **corpus**: fictional
- **device class**: fictional-laboratory
- **creator**: Games Workshop
- **disclosure**: Codex: Tau (Games Workshop, 2001, ISBN 1-84154-156-4); Codex: Tau Empire (Games Workshop, 2006); Fire Warrior novel (Simon Spurrier, Black Library, 2003)
- **ip status**: fictional
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a Tau-engineered (i.e. high-tech, non-mystical, distinctly 'science-fiction medical') immersion pod combining oxygenated nutrient perfusion, in-pod robotic suturing arms, and continuous telemetry uplink to a caste hospital ship. Pods are modular and chain into field hospital units. Anticipates: networked, modular field-hospital pods that combine immersion perfusion, in-pod actuated surgical end-effectors, and telemetry to a remote command/medical hub. Anticipates the 'pop-up ICU' clinical pod architecture with standardized fluid and data interfaces.
