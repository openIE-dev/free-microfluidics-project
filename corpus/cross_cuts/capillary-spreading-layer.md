## Fujifilm DRI-CHEM Veterinary Slide System (1981)

- **id**: `fujifilm-dri-chem-vet-slide`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Fujifilm Corporation
- **disclosure**: Fujifilm DRI-CHEM dry-slide system commercial launch 1981; Fujifilm DRI-CHEM NX500V veterinary launch 2014; US patent 4,250,257 (Fujifilm dry-film slide)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Fujifilm's parallel multilayer dry-film slide architecture, commercially launched alongside Kodak Ektachem and ultimately incorporated into Fujifilm's NX500V veterinary slide-fed analyzer. Each slide is a layered film stack on a PET base where capillary action wicks plasma through the spreading and reagent layers; reflectance through the PET base quantifies color. Anticipates: parallel-development dry-film slide architectures alongside Kodak/Ortho-Clinical platforms; slide-feeding instrument designs handling stacked individual slides one-at-a-time; and the architectural pattern of vendor-locked slide chemistry as a long-lived alternative to liquid-reagent rotor cartridges in veterinary clinical chemistry.

## IDEXX VetTest 8008 Chemistry Analyzer (1992)

- **id**: `idexx-vettest-8008-dry-chemistry`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: IDEXX Laboratories, Inc. (Kodak Ektachem OEM)
- **disclosure**: IDEXX VetTest 8008 product launch (Kodak Ektachem dry-slide platform OEM rebrand) 1992; IDEXX operator's manual; US patent 4,303,407 (multilayer dry slide, Kodak)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Historic veterinary deployment of the Kodak/Ektachem multilayer dry-film slide format (originally disclosed in US 4,303,407): a stack of porous spreading layer, reagent matrix and indicator film on a PET base, where capillary action wicks plasma through the stack and reaction is read by reflectance through the transparent base. Anticipates: single-slide multilayer dry chemistry as the architectural primitive later wrapped in CLIP/rotor cartridges; the layered-functional-zone approach to capillary-driven dry-reagent assays; and reflectance readout through transparent backings.

## Arkray Spotchem EZ SP-4430 Dry-Chemistry Analyzer (2003)

- **id**: `arkray-spotchem-ez-dry-strip`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Arkray, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Arkray Spotchem EZ SP-4430 product launch 2003; Arkray Spotchem operator's manual; US patent 5,234,665 (dry chemistry strip), US patent 6,632,675
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Arkray's flagship Spotchem dry-chemistry tray architecture: a thermoplastic tray holding up to six layered dry-reagent strips, each with spreading layer, reagent layer and indicator film, read by reflectance after capillary plasma application. Anticipates: tray-based dry-strip clinical chemistry where each strip is independently lot-tracked; the OEM lineage that underlies Heska Element DC5X and similar veterinary platforms; and reflectance-photometry-of-dry-strip as a recurring analytical primitive separable from the rotor/cartridge architecture used by competing systems.

## Heska Element DC5X Chemistry Analyzer (2019)

- **id**: `heska-element-dc5x-chemistry`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Heska Corporation (Arkray OEM)
- **disclosure**: Heska Element DC5X product launch 2019 (OEM of Arkray Spotchem family); Heska operator's guide; US patent 5,234,665 (Arkray dry chemistry strip)
