## IsoPlexis IsoLight / IsoSpark Single-Cell Secretome Platform (2017)

- **id**: `isoplexis-isolight-single-cell-secretome`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: IsoPlexis Inc. (Fan lab Yale spinout, acquired by Bruker)
- **disclosure**: IsoPlexis Inc. IsoLight launch 2017; Lu Y., Xue Q. et al. Nature Medicine 21:1378 (2015), doi:10.1038/nm.3973; US patents 9,857,353 and 10,073,091; Bruker acquisition 2023
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: IsoPlexis IsoCode chip discloses a PDMS microchamber array bonded over a glass slide patterned with a spatially encoded antibody barcode (DNA-directed antibody assembly); single cells loaded stochastically secrete cytokines that are captured at the underlying barcode and detected with a multiplex sandwich assay. Anticipates: (i) single-cell secretomic profiling chips with multiplexed antibody barcode floors; (ii) DNA-directed antibody patterning combined with PDMS microchamber arrays for single-cell capture; (iii) the IsoLight automated reader workflow for >30-plex per-cell cytokine readout. Sister to nanostring-cosmx but different mode (functional secretion vs spatial transcriptomics).
