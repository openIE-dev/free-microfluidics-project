## Maxam-Gilbert chemical DNA sequencing (1977)

- **id**: `maxam-gilbert-1977-chemical-sequencing`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Allan Maxam, Walter Gilbert (Harvard University)
- **disclosure**: Maxam, A. M.; Gilbert, W. A new method for sequencing DNA. Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 1977, 74, 560-564. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.74.2.560
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: The chemical-cleavage sequencing method that, alongside Sanger, established that DNA sequence could be read from a size-separated fragment ladder. Although largely superseded by enzymatic methods, it anticipates: base-specific chemical modification chemistry, end-labeling for fragment detection, and high-resolution electrophoretic ladder readout. Relevant prior art for any device claiming chemical-cleavage-based nucleic-acid analysis or footprinting on chip.
