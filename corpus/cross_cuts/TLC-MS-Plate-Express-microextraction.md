## Advion expression CMS Compact Mass Spectrometer (2010)

- **id**: `advion-expression-cms`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Advion Inc.
- **disclosure**: Advion Inc. expression CMS product launch 2010; brochure CMS-DAT-001.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Compact benchtop quadrupole MS aimed at organic-synthesis monitoring and educational use. Microfluidic-relevant content: the Plate Express accessory provides automated extraction of TLC spots into a flowing solvent that is delivered to the ESI source — a focused micro-extraction interface coupling traditional TLC to MS. Discloses: compact quadrupole optimized for benchtop footprint; Plate Express TLC-elution accessory generating ~uL/min solvent flow that extracts and delivers a TLC spot to the MS; APCI / ESI / ASAP ion source flexibility. Anticipates: TLC-MS with automated micro-extraction interface; compact MS for fume-hood-mounted synthesis monitoring; the architectural pattern of single-quadrupole MS as a 'detector for chemists' rather than a centralized analytical instrument.
