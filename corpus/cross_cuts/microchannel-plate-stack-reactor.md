## CompactGTL Microchannel Gas-to-Liquids Reactor (2008)

- **id**: `compactgtl-microchannel-reactor`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: CompactGTL Ltd
- **disclosure**: CompactGTL Ltd corporate disclosures 2008; Petrobras Aracaju demonstration plant commissioning 2010-2011; CompactGTL technology brochure 2012.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Compact gas-to-liquids skid design competing with Velocys for monetization of stranded / associated gas. Discloses: paired steam-methane-reformer (SMR) and Fischer-Tropsch microchannel reactors in a single skid; removable catalyst cassettes for in-situ catalyst replacement without unbolting the diffusion-bonded reactor; designed for 1000-2000 bbl/day modular plant scale. Anticipates: integrated SMR-plus-FT microchannel reactor pairing for stranded-gas GTL; removable catalyst cassettes within diffusion-bonded microchannel reactors enabling in-situ regeneration; modular skid sizing for offshore-platform deployment.

## Velocys SiC-Coated Microchannel Fischer-Tropsch Catalyst Monolith (2009)

- **id**: `velocys-sic-monolith-catalyst`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Velocys plc (formerly Oxford Catalysts; spinout of PNNL/UK CPI work)
- **disclosure**: Tonkovich et al., Catal. Today 2007, 120, 21-29 DOI:10.1016/j.cattod.2006.07.002; Velocys plc Annual Report 2009; ENVIA Energy Oklahoma City plant commissioning 2017.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Velocys microchannel Fischer-Tropsch reactor with SiC-coated cobalt-impregnated catalyst monolith. Discloses: diffusion-bonded stainless-steel microchannel plate stack with alternating reaction channels (containing SiC-washcoated Co catalyst) and coolant channels; intimate thermal coupling between exothermic FT reaction and coolant boiling water; SiC washcoat providing the high-surface-area support for Co metal active sites with enhanced thermal conductivity. Anticipates: SiC-monolith microchannel catalyst structures for highly exothermic catalysis; the architectural pattern of alternating reactive + coolant channels in a diffusion-bonded plate stack; the specific design of SiC washcoat as a high-thermal-conductivity catalyst support enabling near-isothermal microchannel FT operation.
