## Polaroid SX-70 Integral Film Self-Contained Fluid Pod (Historic Microfluidic Prior Art) (1972)

- **id**: `polaroid-sx-70-fluid-pod-1972`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Polaroid Corporation (Edwin H. Land)
- **disclosure**: Land E.H. 'An experimental study of the photographic processes' Photographic Science and Engineering 16:247 (1972); US3415644A 'Photographic products' Land 1968; Polaroid SX-70 product launch 1972-04-25
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a self-contained, factory-sealed reagent pod laminated into each integral film unit, ruptured and spread by motorized rollers as the unit is ejected, releasing a viscous monobath chemistry that develops, couples, and stabilizes the image in seconds. This is foundational microfluidic prior art: (a) blister-pod reagent storage with mechanical rupture; (b) roller-spread laminar chemistry deposition; (c) self-contained one-shot point-of-use chemistry on a consumable cartridge; (d) factory-sealed wet chemistry that survives shelf life and activates on demand. Predates and anticipates many lab-on-chip cartridge architectures (BioFire, GeneXpert, ID NOW). Strong primary literature anchor and patent.

## Polaroid Spectra Integral Film Fluid Pod (1986)

- **id**: `polaroid-spectra-1986`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Polaroid Corporation
- **disclosure**: Polaroid Corporation product launch 'Spectra System' (1986); US4663296A Polaroid integral film unit
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a wider-format integral film unit with a redesigned reagent pod and roller-spread chemistry geometry derived from the SX-70 architecture. Anticipates: (a) format-scaling of blister-pod consumables; (b) improved laminar-spread geometry for wide cartridges; (c) successor-generation pod chemistry. Distinct from SX-70 by format and chemistry detail.

## Fujifilm Instax Mini Integral Film Fluid Pod (1998)

- **id**: `fujifilm-instax-fluid-pod`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Fuji Photo Film Co. Ltd
- **disclosure**: Fuji Photo Film Co. Ltd press release 'Instax Mini camera and film system' 1998-11
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a Fujifilm-original integral instant film unit using a sealed reagent pod ruptured by camera-internal rollers to spread monobath chemistry across the film, with chemistry tuned for ambient-light development. Anticipates: (a) Polaroid-lineage blister-pod cartridge as a still-current commercial format; (b) compact-format pod-rupture geometry; (c) ambient-light-tolerant monobath chemistry. Continued commercial relevance reinforces the microfluidic prior-art chain.
