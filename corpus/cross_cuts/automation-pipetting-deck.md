## Diagenode IP-Star Compact Automated Magnetic-Bead System (2010)

- **id**: `diagenode-ipstar-magnetic-bead-automation`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Diagenode SA
- **disclosure**: Diagenode IP-Star Compact launch 2010; product manual ME-IP-Star-Compact_v3.1; US patent 8,956,545
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: IP-Star Compact discloses an automated magnetic-bead handler tailored to chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP) and DNA methylation workflows, with automated incubation, washing, elution and reagent dispensing in low-volume strip tubes. Anticipates: (i) ChIP-specific automated bead-handling cartridges; (ii) low-volume bead-handling for limited starting material epigenomic prep; (iii) integration of incubation thermal control with bead handling.

## Agilent Magnis NGS Prep System (2019)

- **id**: `siemens-magnis-dx-ngs-prep`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Agilent Technologies
- **disclosure**: Agilent Technologies Magnis NGS Prep launch press release 2019; Magnis NGS Prep technical overview 5994-0719EN
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Magnis NGS Prep discloses an integrated cartridge-and-instrument system that automates the full NGS library prep workflow including fragmentation, end repair, adapter ligation, and SureSelect bait-based target capture, all driven by automated pipetting on a single consumable deck with magnetic-bead handling. Anticipates: (i) walk-away NGS library prep cartridges with on-board target enrichment; (ii) integrated magnetic-bead pipetting decks for NGS workflows; (iii) cartridge-encoded reagent traceability for clinical NGS prep. Note: Siemens Healthineers MAGNIS DX is a distinct product; this is the Agilent Magnis NGS Prep, the more commonly known platform.

## Tecan Resolvex i300 Bead Handling System (2021)

- **id**: `tecan-resolvex-i300-magnetic`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Tecan Group AG
- **disclosure**: Tecan Group AG Resolvex i300 launch press release 2021; product brochure 30178459 v1.0
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Resolvex i300 discloses an automation deck combining positive-pressure solid-phase extraction (SPE) with magnetic-bead handling and pipetting, allowing combined SPE-bead workflows for clinical sample prep. Anticipates: (i) hybrid SPE-plus-magnetic-bead automation deck designs; (ii) integration of positive-pressure manifolds with Tecan Fluent robotics; (iii) 96-well combined SPE/bead workflows. Distinct from KingFisher (no positive-pressure SPE) and Maxwell RSC (rod, not deck).
