## Lipoid GmbH pharmaceutical-grade phospholipid manufacturing (Lipoid / Phospholipon) (1973)

- **id**: `lipoid-gmbh-pharmaceutical-lipid-manufacturing`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: Lipoid GmbH
- **disclosure**: Lipoid GmbH (founded 1973, Ludwigshafen) pharmaceutical-grade lecithin and synthetic phospholipid product literature; Phospholipon and Lipoid grades technical datasheets.
- **ip status**: trade-secret
- **prior art notes**: Documents GMP-grade phospholipid and PEG-lipid feedstocks (DSPC, DMG-PEG2000, purified PC) as the raw materials consumed by microfluidic liposome and lipid-nanoparticle assembly devices. Establishes that defined-composition synthetic lipid blends were commercially available for microfluidic nanoparticle manufacture, relevant to claims tying particular lipid identities to LNP/liposome composition.

## Avanti Polar Lipids Mini-Extruder / LIPEX large-volume extruder (1985)

- **id**: `avanti-polar-lipids-mini-extruder`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Avanti Polar Lipids (now Croda); LIPEX from Northern Lipids / Transferra Nanosciences
- **disclosure**: Olson F. et al., Preparation of liposomes of defined size distribution by extrusion through polycarbonate membranes, Biochim Biophys Acta 557:9-23 (1979); commercial Avanti Mini-Extruder / Lipex extruder from ~1985.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses production of size-defined liposomes by repeatedly forcing multilamellar vesicles through a track-etched nanoporous polycarbonate membrane under pressure, with pore size setting vesicle diameter - a nanofluidic membrane-extrusion process. Anticipates claims to liposome manufacture by membrane extrusion of defined pore size, to handheld two-syringe extruders, and to scaled barrel/cartridge extruders for liposomal drug and adjuvant manufacture. Upstream prior art for gsk-as01-liposomal-adjuvant-system.

## GSK AS01 liposomal adjuvant system (AS01B / AS01E) (2009)

- **id**: `gsk-as01-liposomal-adjuvant-system`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: droplet-generator
- **creator**: GSK (GlaxoSmithKline Biologicals / GSK Vaccines)
- **disclosure**: Garcon N. et al., Recent clinical experience with vaccines using MPL- and QS-21-containing adjuvant systems, Expert Rev Vaccines 8(11):1459-1476 (2009).
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses ~100 nm cholesterol-containing liposomes co-presenting MPL (TLR4 agonist) and QS-21 saponin manufactured by microfluidic extrusion/homogenization with cholesterol used to attenuate QS-21 lytic activity. Anticipates claims to MPL+QS-21 liposomal adjuvants of defined size, saponin-toxicity quenching by membrane sterol, and recombinant subunit vaccines (zoster gE, RSV F, RTS,S) adjuvanted therewith. Liposome-production process anticipated by avanti-polar-lipids-mini-extruder and microfluidics-corp-microfluidizer-high-shear-processor.

## Novavax Matrix-M saponin nanoparticle adjuvant (2012)

- **id**: `novavax-matrix-m-saponin-nanoparticle-adjuvant`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: droplet-generator
- **creator**: Novavax AB (formerly Isconova AB; ISCOM lineage from Morein)
- **disclosure**: Reimer J.M. et al., Matrix-M adjuvant induces local recruitment activation and maturation of central immune cells in absence of antigen, PLoS ONE 7(7):e41451 (2012); platform from Isconova AB / Novavax AB (ISCOM-Matrix lineage from Morein).
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses ~40 nm self-assembled saponin/cholesterol/phospholipid nanocages built from two purified Quillaja saponin fractions produced by detergent-removal/controlled-mixing assembly used as a vaccine adjuvant. Anticipates claims to two-fraction Quillaja-saponin nanoparticle adjuvants and to recombinant subunit vaccines (SARS-CoV-2 spike NVX-CoV2373, R21 CSP) adjuvanted therewith. The controlled-mixing assembly step is anticipated by microfluidic nanoparticle-formulation prior art.
