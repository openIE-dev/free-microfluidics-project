## Lab on a Chip launch editorial (RSC, 2001) (2001-05)

- **corpus**: academic

- **creator**: Andreas Manz, Holger Becker (RSC)
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1039/B105314F; Lab Chip 1, 1-2 (2001) — Manz & Becker editorial 'Lab on a Chip: A new opportunity for the analytical chemist'
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Inaugural editorial of the Royal Society of Chemistry's Lab on a Chip journal, by the originators of the µTAS concept. Functions as a published roadmap document declaring the field's scope: integrated chemical and biological analysis on planar substrates using microfabricated channels, valves, mixers, separators, and detectors. By 2001 disclosure-genus, this editorial is citable to defeat any later attempt to patent 'an integrated lab-on-chip system' as a novel concept.
