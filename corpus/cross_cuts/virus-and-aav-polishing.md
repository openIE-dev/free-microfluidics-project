## BIA Separations CIM (Convective Interaction Media) Monolithic Column (1998)

- **id**: `sartorius-bia-cim-monolith`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: BIA Separations d.o.o. (Sartorius Stedim Biotech subsidiary since 2020)
- **disclosure**: BIA Separations CIM launch 1998; Sartorius acquisition of BIA Separations 2020-10-19; CIMmultus QA product datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an in-situ polymerized polymethacrylate monolith with macroporous channel structure providing convective mass transport, used for large-biomolecule purification (virus, AAV, mRNA, plasmid DNA) where conventional bead diffusion is mass-transport-limited. Anticipates: monolithic chromatography for large-biomolecule purification in CGT and mRNA manufacturing; specifically the architectural pattern of a single-piece polymerized matrix with convective channels avoiding the resin-bead pore-diffusion limitation that constrains throughput for >MDa-scale biomolecules. Critical for AAV, lentivirus, and large-mRNA polishing where bead resins underperform.
