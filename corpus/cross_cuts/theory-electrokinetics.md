- **prior art notes**: Comprehensive Annual Reviews chapter cataloguing the physics governing microfluidics for biology: laminar flow, diffusional mixing, electrokinetic phenomena, capillarity, two-phase flow, and surface chemistry; with applied examples in cell handling, immunoassays, PCR, and DNA analysis. Very high-density bibliographic entry; cited 1000+ times in subsequent prior-art landscapes.

- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Patrick Tabeling
- **disclosure**: ISBN 978-0198568643; Oxford University Press, 2005
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Foundational graduate textbook by ESPCI's Patrick Tabeling. Systematically covers low-Re hydrodynamics, diffusion, capillarity, electrokinetics, two-phase flow, and surface chemistry as applied to microfluidic devices; with worked engineering examples. As a textbook, the engineering descriptions of the underlying physics and unit operations are unencumbered prior art (copyright attaches only to the specific text). A standard examiner reference.
