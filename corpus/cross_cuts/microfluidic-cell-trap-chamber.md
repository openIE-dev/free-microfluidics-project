## Quantum Diamond Technologies NV-Diamond Cancer-Cell Magnetic-Bead Detector (2017-08-08)

- **id**: `lukin-quantum-diamond-technologies`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: nanofluidic-chip
- **creator**: Quantum Diamond Technologies Inc. (Harvard Lukin / Walsworth spinout)
- **disclosure**: Glenn et al. (Lukin lab Harvard) Nat. Commun. 2018, 9, 33. DOI:10.1038/s41467-017-02398-z; Quantum Diamond Technologies Inc. founded 2017; SBIR NIH contract 2018.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Commercial NV-diamond cell-detection platform from Lukin / Walsworth lab spinout. Discloses: NV-diamond substrate with microfluidic chamber for liquid biopsy sample; antibody-conjugated magnetic beads selectively label target cells (e.g. CTCs, infected cells); NV magnetometer detects bead-labeled cells with single-cell sensitivity over a wide field of view. Anticipates: NV-diamond magnetic detection in clinical cell-counting applications; the architectural pattern of diamond-substrate + microfluidic-cell-chamber as a quantum-enabled liquid-biopsy platform; combination of magnetic bead labeling with quantum-sensor readout in a single integrated cartridge.
