## Aptar Pharma Plastic and Elastomer Closures (2015)

- **id**: `aptar-pharma-closures`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: AptarGroup, Inc. (Aptar Pharma)
- **disclosure**: Aptar Pharma PremiumCoat launch 2015; Aptar Pharma closure portfolio datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an ETFE-film-laminated elastomer closure (PremiumCoat) providing barrier performance via film lamination rather than spray coating, achieving more uniform barrier coverage than competitors' coated stoppers. Anticipates: film-laminated closure architectures for biopharma fill-finish where ETFE / PTFE film provides more uniform barrier coverage than spray-coated alternatives; specifically the architectural pattern of pre-laminated barrier film bonded during compression molding rather than post-molding coating, eliminating coating-coverage defects. Differentiates Aptar from West and Datwyler in the closure market.
