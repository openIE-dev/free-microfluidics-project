## Microsoft / University of Washington DNA Storage Research Demonstrator (2016-04-07)

- **id**: `microsoft-uw-dna-storage`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Microsoft Research + University of Washington Molecular Information Systems Lab (Strauss/Ceze)
- **disclosure**: Bornholt et al., ASPLOS 2016, A DNA-Based Archival Storage System. DOI:10.1145/2872362.2872397; Takahashi et al., Sci. Rep. 2019, 9, 1-9 (Demonstration of end-to-end automation of DNA data storage).
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: First demonstration of fully automated end-to-end DNA data storage workflow integrating commercial DNA synthesis (write), microfluidic reagent handling (sample prep + amplification), and nanopore sequencing (read). Discloses: PCB-based electromagnetic-actuated fluidic system performing all between-step sample handling autonomously; encoding/decoding software pipeline; integration architecture connecting Twist DNA synthesis to MinION nanopore readback through a custom microfluidic preparation module. Anticipates: end-to-end automated DNA archival storage systems; the architectural pattern of commercial-synth-as-writer + custom-microfluidic-prep + commercial-nanopore-as-reader; encoding schemes optimized for DNA storage error profiles.
