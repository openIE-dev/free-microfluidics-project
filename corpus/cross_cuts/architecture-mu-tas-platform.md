## Manz et al. 1992 — Planar chips technology for miniaturization of separation systems (first electrophoretic separation on chip) (1992)

- **id**: `manz-1992-electrophoretic-separation-on-chip`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Andreas Manz, Daniel J. Harrison, Elisabeth Verpoorte, Carlo S. Effenhauser, Albert Paulus, Hans Lüdi, H. Michael Widmer
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1016/0021-9673(92)80293-4; J. Chromatogr. 593, 253-258 (1992)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: First demonstration of an electrophoretic separation performed on a planar microfabricated chip. Predates Harrison 1992/1993 Anal. Chem. coverage of capillary electrophoresis on chip. Anticipates: any patent claim to 'a microfluidic chip comprising microfabricated channels in a planar substrate for electrophoretic separation of analytes' filed after 1992. Foundational disclosure for the entire chip-CE industry.

## Manz, Becker & Lee 1998 — Microsystem Technology in Chemistry and Life Sciences (textbook) (1998-12)

- **id**: `manz-becker-1998-microsystem-technology-textbook`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Andreas Manz, Holger Becker (eds.)
- **disclosure**: ISBN 978-3540655558; Springer Topics in Current Chemistry vol 194, 1998
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Edited Springer volume by the originators of µTAS/lab-on-a-chip; chapters cover microreaction technology, capillary electrophoresis chips, miniaturized analysis, and integrated bio-detection. By 1998 disclosure-genus, this volume time-stamps the integrated chemical-and-life-science 'lab on chip' platform concept in book-of-record form. Anticipates: any post-1998 patent claim to an 'integrated microsystem for chemical or biological analysis' as obvious; supplements the Manz 1990 Sensors and Actuators paper.

## Lab on a Chip launch editorial (RSC, 2001) (2001-05)

- **id**: `lab-on-chip-2001-launch-editorial`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Andreas Manz, Holger Becker (RSC)
- **disclosure**: doi:10.1039/B105314F; Lab Chip 1, 1-2 (2001) — Manz & Becker editorial 'Lab on a Chip: A new opportunity for the analytical chemist'
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Inaugural editorial of the Royal Society of Chemistry's Lab on a Chip journal, by the originators of the µTAS concept. Functions as a published roadmap document declaring the field's scope: integrated chemical and biological analysis on planar substrates using microfabricated channels, valves, mixers, separators, and detectors. By 2001 disclosure-genus, this editorial is citable to defeat any later attempt to patent 'an integrated lab-on-chip system' as a novel concept. Critically, it establishes the journal of record whose archive then continuously time-stamps disclosures of every subsequent micro-platform.
