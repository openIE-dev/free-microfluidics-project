## Berkeley Lights Beacon Optoelectronic Positioning Patent Family (2010)

- **id**: `berkeley-lights-beacon-oet-patent-family`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Berkeley Lights, Inc.
- **disclosure**: US7956339B2 'Single-sided lateral-field and phototransistor-based optoelectronic tweezers apparatus' (priority 2007); US8921055B2; US9533879B2; Berkeley Lights Beacon platform launch 2016
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Beacon platform's optoelectronic positioning (OEP) chip: a photoconductive amorphous-silicon layer sandwiched between transparent electrodes, where a digital micromirror device (DMD) projects light patterns that locally modulate the photoconductor and create position-controllable dielectrophoretic (DEP) traps. Anticipates: (a) any cell-handling system using projector-driven DEP for single-cell manipulation in microfluidic chambers; (b) integrated culture-and-export workflows where individual cells are penned, fed, and selectively exported by light-patterned forces; (c) photoconductive substrates compatible with both negative and positive DEP at MHz frequencies. Foundational anchor: Chiou, Ohta & Wu 2005 Nature paper on OET (already in corpus).
