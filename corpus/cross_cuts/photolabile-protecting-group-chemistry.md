## Fodor 1991 Affymax/Affymetrix Light-Directed Microarray Patent (1991-02-15)

- **id**: `fodor-1991-affymax-microarray-patent`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Stephen P. A. Fodor et al. (Affymax / Affymetrix)
- **disclosure**: Fodor et al. 'Light-directed, spatially addressable parallel chemical synthesis' Science 251:767-773 (1991-02-15) doi:10.1126/science.1990438; US5143854 'Light-directed, spatially addressable parallel chemical synthesis' (priority 1989, issued 1992)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Foundational 1991 patent for light-directed combinatorial in-situ chemical synthesis on solid supports, in which photolabile protecting groups on growing oligo chains are selectively removed by patterned UV illumination through a photomask, enabling parallel synthesis of millions of unique sequences in a single chip. Anticipates: (a) any in-situ-synthesized DNA microarray architecture using mask- or DMD-based photolithography; (b) the architectural pattern of using semiconductor lithography tools to manufacture biological probe arrays; (c) photolabile NPPOC/MeNPOC protecting group chemistries adapted for nucleic-acid synthesis. The Affymetrix GeneChip product line and downstream Roche NimbleGen, Agilent SurePrint, and Twist silicon-DRIE arrays trace back to this disclosure. Patent expired 2010.
