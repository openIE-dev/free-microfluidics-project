## Wyatt Technology DAWN HELEOS Multi-Angle Light Scattering for LNP (2007)

- **id**: `wyatt-dawn-heleos-lnp-mals`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Wyatt Technology Corporation (Waters Corp.)
- **disclosure**: Wyatt DAWN HELEOS product launch 2007; Wyatt acquisition by Waters 2023; DAWN HELEOS-II datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an 18-angle MALS detector flow cell coupled in-line with SEC or AF4 separation, providing absolute molar mass and root-mean-square radius measurement for LNP populations. Anticipates: SEC-MALS and AF4-MALS LNP characterization workflows providing absolute size and molar mass without calibration standards; the AF4-MALS combination is the gold-standard CMC release assay for mRNA-LNP heterogeneity analysis. Distinct from Refeyn (single-particle) and Malvern (ensemble batch DLS) by providing absolute molar mass distribution.
