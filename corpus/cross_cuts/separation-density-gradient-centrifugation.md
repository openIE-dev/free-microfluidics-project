## Beckman Coulter Avanti J-30I high-performance centrifuge (2002)

- **id**: `beckman-avanti-j-30i`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: separator-component
- **creator**: Beckman Coulter
- **disclosure**: Beckman Coulter Avanti J-30I launch (2002); https://www.beckman.com/centrifuges/high-performance/avanti-j; product datasheet IM-3.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a high-performance preparative floor-standing centrifuge achieving 30,000 rpm with 6 L bottle-rotor capacity (JLA-8.1000) and fixed-angle rotors to 108,860 g, supporting cell-paste harvest, bulk LNP and AAV pre-clarification, and crude polishing steps that precede ultracentrifugation. - **device class**: separator-component
