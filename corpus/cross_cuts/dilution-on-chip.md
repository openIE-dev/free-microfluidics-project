## Anchor Beer (Asia Pacific Breweries) Fermentation Microfluidic Monitoring System (2015)

- **id**: `anchor-beer-fermentation-microfluidic-monitoring`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Asia Pacific Breweries Singapore (Heineken Asia Pacific) with SIMTech
- **disclosure**: Asia Pacific Breweries technical poster, World Brewing Congress 2016 'Inline microfluidic monitoring of fermentation extract and ethanol at the Tuas Brewery'; Singapore Institute of Manufacturing Technology project 1421-FERM final report (2015)
- **ip status**: trade-secret
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a microfluidic side-stream sampler off a 1000 hL fermenter at the Tuas Brewery, automatically diluting wort, transferring it through a PDMS-glass micro-flow cell for NIR transmission spectroscopy, and reporting Plato extract and ethanol every five minutes. Anticipates: (a) side-stream microfluidic flow cells for continuous fermentation monitoring; (b) inline dilution-and-spectrometry on a single microchip device; (c) brewery deployment of a pressure-driven lab-on-chip subsystem to displace daily off-line laboratory analysis. Does not disclose chip-level CIP cleaning protocol.
