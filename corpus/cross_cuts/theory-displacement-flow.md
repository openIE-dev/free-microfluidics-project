## Saffman & Taylor 1958 — The penetration of a fluid into a porous medium or Hele-Shaw cell containing a more viscous liquid (viscous fingering) (1958-06-24)

- **id**: `saffman-taylor-1958-viscous-fingering`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Philip Geoffrey Saffman, Geoffrey Ingram Taylor
- **disclosure**: P.G. Saffman, G.I. Taylor, 'The penetration of a fluid into a porous medium or Hele-Shaw cell containing a more viscous liquid', Proc. Roy. Soc. A 245, 312-329 (1958); doi:10.1098/rspa.1958.0085
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Foundational analysis of the interfacial instability when a less-viscous fluid displaces a more-viscous fluid in a Hele-Shaw cell or porous medium. Anticipates: microfluidic patents claiming 'controlled fingering for emulsion generation', 'pattern formation in microchannels', or 'porous-media-on-chip' devices — the underlying instability and its scaling are 68 years old. Critical reference for any displacement-flow microfluidic patent (chip-based EOR studies, blood-plasma separation by displacement, etc.).
