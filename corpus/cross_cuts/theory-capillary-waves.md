## Kelvin 1871 — Hydrokinetic solutions and observations (capillary waves and surface tension) (1871)

- **id**: `kelvin-1871-capillary-waves`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: William Thomson, 1st Baron Kelvin
- **disclosure**: W. Thomson (Lord Kelvin), 'Hydrokinetic solutions and observations', Phil. Mag. ser. 4 vol 42, 362-377 (1871)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Kelvin's analysis of capillary-gravity waves on liquid surfaces; establishes the dispersion relation for surface waves under combined surface-tension and gravity. Foundational for: any acoustofluidic device exploiting Faraday waves on a liquid film, capillary-wave-based atomization, or surface-acoustic-wave (SAW) liquid manipulation where capillary waves on the droplet surface participate. Anticipates: capillary-wave atomizer claims and surface-wave-driven micro-mixing claims — the dispersion relation is 155 years old.
