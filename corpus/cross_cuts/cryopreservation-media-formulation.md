## BioLife Solutions CryoStor CS5 / CS10 cryopreservation media (2003)

- **id**: `biolife-cryostor-cs10`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: BioLife Solutions Inc.
- **disclosure**: BioLife Solutions CryoStor product launch; https://biolifesolutions.com/products/cryostor/; US7270946B2; US8709797B2.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses serum-free, protein-free, animal-component-free DMSO-containing cell-cryopreservation media (CS5 with 5% DMSO and CS10 with 10% DMSO) optimized for autologous and allogeneic CAR-T, MSC, iPSC, and other CGT cell products, with cGMP master file supporting IND/BLA submissions. Anticipates: chemically-defined cryopreservation media as drug-substance-grade reagents for CGT manufacturing; specifically the formulation pattern of intracellular-mimicking ionic balance combined with DMSO at 5-10% as a defined cryoprotectant matrix replacing the historical FBS+DMSO formulation. Foundational prior art for CGT manufacturing patents asserting closed-system cryopreservation workflows that depend on a defined cryopreservation reagent.
