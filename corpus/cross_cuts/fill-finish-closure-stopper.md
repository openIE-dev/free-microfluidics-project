- **id**: `west-novapure-vist-closures`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: West Pharmaceutical Services, Inc. (Daikyo Seiko partnership)
- **disclosure**: West NovaPure platform launch 2010; West Vist polymer closure launch 2013; West NovaPure datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the NovaPure tight-tolerance halobutyl closure platform with FluroTec barrier coating, deployed at scale for COVID-19 mRNA vaccine fill-finish. ## Aptar Pharma Plastic and Elastomer Closures (2015)

- **id**: `aptar-pharma-closures`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: consumable-bulk
- **creator**: AptarGroup, Inc. (Aptar Pharma)
- **disclosure**: Aptar Pharma PremiumCoat launch 2015; Aptar Pharma closure portfolio datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
