## EPO Espacenet Worldwide Patent Search (1998)

- **id**: `espacenet-epo-patent-search`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: European Patent Office
- **disclosure**: European Patent Office Espacenet public launch 1998; URL: worldwide.espacenet.com; current dataset covers 130M+ patent documents from 100+ jurisdictions
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Espacenet is the EPO's worldwide patent search platform, providing free access to 130M+ patent documents with machine translation in 30+ languages. Material to defensive publication because: (a) it is the canonical access point for European patent and EP-equivalent family member records; (b) its INPADOC-DOCDB family expansion is the gold standard for international family identification; (c) the Patent Translate function (powered by EPO+Google) enables retrieval of substantive prior art from JP, CN, KR, RU patents. Inclusion in commons supports recommendation as a required step in any defensive-publication search workflow targeting non-US/non-English prior art.

## Lens.org / Cambia Open Patent Search Platform (2000)

- **id**: `lens-org-cambia-open-patent-search`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Cambia / Lens.org
- **disclosure**: Cambia / Lens.org launched 2000 (originally as 'Patent Lens'); current platform: lens.org; founded by Richard Jefferson at Cambia (CSIRO spin-out)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Lens.org is a non-profit public patent (and scholarly literature) search platform that uniquely integrates patent records with PubMed-style scholarly literature linkage and ORCID author resolution. Material to defensive publication because: (a) it is one of very few platforms that lets searchers correlate scholarly publications with corresponding patent filings via author/inventor matching; (b) its 'Patent Family Map' visualization is essential for tracing international family chains; (c) Cambia's BiOS open license framework, hosted on the same platform, has historically supported agricultural-biotech and microfluidic open licensing. Includes free CC-BY exports of patent data, suitable for incorporation into defensive-publication corpora like this one.

## WIPO PATENTSCOPE Search Tool (2008)

- **id**: `wipo-patentscope-search-tool`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)
- **disclosure**: World Intellectual Property Organization PATENTSCOPE database public launch 2008; URL: patentscope.wipo.int; current dataset covers 100M+ patent records from 70+ national/regional offices
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: PATENTSCOPE is the canonical international patent search interface providing free public access to PCT applications and ~70 national patent collections including CN, JP, KR, RU, IN, BR. Material to defensive publication because: (a) it is the primary mechanism for retrieving non-English patent prior art that may anticipate later filings; (b) it provides chemistry/sequence search for biotech filings; (c) its full-text translation capability enables global prior-art searches needed for cross-jurisdiction defensive contentions. Tool itself is public-domain access infrastructure; documenting its existence and capabilities supports its use as a recommended search step in any commons-grade prior-art investigation.

## USPTO Patent Public Search (PPUBS) Tool (2022-02-01)

- **id**: `uspto-ppubs-patent-public-search-tool`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: United States Patent and Trademark Office
- **disclosure**: USPTO Patent Public Search public launch 2022-02-01 replacing legacy PatFT and AppFT; URL: ppubs.uspto.gov
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: PPUBS is the USPTO's modern free public search interface replacing PatFT/AppFT. It exposes the same Boolean operators, classification (CPC) browsing, and image PDF retrieval that USPTO examiners use internally. Material to defensive publication because: (a) it is the canonical primary source for US patent text after Feb 2022; (b) its classification-tree navigation supports 'CPC peer review' methodology required for thorough prior-art searches; (c) it provides reverse-citation data critical for tracing patent family chains. Documenting its existence in this commons supports its inclusion as a recommended step in commons-grade prior-art investigations.
