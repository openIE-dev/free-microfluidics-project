## Abbott PRISM chemiluminescent immunoassay blood-donor screening system (2005)

- **id**: `abbott-prism-chemiluminescent-blood-screening`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Abbott Laboratories (Diagnostics Division)
- **disclosure**: Abbott PRISM System operator manual; FDA approval of PRISM HBsAg and PRISM HIV O Plus assays ~2006-2008; Abbott Diagnostics product literature.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a continuous-conveyor blood-donor serology screening analyzer that transports discrete coated-solid-phase reaction wells through serial wash/reagent stations to an acridinium chemiluminescence reader. Anticipates claims to high-throughput continuous-flow chemiluminescent immunoassay screening of donor plasma for multiple transfusion-transmissible agents. Predecessor in the Abbott blood-screening line to abbott-alinity-s-blood-screening.

## Grifols Procleix Panther NAT blood-donor screening system (2014)

- **id**: `grifols-procleix-panther-nat-blood-screening`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Grifols Diagnostic Solutions (assay) / Hologic (Panther instrument)
- **disclosure**: Grifols / Hologic Procleix Panther System operator manual; CE mark 2014, FDA approval of Procleix Ultrio Elite assay on Panther 2015-2017.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a random-access blood-donor screening platform that performs magnetic-bead target capture, isothermal transcription-mediated amplification, and real-time chemiluminescent hybridization-protection detection in sealed single-use vessels on a continuous-load track. Anticipates claims to integrated TMA-based NAT blood screening, dual-kinetic chemiluminescent readout, and consolidated random-access infectious-disease screening of plasma. Related to hologic-panther-fusion-cartridge (same instrument family).

## Abbott Alinity s blood-donor screening system (2018)

- **id**: `abbott-alinity-s-blood-screening`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Abbott Laboratories (Diagnostics Division)
- **disclosure**: Abbott Alinity s System operator manual; CE mark 2018; Abbott Diagnostics Alinity family product literature.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a random-access blood-screening analyzer using paramagnetic-microparticle capture, magnetic separation, and chemiluminescent triggering in disposable reaction vessels with integrated robotic sample/reagent handling. Successor to abbott-prism-chemiluminescent-blood-screening; shares architecture with abbott-alinity-i-immunoassay-fluidics.
