- **device class**: mixer-component

- **disclosure**: Knauer NanoProducer product launch press release 2022-04; Knauer datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a process-scale LNP encapsulation system using an impingement-jet mixer driven by Knauer's Azura HPLC pumps, providing an alternative geometric architecture to staggered-herringbone (PNI) and T-mixer (BioNTech-Marburg-style) LNP mixers.
