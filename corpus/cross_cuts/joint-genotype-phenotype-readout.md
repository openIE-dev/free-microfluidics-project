- **disclosure**: WO2021113505A1 'Methods for single cell DNA and protein analysis' (priority 2019-12); Mission Bio Tapestri Multi-omics product release 2020

- **prior art notes**: Discloses simultaneous capture of single-cell genotype (targeted DNA panel) and surface protein expression (oligo-conjugated antibody panel a la CITE-seq/AbSeq) in the Tapestri droplet workflow.
