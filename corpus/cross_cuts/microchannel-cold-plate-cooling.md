## Lockheed Martin AEGIS / SPY-1 / SPY-6 Radar Microchannel Cooling (1998)

- **id**: `lockheed-martin-aegis-microchannel-cooling`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: cooling-substrate
- **creator**: Lockheed Martin Corporation
- **disclosure**: Bar-Cohen et al., IEEE Trans. Adv. Packag. 2008, 31, 723-733 DOI:10.1109/TADVP.2008.2010253 (review of high-power radar cooling); Lockheed Martin AEGIS open-publications 1998 onward.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Cold-plate microchannel cooling for AEGIS shipboard radar T/R modules. Discloses: EDM- or skive-fabricated microchannel cold plate with sub-mm channel widths providing convective cooling; PAO (polyalphaolefin) or glycol-water coolant loops; manifold distribution to per-T/R-module cold plates. Anticipates: liquid microchannel cooling of high-power active electronically scanned array (AESA) radar; the architectural pattern of cold-plate-per-module + central pump skid that has become standard in Naval and Aegis-class radar; high heat flux >200 W/cm^2 single-phase microchannel cooling.

## Raytheon AESA Radar T/R Module Microchannel Cooling (2005)

- **id**: `raytheon-aesa-microchannel-cooling`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: cooling-substrate
- **creator**: Raytheon Technologies (now RTX)
- **disclosure**: Bar-Cohen & Iyengar, IEEE Trans. Compon. Packag. Technol. 2003, 26, 6-25; Raytheon Technologies AESA radar press materials.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Microchannel cold plates bonded directly to GaN T/R module substrates for fighter and missile-defense radar. Discloses: diffusion-bonded copper-or-CuMo cold plate with sub-mm microchannels machined or etched at the die-attach interface; PAO coolant manifolds per LRU; direct heat-spreader integration with GaN amplifier dies. Anticipates: diffusion-bonded microchannel cold plates as the standard solution for GaN AESA cooling; integrated die-attach + cold-plate co-fabrication for >200 W/cm^2 power densities; the architectural pattern of co-designed microchannel + GaN MMIC for advanced AESA radars.

## F-35 Lightning II AESA + Avionics Microchannel Liquid Cooling (2006)

- **id**: `f-35-microchannel-cooling`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: cooling-substrate
- **creator**: Lockheed Martin Aeronautics (lead) + Pratt & Whitney + BAE Systems + Northrop Grumman
- **disclosure**: Avant & Morgan, AIAA-2006-7937 (F-35 thermal management); Lockheed Martin F-35 fact sheets; Bar-Cohen et al. IEEE Trans. Adv. Packag. 2008.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: F-35 Lightning II uses centralized PAO coolant loop with microchannel cold plates at every high-power electronics LRU including APG-81 AESA radar, electro-optical targeting, distributed aperture system, and main mission processors; heat is ultimately rejected to fuel as the airframe heat sink. Discloses: distributed cold-plate architecture across multiple LRUs with shared coolant loop; microchannel cold plates bonded to GaAs-or-GaN AESA T/R modules; PAO-to-fuel heat exchanger as primary heat rejection (with secondary air cycle). Anticipates: airframe-scale microchannel liquid cooling architectures for fighter aircraft; fuel-as-ultimate-heat-sink pattern; the integrated architectural choice of centralized PAO loop + per-LRU microchannel cold plate that has shaped subsequent fighter (F-15EX, NGAD) thermal designs.
