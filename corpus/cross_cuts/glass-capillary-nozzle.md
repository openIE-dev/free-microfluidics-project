## Microdrop Technologies Piezo Microdispenser Heads (1995)

- **id**: `microdrop-technologies-microdispenser`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: dispenser-pipettor
- **creator**: microdrop Technologies GmbH
- **disclosure**: microdrop Technologies GmbH (Norderstedt, Germany) founded 1995, MD-K-130 dispenser datasheet; microdrop publications in Sensors and Actuators 1996-2000
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-drop microdispenser using the squeeze-tube architecture (predates Zoltan only conceptually but with a glass-capillary-and-PZT-tube refinement specifically for laboratory dispensing). The architectural primitive is the single-nozzle, single-drop-on-demand piezo squeeze of a borosilicate-glass capillary with a tapered orifice - allowing repeatable 30-100pL deposition of any low-viscosity fluid including biologicals. Anticipates: laboratory single-drop dispensers using PZT squeeze-tube actuation of glass capillaries for picoliter dispensing, including the use of stroboscopic illumination for closed-loop drop verification. Foundational to academic and life-sciences microdispensing.

## MicroFab Jetlab 4 Life-Sciences Inkjet Print Station (2002)

- **id**: `microfab-jetlab-4-life-sciences-printer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: dispenser-pipettor
- **creator**: MicroFab Technologies, Inc. (Plano, TX)
- **disclosure**: MicroFab Technologies Jetlab II launch 2002; Jetlab 4 datasheet 2008; Jetlab 4xl extended-stage datasheet 2014
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Extends wave 1 microfab-mj-at coverage with the integrated Jetlab printer station. Architectural primitives: integration of the MJ-AT piezo glass-capillary dispenser onto an XYZ-positioning stage with synchronized strobe imaging for in-situ jet calibration; multi-printhead capability for simultaneous parallel deposition of different fluids (e.g., one printhead per ink). Anticipates: research-grade life-sciences inkjet print stations using glass-capillary piezo microdispensers on XYZ stages with strobed drop verification. Foundational instrument in academic bioprinting and microarray research.
