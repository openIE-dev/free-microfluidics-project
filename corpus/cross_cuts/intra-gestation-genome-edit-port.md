## Vorkosigan Saga Uterine Replicator (1986)

- **id**: `bujold-vorkosigan-uterine-replicator`
- **corpus**: fictional
- **device class**: fictional-laboratory
- **creator**: Lois McMaster Bujold
- **disclosure**: Lois McMaster Bujold, 'Shards of Honor' (Baen Books, 1986, ISBN 0-671-65644-8); central to 'Barrayar' (Baen, 1991, ISBN 0-671-72083-9); 'Mirror Dance' (Baen, 1994); 'Cetaganda' (Baen, 1996); detailed in 'A Civil Campaign' (Baen, 1999)
- **ip status**: fictional
- **prior art notes**: Discloses ex-vivo gestation of a human embryo to term inside an instrumented perfused chamber, as a mass-produced civilian biotech product. Element-by-element discloses (a) sealed gestational chamber with sterile interface, (b) nutrient/oxygen perfusion loop tuned across gestational stages, (c) mid-gestation embryo transfer protocol from natural womb to replicator, (d) intra-gestation gene-editing access ports, (e) rack-mountable form factor for clinic deployment, (f) interstellar-transport-grade ruggedization. Anticipates the entire ex-vivo gestation / 'EctoLife'-style artificial-womb design space, including transfer protocols, gestation-stage-tuned perfusion, and rack-bank deployment. Bujold's 1986 disclosure substantially predates the 2017 Partridge et al. (CHOP) Biobag publication.
