## Trace Genomics Soil Microbiome Microfluidic Cartridge (2018)

- **id**: `trace-genomics-soil-microbiome-cartridge`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Trace Genomics Inc.
- **disclosure**: Trace Genomics Inc product page 'Soil microbiome analysis service' (2018); USDA partnership announcement 2019
- **ip status**: trade-secret
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a mail-in soil sampling cartridge feeding a high-throughput microfluidic DNA extraction line at a centralized lab, returning a fungal-pathogen and beneficial-microbe report to growers. Anticipates: (a) consumer-mail soil microbiome cartridge format; (b) high-throughput central-lab microfluidic soil prep; (c) the workflow pattern of cartridge-as-shipping-container for soil microbiome. Does not disclose primer multiplex.

## LoaMC Mobile Soil DNA Microfluidic Platform (2019)

- **id**: `loamc-mobile-soil-dna-platform`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: James Hutton Institute (Bell, Squire et al.)
- **disclosure**: Bell K.S. et al. 'LoaMC: A field-deployable microfluidic platform for soil microbial DNA extraction and qPCR' Microfluidics and Nanofluidics 23:65 (2019) doi:10.1007/s10404-019-2233-y
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a field-deployable PMMA microfluidic platform for end-to-end soil microbiome workflow (lyse, capture, elute, qPCR). Anticipates: (a) integrated soil microbiome microfluidic chip; (b) field-portable qPCR coupled to soil DNA extraction; (c) PMMA laser-cut microfluidic format for environmental microbiology. Distinct from Bridle 2014 by integration of qPCR.
