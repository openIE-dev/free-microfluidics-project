## GEA ConsiGma 1 Continuous Pilot Tableting Line (2014)

- **id**: `gea-consigma-1-continuous-pilot-line`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: GEA Pharma Systems (Belgium)
- **disclosure**: GEA ConsiGma-1 product brochure (GEA Pharma Systems 2014); De Beer, T. et al. 'Continuous Manufacturing Tablet Press Line: From Granulation to Compression.' International Journal of Pharmaceutics 519, 158-171 (2017), doi:10.1016/j.ijpharm.2017.01.022
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the R&D-scale ConsiGma-1 module providing the formulation-development counterpart to ConsiGma-25. Anticipates: (a) material-sparing 1 kg/h continuous wet-granulation skids designed to match RTD characteristics of production-scale lines; (b) the design pattern of bidirectional formulation transfer between R&D and production using the same software stack and unit operations. Cite against later patents claiming material-sparing pilot-scale continuous wet-granulation modules with RTD parity to production scale.

## Glatt MODCOS / GMG-IIR Continuous Granulation System (2014)

- **id**: `glatt-modcos-continuous-granulation`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Glatt GmbH (Germany)
- **disclosure**: Plumb, K. 'Continuous Processing in the Pharmaceutical Industry.' Chemical Engineering Research and Design 83, 730-738 (2005), doi:10.1205/cherd.04359; Glatt MODCOS technical brochure 2014; Glatt Group product documentation 2014-2018
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Glatt's MODCOS continuous fluid-bed granulation system with rotary distribution disc that segments the fluid-bed into pseudo-batch zones operating in continuous mode. Anticipates: (a) rotary-distribution-disc fluid-bed granulators for continuous granulation; (b) the MODCOS architectural pattern subsequently licensed/influenced by GEA ConsiGma; (c) shared continuous-fluid-bed templates between pharmaceutical and food-industry granulation. Cite against later patents claiming rotary-distribution-disc fluid-bed continuous granulators.

## Pfizer ECO/ECOnTab Continuous Tableting Platform (2017)

- **id**: `pfizer-eco-econtab-continuous-tableting`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Pfizer Inc., Groton CT and Sandwich UK
- **disclosure**: Allison, G. et al. 'Regulatory and Quality Considerations for Continuous Manufacturing.' Journal of Pharmaceutical Sciences 104, 803-812 (2015), doi:10.1002/jps.24324; Pfizer continuous-manufacturing program, ISPE conference presentations 2017-2019
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Pfizer's ECO/ECOnTab continuous tableting platform integrating wet granulation, drying, and tableting in a sealed skid. The platform shares the GEA-Pfizer-Glatt collaboration heritage but introduces Pfizer-proprietary control software and PAT integration patterns. Anticipates: (a) two-tier scale (5 kg/h portable / 100 kg/h fixed) implementations of the same control software stack; (b) Daurismo-class continuous-manufacturing approvals using ECO; (c) integration of in-line viscosity and torque sensors as soft-sensors for real-time release. Cite against later patent claims for two-tier-scale continuous tableting platforms.

## Janssen ConTabLine Continuous Tableting Platform (2018)

- **id**: `janssen-contabline-continuous-tableting-line`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: printer-tooling
- **creator**: Janssen Pharmaceuticals (Johnson & Johnson)
- **disclosure**: Vanhoorne, V. et al. 'Continuous Manufacturing of Solid Oral Dosage Forms.' Drug Discovery Today: Technologies 28, 27-37 (2018), doi:10.1016/j.ddtec.2018.03.003; Janssen ConTabLine technical disclosure ISPE Pharmaceutical Engineering 38(4), 2018
- **ip status**: patented

Cite against later patents claiming integrated wet-granulation-to-tableting modular skids with PAT control loops.
