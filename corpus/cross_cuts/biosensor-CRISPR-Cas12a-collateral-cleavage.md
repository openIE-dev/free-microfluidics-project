## iGEM EPFL 2018 — CRISPR-Cas12a ctDNA Detection Microfluidic Chip (2018-10)

- **id**: `igem-epfl-2018-crispr-ctdna-microfluidics`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: iGEM EPFL 2018 team (EPF Lausanne, Switzerland)
- **disclosure**: iGEM EPFL 2018 wiki https://2018.igem.org/Team:EPFL/Notebook-Detection-ctDNA (jamboree disclosure 2018-10-25)
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Element-by-element discloses a microfluidic CRISPR diagnostic targeting circulating tumor DNA: (1) on-chip PCR amplification chamber, (2) downstream Cas12a + crRNA reaction chamber containing fluorescent ssDNA reporter, (3) optical readout window measuring fluorescence increase upon collateral ssDNA cleavage. Time-stamped 2018-10 jamboree disclosure predates many subsequent CRISPR-Dx microfluidic patent filings. Anticipates: (a) claims directed to combined PCR + Cas12a-microfluidic ctDNA assays, (b) one-pot CRISPR + amplification cartridge workflows for cancer liquid biopsy, (c) integrated fluorescence-based collateral-cleavage readout in disposable cartridges.
