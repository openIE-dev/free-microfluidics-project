## Univercells Naturoxidor Closed-Loop Bioreactor System (2018)

- **id**: `univercells-naturoxidor-closed-loop`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Univercells SA
- **disclosure**: Univercells Naturoxidor product literature ~2018; Univercells corporate site
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a single-use bioreactor with external membrane oxygenator providing low-shear gas exchange in a closed-loop recirculation architecture, distinct from the structured-packing fixed-bed scale-X. Anticipates: membrane-oxygenator closed-loop bioreactor architectures for shear-sensitive cells (e.g., suspension iPSC, sensitive viral-vector-producing cell lines) where conventional sparging would damage cells; specifically the architectural pattern of separating the gas-exchange unit operation from the cell-containing vessel via external recirculation through a hollow-fiber membrane oxygenator.
