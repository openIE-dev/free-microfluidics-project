## Inscopix nVista Head-Mounted Miniature Calcium-Imaging Microscope (2011-09)

- **id**: `inscopix-nvista-miniscope`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: other
- **creator**: Inscopix Inc. (Schnitzer lab Stanford spinout)
- **disclosure**: Ghosh et al., Nat. Methods 2011, 8, 871-878. DOI:10.1038/nmeth.1694; Inscopix nVista commercial launch 2014.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Head-mounted single-photon fluorescence microscope using an implanted GRIN (gradient-index) lens to access deep brain structures (striatum, hippocampus). Microfluidic-relevant content: the GRIN lens forms a chronic optical-fluidic boundary into the brain (similar role to electrode parylene); LED illumination thermal management uses a heat-spreading interface where saline / tissue contact mediates heat dissipation. Discloses: integrated 2-g microscope including LED, dichroic, GRIN coupling, CMOS sensor, and DAQ; chronic GRIN implantation with bone-cement skull mount; freely-behaving fluorescence imaging at single-cell resolution. Anticipates: head-mounted miniaturized fluorescence microscopes with chronic implanted optical access ports; nVoke pattern of co-located imaging + optogenetic actuation through a single GRIN port.
