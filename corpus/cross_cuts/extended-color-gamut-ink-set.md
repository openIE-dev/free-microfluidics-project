## Canon imagePROGRAF GP Series with Fluorescent Pink Printhead (2022)

- **id**: `canon-imageprograf-gp-series-fluorescent-pink-printhead`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: inkjet-printhead
- **creator**: Canon Inc.
- **disclosure**: Canon imagePROGRAF GP-300 / GP-500 launch June 2022; GP-2000 / GP-4000 launch June 2023
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses an aqueous wide-format inkjet system that adds a dedicated fluorescent-pigment ink channel to the standard 5-color stack. The architectural primitive is the integration of fluorescent-pigment ink within an aqueous DOD thermal-bubble-jet system, with stable jetting under repeated firing despite the larger fluorescent-pigment particle size that historically made fluorescent inks incompatible with thermal MEMS heads. Anticipates: aqueous-DOD wide-format printers using a fluorescent-pigment ink channel on the same printhead carriage as standard CMYK pigment, with printhead nozzle geometry tuned for larger fluorescent particles. Defends against any patent claim covering fluorescent-pigment + thermal-bubble-jet integration.
