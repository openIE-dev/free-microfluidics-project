## Chiou & Wu 2005 Optoelectronic Tweezers Academic Anchor (Berkeley UCB) (2005-07-21)

- **id**: `chiou-wu-2005-oet-academic-anchor`
- **corpus**: academic
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Pei Yu Chiou, Aaron T. Ohta, Ming C. Wu (UC Berkeley)
- **disclosure**: Chiou, Ohta & Wu, 'Massively parallel manipulation of single cells and microparticles using optical images', Nature 436:370-372 (2005-07-21) doi:10.1038/nature03831; UC Berkeley provisional filings 2003-2004
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Foundational 2005 academic disclosure of optoelectronic tweezers (OET): an amorphous-silicon photoconductor sandwiched between transparent electrodes is illuminated by patterned light to create position-controllable DEP traps. Cells and beads are manipulated by re-shaping the light pattern in real time. This Nature paper is the 102 prior-art anchor for any post-2005 patent claim on light-defined DEP particle manipulation, and is incorporated by reference in Berkeley Lights' downstream patent estate. Confirms 2005 public availability of: (i) photoconductive sandwich substrates, (ii) DMD/projector-driven DEP control, (iii) parallel single-cell manipulation, (iv) operation at electrically-safe MHz frequencies.

## Berkeley Lights Beacon Optoelectronic Positioning Patent Family (2010)

- **id**: `berkeley-lights-beacon-oet-patent-family`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: single-cell-platform
- **creator**: Berkeley Lights, Inc.
- **disclosure**: US7956339B2 'Single-sided lateral-field and phototransistor-based optoelectronic tweezers apparatus' (priority 2007); US8921055B2; US9533879B2; Berkeley Lights Beacon platform launch 2016
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Beacon platform's optoelectronic positioning (OEP) chip: a photoconductive amorphous-silicon layer sandwiched between transparent electrodes, where a digital micromirror device (DMD) projects light patterns that locally modulate the photoconductor and create position-controllable dielectrophoretic (DEP) traps. Anticipates: (a) any cell-handling system using projector-driven DEP for single-cell manipulation in microfluidic chambers; (b) integrated culture-and-export workflows where individual cells are penned, fed, and selectively exported by light-patterned forces; (c) photoconductive substrates compatible with both negative and positive DEP at MHz frequencies. Foundational anchor: Chiou, Ohta & Wu 2005 Nature paper on OET (already in corpus).
