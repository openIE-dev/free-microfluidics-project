## Microsaic Systems 4000 MiD Microspray Mass Spectrometer (2010-07)

- **id**: `microsaic-4000-mid`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Microsaic Systems plc (Imperial College spinout)
- **disclosure**: Wright et al., Anal. Chem. 2010, 82, 8217-8221. DOI:10.1021/ac101481k; Microsaic Systems plc Annual Report 2010; product launch July 2010.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Miniaturized benchtop quadrupole MS using a silicon-MEMS microspray emitter (the ChipSpray) replacing the conventional metal-needle electrospray ion source. Discloses: MEMS-fabricated silicon emitter with sub-um aperture geometry that produces a stable Taylor-cone microspray at flow rates 50-1000 nL/min; coupled to a miniaturized ceramic-rod quadrupole mass analyzer; benchtop footprint suitable for fume-hood deployment in pharma kilo-lab. Anticipates: silicon-MEMS electrospray emitters as the ion source for compact MS platforms; benchtop-scale quadrupole MS without conventional vacuum-pump infrastructure (turbo + diaphragm only); the architectural pattern of process-MS at the reaction vessel rather than centralized analytical lab.

## Microsaic 4500 MiD Compact Mass Spectrometer (2014)

- **id**: `microsaic-4500-mid`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Microsaic Systems plc
- **disclosure**: Microsaic Systems plc Annual Report 2014; product launch 2014; Wells et al., Org. Process Res. Dev. 2015, 19, 1500-1506.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Successor to 4000 MiD with extended mass range and improved sensitivity. Discloses: refined silicon ChipSpray emitter with longer service life (>30 days continuous); extended mass range to 2000 Da to cover small biomolecules; integrated GMP-compliant PAT software stack. Anticipates: in-line process-MS for pharmaceutical kilo-lab synthesis monitoring; the architectural distinction between centralized lab-MS and at-reaction MS using MEMS emitters.
