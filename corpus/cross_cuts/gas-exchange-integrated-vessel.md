## Pall iCellis 1000 Fixed-Bed Bioreactor (2015)

- **id**: `pall-icellis-1000-fixed-bed-bioreactor`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Pall Life Sciences (now Cytiva, formerly ATMI Life Sciences)
- **disclosure**: Pall Life Sciences iCellis 1000 product brochure 2015-2019 (acquired by Cytiva 2021); Drugmand, J.-C. et al. 'Adherent-cell-culture in single-use technology: the iCellis Nano bioreactor.' BMC Proceedings 5 (Suppl 8), P32 (2011), doi:10.1186/1753-6561-5-S8-P32
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Pall iCellis 500/1000 fixed-bed bioreactor for adherent-cell viral vector production. The fixed bed of polyester BioNOC II microfibers provides 200-1000 m^2 of attachment surface in a compact vessel, with integrated gas-exchange and impeller. Anticipates: (a) single-use fixed-bed bioreactor architecture using polyester macrocarrier media for adherent-cell viral-vector production; (b) integrated gas-exchange and impeller within the bioreactor body (eliminating sparger); (c) the scale-up template from iCellis Nano (research) to iCellis 500 (clinical) to iCellis 1000 (commercial gene therapy). Cite against later patents claiming single-use polyester-fiber fixed-bed bioreactors for AAV/lentivirus production.
