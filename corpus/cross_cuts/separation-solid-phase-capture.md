- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Thermo Fisher Scientific (Phadia AB; formerly Pharmacia Diagnostics)
- **disclosure**: Pharmacia CAP System RAST FEIA introduced 1989 (succeeding the 1974 Phadebas RAST); Phadia AB ImmunoCAP and Phadia 250/1000/2500/5000 instrument manuals; foundational solid-phase cellulose-sponge patent (Ceska, Pharmacia).

- **prior art notes**: Discloses a porous hydrophilic-polymer (cellulose-sponge) capsule solid phase with very high binding-area-to-volume ratio used in an automated fluoroenzyme immunoassay for specific IgE and autoimmune antibodies. Extends the existing thermo-fisher-phadia-2500-immunoassay entry by detailing the ImmunoCAP solid phase and the full instrument family.
