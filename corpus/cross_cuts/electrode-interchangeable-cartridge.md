## Vapourtec Ion Electrochemistry Flow Module (2018)

- **id**: `vapourtec-ion-electrochemistry-module`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Vapourtec Ltd. (UK)
- **disclosure**: Vapourtec Ion electrochemistry module product brochure 2018-2020; Atobe, M. et al. 'Applications of Flow Microreactors in Electrosynthetic Processes.' Chemical Reviews 118, 4541-4572 (2018), doi:10.1021/acs.chemrev.7b00353
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Vapourtec Ion electrochemistry flow cell module integrated with the R-series flow chemistry platform. The cell architecture comprises two parallel-plate electrodes separated by a defined inter-electrode gap (0.5-1.0 mm) with continuous-flow electrolyte through the gap. Anticipates: (a) modular electrochemistry cells designed as drop-in modules for existing flow-chemistry platforms; (b) variable inter-electrode gap geometry enabling switching between diffusion-limited and kinetically-limited regimes; (c) interchangeable electrode-plate cartridges (C/Pt/Ni/Cu/Pb/BDD) for material-screening DoE in electrosynthesis. Cite against later patents claiming modular parallel-plate flow electrochemistry cells with interchangeable electrode plates.
