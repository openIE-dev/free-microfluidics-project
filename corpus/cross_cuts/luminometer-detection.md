## Charm Sciences PasLite/AP Pasteurization Phosphatase Test (1997)

- **id**: `charm-pasteurization-phosphatase-test`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Charm Sciences, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Charm Sciences PasLite product launch 1997; ISO 11816-1 method reference; US patent 5,891,649 (chemiluminescent ALP detection)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a dioxetane-substrate-based chemiluminescent assay for residual alkaline phosphatase as a pasteurization verification test in dairy. Sample is pipetted into a single-use cuvette pre-loaded with substrate; light output is measured in a benchtop luminometer. Anticipates: cuvette-format chemiluminescent ALP assays as the dairy-industry replacement for color-based pasteurization tests; ISO 11816-aligned cartridge formats; and the architectural pattern of single-use luminometer cuvettes for regulatory dairy QC.

## Hygiena Innovate ATP Hygiene Monitoring System (2018)

- **id**: `hygiena-innovate-atp-luminometer`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: point-of-care-cartridge
- **creator**: Hygiena, LLC
- **disclosure**: Hygiena Innovate / EnSURE Touch product launch 2018; UltraSnap swab datasheet; US patent 6,524,795 (snap-valve ATP swab cartridge)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses Hygiena's flagship ATP swab-cartridge architecture: a polypropylene tube with an upper bulb containing liquid-stable luciferin-luciferase, a lower swab pre-moistened with extractant, and a snap-valve that joins the two. Pressing the bulb mixes reagent with swab in a single sealed cartridge read in a benchtop luminometer. Anticipates: snap-valve mixing of liquid reagent with sample-loaded swab in a single sealed disposable; cloud-managed multi-site ATP monitoring ecosystems; and the architectural pattern of swab + reagent in one cartridge as a defining primitive of food-plant hygiene programs.
