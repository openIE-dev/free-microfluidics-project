## Wilson Wolf G-Rex 2.0 Gas-Permeable Cell Expansion Device (2020)

- **id**: `wilson-wolf-grex-2-0`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Wilson Wolf Manufacturing Corporation
- **disclosure**: Wilson Wolf Manufacturing Corporation G-Rex 2.0 product launch 2020; Vera et al., Cytotherapy 2010, 12, 418-422 (academic predecessor); Bajgain et al., Cytotherapy 2014, 16, 1216-1226.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: G-Rex 2.0 (M-Series) extends the original G-Rex (already in corpus as wilson-wolf-grex) with integrated sensor and sampling port. Discloses: bottom-mounted gas-permeable silicone membrane providing O2/CO2 transfer at 10-100x the rate of conventional headspace-only diffusion, enabling 10x deeper culture media columns and 10x cell density per footprint; integrated single-use sensor port and sterile-sampling port in the M-Series variant. Anticipates: gas-permeable-membrane-bottom cell expansion vessels for cell therapy manufacturing; the architectural pattern of static culture + gas-permeable bottom + deep media column as the standard for adoptive-cell-therapy expansion; integrated sensor + sampling ports added to gas-permeable vessels for closed-process compliance.
