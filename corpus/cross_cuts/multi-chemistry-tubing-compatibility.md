## Cytiva BioWelder TC Sterile Tubing Welder (2008)

- **id**: `cytiva-biowelder-tc`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Cytiva (formerly GE Healthcare Life Sciences)
- **disclosure**: GE Healthcare BioWelder TC launch 2008; Cytiva (rebrand) datasheet 29-1014-23 AB rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a thermoplastic tubing welder supporting multiple tubing chemistries (C-Flex, PharMed, Tygon) with a disposable blade per weld cycle. Anticipates: thermoplastic-tubing-welder architectures with disposable blade-per-weld eliminating cross-contamination, supporting the broad range of single-use tubing chemistries used across CGT, mRNA, and traditional biopharma. Direct competitor to Terumo TSCD-Q and Sartorius BioWelder in the sterile-connection equipment market.
