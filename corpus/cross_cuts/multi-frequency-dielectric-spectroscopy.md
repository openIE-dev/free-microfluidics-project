## Aber Instruments Futura Biomass System Controller (2010)

- **id**: `aber-biomass-system-controller`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Aber Instruments Ltd. (Hamilton Company since 2017)
- **disclosure**: Aber Instruments Futura launch ~2010; Hamilton acquisition of Aber Instruments biomass-monitoring business 2017; product datasheet rev 2024
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Extends the existing aber-incyte-capacitance-probe entry by disclosing the integrated controller (Futura) that performs multi-frequency dielectric spectroscopy scanning to extract viable cell density AND cell-size distribution from the beta-dispersion of the cell-suspension dielectric response. Anticipates: integrated multi-frequency capacitance controllers that derive cell-size information beyond simple biomass measurement; specifically the architectural pattern of frequency-scanning capacitance probes outputting both cell concentration and cell-size data from the Cole-Cole fit of the beta-dispersion curve. Critical for CGT manufacturing where cell size correlates with manufacturing-train health.
