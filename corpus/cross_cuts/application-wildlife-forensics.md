## US Fish and Wildlife Service Forensics Lab Microfluidic Workflow (2012)

- **id**: `us-fws-forensic-lab-microfluidic`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: US Fish and Wildlife Service - Clark R. Bavin National Fish and Wildlife Forensics Laboratory
- **disclosure**: USFWS National Fish and Wildlife Forensics Laboratory annual report 'Microfluidic capillary electrophoresis for wildlife species ID' (2012)
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the USFWS National Fish and Wildlife Forensics Lab adoption of microfluidic capillary electrophoresis (Agilent 2100/Caliper LabChip and Applied Biosystems chip CE) for routine species identification and STR profiling in wildlife crime cases. Anticipates: (a) federal-evidence-grade microfluidic CE workflow for wildlife forensics; (b) the architectural pattern of chip CE as a courtroom-admissible analytical tool; (c) microfluidic species-ID as a routine national-lab method. Does not disclose specific marker panels.

## TRACE Wildlife Forensics Network Microfluidic DNA Protocols (2014)

- **id**: `trace-wildlife-forensics-network-microfluidic-protocols`
- **corpus**: open
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: TRACE Wildlife Forensics Network (Edinburgh)
- **disclosure**: TRACE Wildlife Forensics Network technical guidelines 'Microfluidic DNA extraction for CITES-listed species' (2014); Ogden R. 'Forensic science, genetics and wildlife biology' Forensic Sci. Med. Pathol. 6:172 (2010) doi:10.1007/s12024-010-9165-x
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Discloses normative microfluidic DNA extraction and species-identification protocols for CITES-listed wildlife products (elephant ivory, rhino horn, pangolin scale) tailored to challenging matrices. Anticipates: (a) microfluidic-protocol governance layer for wildlife forensics; (b) standardized lab-on-chip extraction from keratin and dentin matrices; (c) CITES-compliant chain-of-custody on microfluidic instrument workflows. Does not specify chip designs.
