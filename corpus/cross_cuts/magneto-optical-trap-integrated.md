## ColdQuanta MIQRO Cold-Atom MEMS Quantum Sensor Module (2019-12)

- **id**: `coldquanta-miqro`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: nanofluidic-chip
- **creator**: ColdQuanta Inc. (now Infleqtion)
- **disclosure**: ColdQuanta (now Infleqtion) MIQRO product brief 2019; Salim et al., New J. Phys. 2011, 13, 083035 (academic UHV cell predecessor); DARPA A-PhI program.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: MEMS-fabricated ultra-high-vacuum vapor cell trapping laser-cooled rubidium atoms. Microfluidic-relevant content: the MEMS UHV cell is an anodically-bonded silicon-glass-silicon nanofluidic structure containing Rb vapor at <1e-9 Torr, with integrated optical-access windows and magnetic-trap pads. Discloses: MEMS UHV cell fabrication via anodic bonding with internal getter pumping; integrated magneto-optical trap geometry for atom cooling and confinement; modular packaging with laser-cooling beam-delivery interface. Anticipates: MEMS UHV cells as the substrate for portable cold-atom quantum sensors; the architectural pattern of anodically-bonded UHV cell + getter + integrated trap that has become canonical for chip-scale atom-interferometer development.
