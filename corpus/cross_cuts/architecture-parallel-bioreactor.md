## Sartorius BIOSTAT B-DCU Bench-Scale Bioreactor (2008)

- **id**: `sartorius-biostat-b-dcu-bioreactor`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: flow-controller
- **creator**: Sartorius Stedim Biotech (Germany)
- **disclosure**: Sartorius Stedim Biotech BIOSTAT B-DCU product brochure 2008-2018; Eibl, R. et al. 'Bag bioreactor based on wave-induced motion: characteristics and applications.' Bioprocess and Biosystems Engineering 31, 73-89 (2008)
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses the Sartorius BIOSTAT B-DCU benchtop bioreactor architecture: a 1-10 L autoclavable glass vessel with separate Digital Control Unit (DCU) tower hosting probes, gas mixing, peristaltic pumps, and feedback control. Multiple B-DCU units can be paralleled under MFCS-DA for small-DoE studies. Anticipates: (a) physically separated DCU+vessel bioreactor architecture enabling probe-tower reuse across multiple vessels; (b) hybrid glass-autoclavable / single-use Univessel-SU swap-capable architecture; (c) parallel-MFCS control of multiple bench-scale bioreactors as a budget-friendly DoE approach. Cite against later patents claiming separated DCU-vessel architectures with hybrid glass/single-use vessel options.
