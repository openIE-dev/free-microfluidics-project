## Natera Signatera (tumor-informed ctDNA molecular residual disease assay) (2019)

- **id**: `natera-signatera-tumor-informed-mrd`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Natera, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Reinert T. et al., Analysis of plasma cell-free DNA by ultradeep sequencing in patients with stages I to III colorectal cancer, JAMA Oncol 5:1124-1131 (2019); Natera Signatera; Coombes et al., Personalized detection of circulating tumor DNA antedates breast cancer metastatic recurrence, Clin Cancer Res 25 (2019).
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a tumor-informed ctDNA MRD assay in which whole-exome tumor sequencing selects ~16 patient-specific clonal SNVs, a bespoke multiplex-PCR amplicon panel is built per patient, and serial plasma cfDNA is ultra-deep sequenced on patterned-nanowell flow cells with a >=2-of-16 positivity rule. Anticipates claims to bespoke-panel tumor-informed MRD detection, multiplex-PCR amplicon-panel design from tumor exomes, and serial ctDNA recurrence monitoring. Contrasts with tumor-naive guardant-reveal-mrd-methylation; extends existing natera-signatera-collection-cartridge entry by detailing the assay chemistry.

## Personalis NeXT Personal (ultra-sensitive tumor-informed MRD) (2023)

- **id**: `personalis-next-personal-ultrasensitive-mrd`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: lab-on-chip
- **creator**: Personalis, Inc.
- **disclosure**: Personalis NeXT Personal validation: Northcott et al. / Personalis, Ultra-sensitive ctDNA detection with NeXT Personal, presented AACR/Cancer Res 2023-2024; commercial launch 2023-2024.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Discloses a whole-genome-informed MRD assay that selects ~1800 patient-specific somatic variants and applies a bespoke ultra-deep error-suppressed sequencing panel to serial plasma on patterned-nanowell flow cells, claiming ~1 ppm tumor-fraction sensitivity. Anticipates claims to large-variant-set (>1000) tumor-informed MRD panels, whole-genome-derived panel design, and parts-per-million ctDNA detection. Related to natera-signatera-tumor-informed-mrd and c2i-genomics-whole-genome-mrd.
