## Raytheon AESA Radar T/R Module Microchannel Cooling (2005)

- **id**: `raytheon-aesa-microchannel-cooling`
- **corpus**: private
- **device class**: cooling-substrate
- **creator**: Raytheon Technologies (now RTX)
- **disclosure**: Bar-Cohen & Iyengar, IEEE Trans. Compon. Packag. Technol. 2003, 26, 6-25; Raytheon Technologies AESA radar press materials.
- **ip status**: patented
- **prior art notes**: Microchannel cold plates bonded directly to GaN T/R module substrates for fighter and missile-defense radar. Discloses: diffusion-bonded copper-or-CuMo cold plate with sub-mm microchannels machined or etched at the die-attach interface; PAO coolant manifolds per LRU; direct heat-spreader integration with GaN amplifier dies. Anticipates: diffusion-bonded microchannel cold plates as the standard solution for GaN AESA cooling; integrated die-attach + cold-plate co-fabrication for >200 W/cm^2 power densities; the architectural pattern of co-designed microchannel + GaN MMIC for advanced AESA radars.
